# Expand the Korean Creator Photobook Catalog

This batch adds **21 verified product records**: six MAXQ books and 15 Pyo Eunji titles. Across the batch, 14 editions are digital and seven are physical, including the Pyo batch's nine digital photobooks and six physical booklets. The live catalog verification reported **355 physical and 5,081 digital photobooks** after import. The original five MAXQ records, the separate Vol. 10 record, and the 15-work Pyo batch all passed their scoped database audits with zero integrity findings. These are metadata imports: **no covers or paid files were uploaded to R2**, because permission to reuse the specific image assets is not documented. The source links below identify the publisher, retailer, or creator records used; the manifests and database audits are linked for repeatable review.

## No covers were uploaded to R2

No cover images, samples, or paid ebook files were copied to R2 for these records. The Pyo and MAXQ audits both report zero explicit media references, and the project guide requires permission for the exact image asset before adding cover media; a public product listing alone does not grant reuse rights ([catalog source and image-rights guide](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)). The next image step is to document the rights holder's permission for each selected asset, then add only those approved files and verify their R2 keys.
