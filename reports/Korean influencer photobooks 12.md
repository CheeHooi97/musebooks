# Six Works Expand Korea’s Photobook Catalog

This pass adds **six creator-linked digital photobook works, represented by seven editions, to the live database**: four dated Impact Crew DRM eBooks sold through YES24 and two YooSeorin publications, with *RINIQ Vol.6* represented by Lite and Original PDFs. A repeatable-read database read-back confirms all six works and **zero explicit media references**; the accompanying ledger audit reports zero integrity failures and zero people without verification sources across its 110-work, 208-edition scope. The public catalog verifier returned **373 physical and 5,102 digital works** at this check. More physical titles remain under review because edition links or creator identities are unresolved, and none of this batch’s sources grants permission to host an exact cover or page image in R2. ([Batch 12 database read-back](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-db-readback.json), [batch audit](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-db-verification.json), [public verification output](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-public-verification.txt), [import manifest](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-import.json))

## Four dated Impact Crew eBooks meet the catalog threshold

YES24’s exact product pages identify four paid Impact Crew PDF eBooks with dates and valid ISBN-13s. *정유승 임팩트크루 웹화보집* was listed on 2019-12-01 as ISBN **9791196797867** (59.11 MB); its contents name Jeong Yoo-seung, Shin Saerom, and Jung Da-yeon, and Sports Kyunghyang independently identifies Jeong Yoo-seung as a fitness model who released a named Impact photobook. *걸크러쉬 임팩트크루 웹화보집* shares the date, carries ISBN **9791196797874** (61.76 MB), and names Yona, Jia, Bomi, and Taeri; Gwanak FM independently identifies Girl Crush and the same four-member lineup. ([YES24 Jeong Yoo-seung](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731916), [Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/201904111050003), [YES24 Girl Crush](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731917), [Gwanak FM](https://m.radiogfm.net/news/15151))

The other two exact listings are *안정미 임팩트 웹화보*, dated 2020-06-05, ISBN **9791196797881** (85.43 MB), and *레이샤 고은 임팩트 웹화보*, also dated 2020-06-05, ISBN **9791196797898** (288.67 MB). YES24 describes An Jeong-mi as a former model and Goeun as a former Laysha member; Star News separately identifies Goeun as a Laysha member and reports her photo-release announcement. The product pages establish the digital editions, but they do not supply reliable total page counts. YES24 describes these products as DRM eBooks and restricts printing, so the paid files and their artwork remain outside the catalog’s image storage. ([YES24 An Jeong-mi](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474558), [YES24 Laysha Goeun](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474556), [Star News](https://www.starnewskorea.com/music/2021/02/23/2021022311030817945))

## YooSeorin’s storefront documents three digital editions

YooSeorin’s first-party Patreon post links buyers to RINI BOOKS and sells *RINIQ Vol.6 — Velvet Murmur* in two PDF versions: **Lite, 49 pages, and Original, 77 pages**. Those are two editions of one work, not two separate books. The same creator’s *O.N.O #09 Halloween (Spicy Ver.)* page describes a **60-page** digital photo publication with two concepts and links to RINI BOOKS; its stated October 21–24 sale window omits the year, so the database leaves the release date unknown. The posts tag the material as Korean-model content, but they do not establish a legal name or nationality, and this report uses the creator’s public name only. ([RINIQ Vol.6 product post](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/riniq-vol-6-ver-157047855), [YooSeorin creator links](https://litt.ly/its.yoo.seorin), [O.N.O #09 product post](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/o-n-o-09-spicy-172438442))

The 50-page Cupick listing *꾸뿌 / 봄의물결* remains a digital lead, not an imported work. Cupick credits GGuBbu and photographer YURAYURA and offers browser reading, while ChosunBiz identifies GGuBbu as a MAXIM model; the listing does not identify who uploaded it or document that creator’s authorization of the store. The product exists, but its seller relationship needs confirmation before treating the metadata as creator-authorized. ([Cupick product](https://cupick.jp/ko/products/ggubbu-springwaves), [ChosunBiz](https://biz.chosun.com/entertainment/enter_general/2024/02/05/IBGTMK2J3XIGZ55MEYKUNMXHHU/?outputType=amp))

## The database is verified while R2 stays image-free

The batch manifest contains **seven digital edition records across six works**. The database read-back confirms each work, its edition and the featured credits; the Girl Crush credit now resolves once to the existing group identity **걸크러쉬**, with the Gwanak FM source attached, rather than creating a second unverified group-name record. The read-back records zero explicit media references, and the cohort audit reports **zero integrity failures and zero people without provenance**. The public verification file reports 373 physical and 5,102 digital works at its run time; these are public-catalog totals, not a count of this batch alone. ([Batch 12 import manifest](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-import.json), [database read-back](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-db-readback.json), [database audit](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-db-verification.json), [public verification](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-public-verification.txt))

No image was uploaded to R2 for these six works. YES24’s DRM listings, YooSeorin’s paid posts, and the public retailer/editorial pages provide product evidence but no license covering the exact cover, preview, or interior asset and third-party hosting. Crazy Giant’s report expressly reserves rights in its photos and text and prohibits unauthorized reproduction. R2 can be populated for this batch only after the relevant publisher, photographer, or rightsholder grants permission for a specific image and the intended hosting use. ([YES24 Jeong Yoo-seung](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731916), [RINIQ Vol.6](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/riniq-vol-6-ver-157047855), [O.N.O #09](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/o-n-o-09-spicy-172438442), [Crazy Giant rights notice](https://crazygiant.co.kr/giantgirl/?bmode=view&idx=175104830))

## Conclusion

This pass expands the catalog through a clear distinction between a verifiable product and a verifiable image license: six works now have bibliographic records, while their commercial artwork remains unmirrored. The next research gain is likely to come from resolving exact print SKUs and creator identity for the held candidates; adding images requires separate, asset-specific permission rather than more retailer screenshots.
