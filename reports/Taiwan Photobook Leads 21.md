# Round 21 Finds Taiwan Photobooks Already Catalogued

**This round produced no safe new import, so no R2 cover or database record was written.** Four direct digital listings that looked fresh resolve to editions already present in MuseBooks: 夏蕾, 瑟七, 林真亦, and the Rakuten Girls group book. The physical-retailer sweep likewise found duplicates, while one vague listing lacked enough bibliographic and creator evidence. A Taiwan photobook by actor-photographer 宋偉恩 is supported by campaign, library, origin, and finished-copy evidence, but the available official cover is explicitly a preview and differs from the finished book; his social-influencer status is also unverified. The remaining uncatalogued leads stay on hold until the missing identity, product, fulfillment, or final-cover evidence is available.

## Four direct digital listings resolve to existing editions

Pubu’s direct listing for 夏蕾’s 《心動蕾達：夏蕾寫真》 identifies an EPUB released on 2026-09-18, with ISBN 9786264446433 and 147 pages; the live MuseBooks result already contains that digital edition and its physical counterpart. The same duplicate check applies to 瑟七’s 294-page EPUB 《微澀．微瑟》, 林真亦’s digital edition 《亦如初見》, and Kobo’s four-member Rakuten Girls Yanmaga edition: each seller product has a matching existing MuseBooks work or edition. These are confirmed catalog matches, not candidates for another DB/R2 write. ([Pubu 夏蕾 product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/696714), [MuseBooks result](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E5%BF%83%E5%8B%95%E8%95%BE%E9%81%94&pageSize=50), [Pubu 瑟七 product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/690306), [MuseBooks result](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E7%91%9F%E4%B8%83&pageSize=50), [Readmoo 林真亦 product](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=73371), [MuseBooks result](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E4%BA%A6%E5%A6%82%E5%88%9D%E8%A6%8B&pageSize=50), [Kobo Rakuten Girls product](https://www.kobo.com/tw/zh/ebook/kobo-rakuten-girls-2), [MuseBooks result](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E6%A8%82%E5%A4%A9%E5%A5%B3%E5%AD%A9%20Yanmaga&pageSize=50))

Two digital leads remain **HOLD** for different reasons. Pubu’s 《關關寫真集2-Vol.02》 has a direct listing and matching cover, but “G-JAN 關關” is not tied by the available primary evidence to a full identity or Taiwan origin. 笑笑’s standalone Yanmaga mini still lacks its own direct Kobo page and matching individual cover; the verified four-person group edition is a separate, already-catalogued product and cannot stand in for it. ([Pubu 關關 product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/604421), [FindBook 笑笑 listing](https://findbook.com.tw/8982002000020), [Kodansha product page](https://www.kodansha.co.jp/book/products/0000429233), [official Yanmaga Taiwan campaign](https://ymclub.kodansha.co.jp/pages/yanmaga-cup-tw))

## Physical retailer checks confirm duplicates or holds

The strongest fresh-looking physical listing, 安于晴 (Charlene)’s 《不安于室》, is already represented in MuseBooks as a physical edition. RedBoy supplies its publication date, 144-page count, dimensions, publisher, and sold-out status, and its official cover image has the same SHA-256 as the cover returned from the existing MuseBooks record. Other sampled bookstore listings also resolved to cataloged works. One sparse RedBoy listing still cannot be imported: its page gives no useful book description, format, subject, ISBN, or page count, and a zero-result catalog search does not establish that it is a photobook by a Taiwan creator. ## 宋偉恩’s published book still fails the import gate

The evidence supports a completed Taiwan photobook, 《貳柒.禮：The Present of Wayne’s 27th》: its official crowdfunding page calls it 宋偉恩’s first personal photobook, the National Central Library record gives a December 2021 Taipei publication with 144 pages and ISBN 9786269545100, and VAP identifies him as born in Taichung. Suruga-ya’s product page shows a finished physical copy, but only inside a composite set photograph. The official campaign artwork is explicitly a reference preview and its design differs from the finished copy, so it cannot verify the final cover. The sources establish him as an actor-photographer and Taiwan creator, but do not establish social-media influencer status; for this influencer-focused catalog, the correct decision remains **HOLD**, with no catalog, DB, or R2 changes. ([official Zeczec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/The-Present-of-Waynes-27th), [National Central Library deposit record](https://nclfile.ncl.edu.tw/files/202204/58dc014c-a17e-4259-ac08-e039a107fc62.pdf), [VAP artist profile](https://www.vap.co.jp/life/special/article2.html), [Suruga-ya product page](https://www.suruga-ya.jp/product/detail/JB7083), [campaign cover preview](https://assets.zeczec.com/asset_345409_image_big.jpg?1626858983=), [finished-book set photo](https://cdn.suruga-ya.jp/database/pics_webp/game/jb7083.jpg.webp))

## Conclusion

Round 21 strengthens the catalog’s duplicate coverage but adds no new record. The clearest next checks are a primary identity/origin source for G-JAN 關關, an individual retailer page and cover for 笑笑’s mini, and a standalone final cover plus influencer-scope evidence for 宋偉恩’s book. Until those gaps close, importing would weaken the catalog’s evidence standard.
