# Screen Korean Book Leads, Preserve Rights

This round found no new title that cleared all three gates: verified Korean influencer/model identity, a physical or digital photobook, and an authorized source for reusable catalog metadata; **no database records were added**. MAXQ says *Secret B* No. 1 began digitally, but the existing catalog already holds the work’s physical edition and no separate digital-edition identifier was found. STOVE lists a downloadable cosplay photobook, but its credited cosplayer’s Korean identity and influencer status remain unverified. Magarin’s *Secret B* No. 4 is already in the catalog, while the open-library record search for it was inconclusive. The latest read-only cover scan found **10 editions with cover references and zero R2 object keys**; the prior broader scan covered 153 published Korean photobook editions. No exact cover or preview image has documented permission for R2 storage and public delivery, so this round made no image copy or upload. The prior round’s Kim Young Ju record remains a metadata-only addition for a model, with influencer reach unverified and no media assets.

## MAXQ’s digital-first No. 1 does not establish a new edition

MAXQ’s publisher announcement says *Secret B* No. 1 first appeared digitally and later received a print edition, featuring Ryu Se-bi and Park Eunhye. MAXQ’s contest profile and a 2021 press profile describe Park as a YouTuber, supporting her creator status, but the catalog already includes the paired work’s physical No. 1 edition and Park’s separate digital Vol. 03 compilation ([MAXQ announcement](https://maxq.kr/articles/sikeurit-b-ryusebibakeunhye-hwabojip-yeyakpanmae-opeun); [MAXQ creator profile](https://maxq.kr/articles/pyojimodeleun-naya-na-maekseukyu-pyojimodel-konteseuteu-bonseon-jinchulja-gonggae); [SportsWorldi profile](https://www.sportsworldi.com/newsView/20210821503607); [catalog verification snapshot](../docs/korean-secret-b-photobooks-db-verification.json)). The sources reviewed did not establish a separately identifiable No. 1 digital edition with its own product identifier or edition-level details, so adding one would risk inventing an edition. MAXQ’s page prohibits unauthorized reproduction and does not state an affirmative commercial metadata-reuse license; the publisher evidence is useful for discovery, but it did not clear the catalog-source gate.

## STOVE lists a real digital book, with creator identity unresolved

STOVE’s official listing names *Circles Kim Ga-eul Cosplay Photo Book*, credits TOPCO/RZCOS, gives a 26 June 2025 release date, and describes a downloadable book featuring the character Kim Ga-eul, with “Coser. Siru [Kim Ga-eul]” as its credit ([STOVE product page](https://store.onstove.com/ko/games/100488)). This confirms a digital photo-book product, but the listing alone does not establish that Siru is a Korean influencer or otherwise identify the credited performer as a real Korean creator. It also does not grant third-party commercial reuse of the listing’s factual metadata. The lead remains outside the catalog until reliable identity evidence and an authorized metadata source are available; no live duplicate query or import was run.

Magarin is a strong subject-fit lead: an influencer-award feature and a 2025 profile describe her racing-model and influencer activity and link her to *Secret B* No. 4 ([Influencer Awards feature](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DQ6pAz5sx4); [Sports Seoul profile](https://news.nate.com/view/20251208n02046)). However, the work and physical edition are already represented in MuseBooks, so it is not a new addition ([existing import manifest](../docs/korean-photobook-magarin-secret-b-4-import.json); [read-only database verification](../docs/korean-photobook-magarin-secret-b-4-db-verification.json)). A bookseller lists the issue as a magazine and supplies a serial-style barcode, while the MAXQ store calls it a photobook. The exact NLK LOD query did not return a usable response, which leaves the open-record search unresolved rather than proving no record exists ([Youngpoong record](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202412304692212430); [NLK LOD service](https://lod.nl.go.kr/sparql); [NLK dataset terms](https://lod.nl.go.kr/home/dataset/datadownload.do)). Kim So-hee’s MAXIM B-SIDE No. 5 is also already represented in the public catalog, and Kim Young Ju’s NLK-sourced photography booklet was imported in the preceding round; neither is a new record this round ([MuseBooks Kim So-hee listing](https://musebooks.my/books/korea-maxim-bside-2019-5-kim-sohee); [round 19 report](Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2019.md)).

The new rights-marked image lead does not solve the catalog gap. The Korea Racing Authority publishes seven Kkaeali images under KOGL Type 2, which is limited to attribution and noncommercial use; the record does not identify a photobook, so these assets are not a cover or preview for a qualifying title ([official Kkaeali asset record](https://www.kogl.or.kr/recommend/recommendDivView.do?division=img&recommendIdx=50613); [project source-policy register](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)). The previous round’s NLK record for Kim Young Ju supports factual metadata reuse only, not reuse of a cover image ([NLK record](https://lod.nl.go.kr/resource/KMO202332009); [database read-back](../docs/korean-kim-young-ju-photography-booklet-2022-db-readback.json)).

## Ten cover references still lack R2 authorization

A read-only query on 30 October 2026 found 10 published Korean photobook editions with cover references. Each had an empty `cover_object_key`, and its stored media URLs remained the same remote source URLs rather than distinct R2 copies. The earlier 153-edition scan identified eight influencer-focused editions across DJ SODA, Kim Seolhwa, and Pyo Eunji; the other two cover-bearing editions in the current query were *BE JOYFUL* and *SANTORINI* ([round 20 exact-image rights audit](../research_notes/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2020/image_rights.md); [round 19 audit](../research_notes/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2019/r2_rights.md)). Shueisha says it does not grant individuals permission to use book covers; KADOKAWA’s limited cover rules route Internet distribution to an inquiry; Apple’s terms do not transfer commercial image rights; and Pyo Eunji’s terms prohibit unauthorized copying and public transmission ([Shueisha FAQ](https://faq.shueisha.co.jp/faq/show/28?back=front%2Fcategory%3Ashow&category_id=2&faq_id=17&site_domain=default); [KADOKAWA cover-use rules](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/support/b/library/); [Apple Media Services terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/us/terms.html); [Pyo Eunji terms](https://pyoeunji.com/pages/terms)). These sources and the catalog’s remote image URLs establish provenance, not permission to store the exact files in R2 and serve them publicly. No rights-holder was contacted, and no image was downloaded, copied, uploaded, or changed in the database.

## Conclusion

This pass narrows two useful leads without overstating them: MAXQ documents a digital-first release history for a work already cataloged in print, while STOVE documents a digital book whose credited performer is not yet verified as a Korean influencer. A future addition needs an exact bibliographic record with reuse terms that cover factual catalog metadata, followed by a duplicate check. A future R2 upload separately needs permission tied to the exact image and covering storage, public delivery, and any required credit or modifications.
