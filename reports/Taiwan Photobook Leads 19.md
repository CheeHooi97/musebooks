# Separate Duplicates From New Photobook Leads

This round found **no new title ready for a MuseBooks or R2 import**. The strongest physical candidates, 《三倍心跳》 and *Babe Fantasy*, already have matching catalog records; new digital discoveries were either already cataloged or remain on hold. Creator campaigns contain credible photobook announcements, but lack a combination of stable product details, title-specific cover evidence, or sufficiently clear Taiwan-origin evidence. The most focused unresolved lead is 笑笑’s separately listed Yanmaga digital mini-book: it appears absent from the catalog, but the direct seller page, complete product metadata, verified cover, and definitive Taiwan-origin evidence are still missing. Round 18’s 洪蓉 Vol.02 preorder remains the previous round’s completed import, not a Round 19 change.

The seller does not supply ISBN, page count, dimensions, or a dispatch date, and does not identify the displayed image as final cover art; those gaps do not change the duplicate decision. ## Conclusion

The remaining work is shifting from broad title discovery toward closing evidence gaps: an individual-title seller page and downloadable cover for 笑笑, post-event product and fulfillment details for creator campaigns, and definitive origin evidence where a Taiwan-market association is the only signal. Round 19 made **no catalog, database, or R2 changes** because all candidates were either duplicates or still held below the import threshold.
