# Taiwan creator searches leave editions unverified

As of 2026-10-30, round 5 produced **no new edition that cleared the import bar for MuseBooks or an R2 cover upload**. The search surfaced two physical leads—Rina 謝艾娜’s 《盛夏・謝艾娜》 and 菲菲／李恩菲’s 《韓你菲》—without reliable product records, and one apparent ebook edition for 鄭羽凡 whose format, date, pagination, and cover could not be reconciled. Other ebook leads already have digital records, while several recent physical titles also resolve to existing catalog entries. This does not mean the project made no catalog progress: the completed round 4 report records three format-specific editions for two creator books, with their covers saved in R2. Round 5 made no catalog, database, or R2 changes; source verification, rather than title discovery alone, determined that outcome. ([Round 4 report](Taiwan%20influencer%20photobook%20leads%204.md))

## 鄭羽凡’s absent ebook remains a hold

Books.com lists an ebook for 鄭羽凡 with EISBN **9786267742044**, fixed-layout EPUB format, publisher 水靈文創, and date **2026-08-18**. MuseBooks currently has her existing work, work-tw-9bcbbed2719c0138ff33c410, as a physical-only edition with ISBN **9786269640614**, and the EISBN query returns no match. The missing API edition is therefore a real reconciliation lead, but the product evidence is incomplete: Books.com gives no ebook page count, and the National Central Library’s 2025 deposit list records the same identifier as a PDF under an expected publication month of 2025-08. The print edition’s **128 pages** cannot be copied onto the ebook record. ([Books.com ebook listing](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050354706); [National Central Library deposit list](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1755143472.pdf); [Kingstone print listing](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2019910048444/); [MuseBooks creator query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E9%84%AD%E7%BE%BD%E5%87%A1); [MuseBooks EISBN query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=9786267742044))

The same EISBN is thus associated with conflicting format and timing records, and no publisher clarification resolves whether the listing was delayed, converted, or entered incorrectly. Although the Books.com page displays an ebook cover, its direct image route returned HTTP 403 during follow-up; the existing MuseBooks image belongs to the paperback and cannot safely stand in for a separately verified ebook asset. The edition should remain on hold until a reliable source confirms the ebook’s format and date, supplies its page count, and provides or verifies its cover. ([Books.com ebook listing](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050354706); [National Central Library deposit list](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1755143472.pdf); [MuseBooks work page](https://musebooks.my/books/work-tw-9bcbbed2719c0138ff33c410))

## Conclusion

The round 5 result is a **verified no-import decision**, not a claim that the search found no creator books or no apparent catalog gaps. Rina’s release needs an accessible primary product record, complete print metadata, and an identified final cover; 《韓你菲》 needs a recoverable seller or creator order page plus edition details and fulfillment evidence; 鄭羽凡’s ebook needs reconciled format/date metadata, ebook pagination, and a direct or independently confirmed digital cover. Until those records are available, title discovery alone does not support a database edition or R2 cover. The search focused on retailer, publisher, news, and creator-announcement trails through 2026-10-30; it was not an exhaustive census of Taiwan influencers or social-only preorder campaigns.
