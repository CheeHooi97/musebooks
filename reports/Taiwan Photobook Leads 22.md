# Round 22 Finds Leads, Not Imports

Round 22 checked physical retailers and creator stores, direct ebook listings, and campaign storefronts for Taiwan-origin influencer photobooks. **No new title met the import threshold, so no catalog, database, or R2 write was made.** The clearest fresh lead is Q尼 / 郭珉妏’s physical booklet 《夏日的天台》: the MyShip product photo shows a finished booklet and creator identifiers, and independent profiles place her in Kaohsiung, while live MuseBooks searches returned no match. It remains a hold because the seller provides no standalone final-cover asset or basic edition metadata, and the product page does not clearly map each cover to each offer. Other surfaced physical works were already cataloged or lacked enough product evidence; digital titles were either duplicates or lacked reliable Taiwan-origin identification.

## Retailer checks found duplicates and one unresolved first book

The physical sweep’s strongest direct-store discovery, 禾羽’s 《禾你相羽 in Okinawa》, already resolves to two MuseBooks works, including the 2026 edition with two physical records. 《一棋一會：斐棋寫真》, 王芯羽’s 《芯裡都是你》, 瑟七’s 《微澀．微瑟》, and the TOKKI CUTIE team’s 《FIRST JUMP》 likewise resolve to existing work records, so these are duplicate checks rather than import candidates. The latter title appears as separate physical and ebook works in the API, a catalog relationship worth reviewing separately, but it is not a missing edition. 《韓你菲》 remains the most promising uncatalogued physical book from this retail pass: FTV identifies it as 菲菲 / 李恩菲’s first personal photobook, and an Instagram-bio aggregator captures a preorder reference. But the accessible trail still lacks a verified creator-direct checkout page and a final cover tied to a fulfilled copy; the exact-title MuseBooks search returning zero is not, by itself, proof that an import is ready. 《向你走來：向理來寫真》 is a verified physical product but out of scope because the seller identifies the featured performer as Japanese-born. ## Q尼’s booklet is the strongest fresh lead, still on hold

Q尼 / 郭珉妏’s own-named 7-Eleven MyShip storefront lists physical photo booklets and dispatch timing; the seller page describes a booklet of about 21 × 19 cm. A product photograph shows a finished booklet titled 《夏日的天台》 alongside a creator card bearing “Qni” and `Instagram @qni510510`, connecting the product to the same handle used by her public profile. A 2015 profile identifies 郭珉妏 as Queenie and links her to Kaohsiung, while a later Mirror Media article describes Q尼 as a social-media influencer. These sources establish a credible Taiwan creator and a real physical-product lead, and live MuseBooks searches for the listing ID, her name and handle, and both title strings returned zero. ([MyShip product listing](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/general/detail/GM2508203612183), [MyShip seller profile](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/seller/profile?id=GM2508203612183), [photographed 《夏日的天台》 booklet](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/i/cgdm/GM2508203612183/2508200780268720.jpg), [Q尼 creator profile](https://msha.ke/qni510510/), [ZEEK profile](https://zeekmagazine.com/archives/12399), [Mirror Media profile](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20210429edi031/index.html), [MuseBooks listing-ID query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=GM2508203612183), [MuseBooks title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E5%A4%8F%E6%97%A5%E7%9A%84%E5%A4%A9%E5%8F%B0))

The decision remains **HOLD**, not GO. The booklet’s cover can be read in a product-set photograph, but there is no separate final-cover file matched to the individual 《夏日的天台》 offer; the listing does not provide pagination, publication date, or ISBN, and it is unclear how that named booklet maps to the separate two-book 《怦然心動》 option. The other two 《怦然心動》 covers appear only as a promotional pair without individual titles. Before import, obtain a standalone final-cover image tied to the exact offer, clarify each booklet’s title and format, and capture the available edition details from the seller or creator. ([MyShip listing](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/general/detail/GM2508203612183), [《夏日的天台》 product photo](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/i/cgdm/GM2508203612183/2508200780268720.jpg), [《怦然心動》 two-cover promotional image](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/i/cgdm/GM2508203612183/2508220782358201.jpg))

## Conclusion

This round improved the evidence around one plausible new Taiwan creator product, but it did not meet the cover-and-edition standard needed to add a trustworthy record. Keep Q尼’s 《夏日的天台》 at the top of the follow-up queue; obtain the exact standalone cover and clarify the booklet options before considering an import. For digital candidates, prioritize a primary creator profile that ties the photographed subject to Taiwan; for 《韓你菲》, verify the creator-linked checkout and the delivered cover. No R2 or database changes were made in Round 22.
