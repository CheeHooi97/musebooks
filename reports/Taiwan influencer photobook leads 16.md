The latest verified addition to MuseBooks is Ely Cosplay’s physical 《L'été~VI》, whose creator shop specifies a 64-page B5 book and whose R2 cover passed the database read-back check. ## Conclusion

The catalog now has one recent, verifiably completed addition—Ely’s physical photobook—while Round 16’s source checks prevented duplicate work and separated genuine preorders from records ready to publish. The next useful checks are tied to concrete evidence changes: confirm shipment and a title-matched jacket for 四月桐, 洪蓉, and Q尼; revisit 晏人物 and 蘋蘋澎澎 after their expected December fulfillment; and verify 李昊禹’s creator origin before treating his digital book as in scope.
