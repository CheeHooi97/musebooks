# Korean Influencer Photobook Research Update — 2026-10-30

This is an incremental catalog update, not an exhaustive inventory. Seven new works are verified in MuseBooks: two physical photobooks and five digital PDF photobooks. The supporting product and creator evidence is linked below; all seven database records are published and pass their read-only integrity audits.
