([Gumroad product page](https://pinkribbon.gumroad.com/l/seul003))

## Conclusion

Creator-store pages can supply enough evidence for a useful catalog record even when conventional bibliographic details are absent, provided the subject’s creator identity and product format are independently grounded. Here the same source that verifies the photobook also sets a clear boundary on media reuse; R2 remains empty until rights for a specific image are established.
