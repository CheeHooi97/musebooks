# 彭彭’s Ebook Expands Taiwan Photobook Catalog

彭彭’s fixed-layout EPUB, **《彭然心動：彭彭寫真（隨書附贈海報1張：共2款，隨機投入1款）》**, is now recorded as a digital edition under her existing physical-book work. The read-only database snapshot shows **two editions**, one featured credit for 彭彭, and one media row for the new ebook cover; its R2 object passed authenticated and public signed-URL hash checks. The ebook listing confirms **40 digital-only photos**, but two retailers give different release dates, so the database leaves the ebook date unset; no ebook page count was found. This builds on the previous import of JTWINS’ two separately identified digital photobooks, BROTHER and BROMANCE, which have distinct Taiwan EISBNs. ([National Central Library deposit list](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1697771178.pdf)) A second physical title, 博文 TTENG’s *Play Bass Boy*, is verifiable but belongs to a musician/artist rather than the influencer scope, while 菲菲’s 《韓你菲》 still lacks the product page and identified cover required for import.

## Conflicting retailer dates keep ebook fields conservative

Books.com dates the ebook **2026-09-11**, while BOOK☆WALKER gives **2026-10-12**. The catalog therefore leaves the digital release date unset instead of selecting one retailer’s date. Neither ebook product page establishes its page count, so the print edition’s 144-page figure stays with the physical edition. The standard ebook price is NT$476 across several listings, while observed promotional prices differ by retailer and date; a single temporary discount should not be mistaken for its stable list price. ## Two physical leads call for different treatment

博文 TTENG’s *Play Bass Boy* is a verified 92-page physical photobook from Warner Music Taiwan, with a 170 × 260 mm book format. Warner identifies 博文 as TRASH’s bassist, and a Taiwan music profile describes TRASH as a Taiwanese band. Eslite lists barcode **EAN 5026854399287**, not an ISBN, and dates the item 2026-01-22; Warner describes a January 23 launch. Its product is a strong adjacent Taiwan musician/artist lead, but the available evidence does not establish a social-media influencer photobook, so it was left outside this import. ([Warner Music Taiwan product page](https://www.warnermusic.com.tw/products/%E5%8D%9A%E6%96%87-tteng-%E9%A6%96%E9%83%A8%E5%80%8B%E4%BA%BA%E5%89%B5%E4%BD%9C%E7%B4%80%E5%AF%A6%E3%80%8Aplay-bass-boy%E3%80%8B%E8%81%B2%E6%99%AF%E5%AF%AB%E7%9C%9F%E8%BC%AF); [Eslite listing](https://www.eslite.com/product/10032019212683082245002); [Warner artist page](https://www.warnermusic.com.tw/pages/-trash); [MyPlus profile](https://www.myplus.com.tw/article-20210828/1672/1))

## Conclusion

The new addition shows how the catalog can grow without duplicating a creator’s work: the ebook shares the existing 彭彭 title identity but keeps its own EISBN, format, media record, and incomplete date/page-count fields. The remaining physical leads show why announcement coverage alone is not enough for a dependable edition record; subsequent research should prioritize recovering direct sales pages and cover provenance for 《韓你菲》, while treating musician photobooks as a separate adjacent category.
