# Five Titles Expand Korea’s Photobook Catalog

This pass added **five creator-linked photobooks** to the live database: three physical 2019 Impact Crew editions and two digital cosplay books sold by Song Joo A (송주아). Retail product pages and Korean press reports support the Impact titles, while Song Joo A’s own link page points directly to both Gumroad products. A live duplicate check found no matching records before import; the database read-back confirms **five works and five editions across four people**, with **zero media references**. The scoped audit found no integrity issues and no people missing provenance, and the public verifier now reports **372 physical and 5,096 digital photobooks**. The records remain metadata-only because no source documents reuse permission for any exact cover or interior image.

## Three Impact Crew editions have matching product and creator evidence

**Min Hanna’s** *민한나 임팩트화보집 [2019]* is listed by 11st/YES24 with publisher Impact Crew, a 2019-11-01 date, and ISBN **9791196797812**. A YES24 used-book record for the same title and ISBN supplies the 152-page count. Sports Seoul identifies Min Hanna as a racing model and influencer and reports that Impact Crew produced and published her solo photobook, linking the creator to the exact type of work cataloged here. ([11st/YES24 product](https://www.11st.co.kr/products/2613122280), [YES24 used-book record](https://m.yes24.com/Goods/Detail/139684649), [Sports Seoul via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20191026112710609?s=print_news))

**Jeong Yuna’s** *임팩트 정유나 화보집* has the strongest cross-catalog bibliographic match in this group: Youngpoong and Kyobo agree on Impact Crew, 2019-08-05, and 144 pages; Youngpoong supplies ISBN **9791196406820**, while Kyobo gives dimensions of 225 × 265 mm. Ilgan Sports identifies Jeong Yuna as a model and influencer at the Impact Crew photobook showcase, tying the title to a public creator identity. ([Youngpoong product](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202308233083264414), [Kyobo product](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000001976016), [Ilgan Sports](https://isplus.com/article/view/isp201908310012))

**Jung Hyeyul’s** *임팩트 정혜율 화보집* is likewise listed by Youngpoong as a 144-page Impact Crew book dated 2019-08-05, with ISBN **9791196406813**; Kyobo’s matching product record corroborates the title and date. Halla Ilbo reports Impact Crew’s production and the preorder period, while Sports Seoul describes Jung Hyeyul’s modeling and social-media profile and notes her photobooks. No dimensions were stored because the available retailer record does not provide a reliable measurement. ([Youngpoong product](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202308233083234211), [Kyobo product](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000001976015), [Halla Ilbo](https://s1.ihalla.com/section.php?page=10&sid=221&total_record=1161), [Sports Seoul via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20200323123309765))

## Song Joo A’s storefront verifies two downloadable cosplay books

Song Joo A (송주아) sells *MAKIMA PHOTOBOOK* and *MIHARA PHOTOBOOK* as downloadable, high-resolution photo collections. Her “Jooasong Official” Linktree links to both exact Gumroad listings, and the listings identify the seller as 송주아 (Song Joo A), which provides a direct creator-to-product connection. A Korean gaming publication identifies 송주아 as a racing model and records the Instagram handle `@ww0205ww`; nationality is not independently established by the sources, so the Korean classification reflects documented creator activity and product context rather than an assertion about citizenship. ([Jooasong Official Linktree](https://linktr.ee/Jooasong), [Digital Post / PC Love profile](https://www.ilovepc.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=21711))

The *MAKIMA* listing describes three *Chainsaw Man* concepts and a 60-page, 560 MB JPEG download. *MIHARA* is described as an A4 digital cosplay book with Maid, Bonding & Chain, and Pain Eater concepts; the seller calls it approximately 70 pages, so the database leaves its normalized page-count field blank. Neither listing states a publication date or publisher. Both records are cataloged as photobooks because the seller presents them as photo-book downloads, though they are character cosplay sets rather than conventional lifestyle portraits. ([MAKIMA PHOTOBOOK](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/makimajooa), [MIHARA PHOTOBOOK](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/miharaphotobook))

## Read-back confirms five metadata records and zero media

The pre-import duplicate check found no matching work or person records. A repeatable-read, read-only database read-back then confirmed all **five expected work IDs**, their five editions, the featured credits, and source provenance. Song Joo A is one person credited on two works, which is why the five works map to four people. The scoped audit reports **zero integrity findings**, **zero people without a verification source**, and **zero explicit media references**; it also records the known gaps—two unknown release dates, one unnormalized page count, and five missing cover URLs. ([Batch 11 database read-back](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-11-db-readback.json), [Batch 11 audit](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-11-db-verification.json))

The catalog verifier output after import is **372 physical and 5,096 digital**. Those totals describe the public catalog at verification time, while the batch artifacts scope their checks to these five records. No image was uploaded to R2: retailer display, public previews, and paid downloads do not grant redistribution rights, and no permission covering any exact image and third-party hosting was found. ([Batch 11 import manifest](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-11-import.json), [Impact Crew product sources](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202308233083264414), [Song Joo A Gumroad listings](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/makimajooa))

## Several promising leads still need exact verification

Other Impact Crew subjects remain outside the import. Lee So-young’s records show conflicting August and December 2019 listings without a resolved edition link; Kim Min-ji’s exact book metadata is available, but the common name lacks an independent identity match and used-book dimensions conflict; UNEED’s listings span several dates, and a 977-prefixed retailer code is not a valid ISBN-13. These records need stable edition identifiers or stronger subject evidence before they can be merged into the catalog. ([Kyobo Impact Crew search](https://search.kyobobook.co.kr/search?chrc=%EC%9E%84%ED%8C%A9%ED%8A%B8%ED%81%AC%EB%A3%A8&keyword=%EC%9E%84%ED%8C%A9%ED%8A%B8%ED%81%AC%EB%A3%A8), [Youngpoong Kim Min-ji product](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202308233955929488), [Aladin UNEED product](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=212290261), [International ISBN Agency](https://www.isbn-international.org/index.php/content/what-is-isbn/10))

The LEEHEE EXPRESS listing *WOOZIC STARS IN OKINAWA PHOTOBOOK_YORU* appears to be a physical book and names a model only as “Woo”; a separate Fanding page links Kim Woohyeon to a WOOZIC STARZ “Extreme” paper-photobook project, but no source directly connects that campaign to the YORU retail title. Song Joo A’s Linktree also names *ASUNA* and *EVE* photobooks without enough product details to import, while WinterD’s CTEE store advertises a web pictorial without a stable item title or adequately verified Korean identity. Each remains a lead rather than a database record. ([LEEHEE EXPRESS YORU product](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060), [Kim Woohyeon Fanding profile](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/), [Jooasong Official Linktree](https://linktr.ee/Jooasong), [겨우디 CTEE store](https://ctee.kr/place/winterd/store?group=8856))

## Conclusion

This pass broadens the collection across retail print editions and creator-sold digital cosplay sets while preserving the distinction between verified product metadata and incomplete leads. The remaining R2 task depends on finding a license or written permission that covers the specific image assets and hosting use; public availability alone does not fill that gap.
