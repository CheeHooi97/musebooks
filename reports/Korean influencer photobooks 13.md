# Five Digital Photobooks Saved; More Leads Need Verification

This pass verified five exact PDF photobooks in the *시니어 모델 강규남 뉴페이스 시니어* series by senior model Kang Gyu-nam. Exact product pages provide release dates, publishers, ISBNs and page counts for Volumes 1, 2, 4 and 6; Kyobo provides complete core metadata for Volume 5, though not a page count. Kyobo/YES24 contents identify a chapter titled “강규남 화보집” in each of the five editions, so Kang is supported as a featured subject as well as author. **Five works and five digital editions were added to the database and verified by read-back. No cover or image was copied to R2.** The previous six works and seven editions from batch 12 remain in the catalog ([batch 12 database read-back](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-db-readback.json); [this batch’s database read-back](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-13-db-readback.json)). No source found here grants reuse rights for an exact cover or image in R2.

## Five digital editions have complete metadata and verified subject evidence

RIDI lists Volumes 1, 2, 4 and 6 as paid PDF e-books, each released on **2022-02-11**. Volume 1 is 58 pages, ISBN **9791190821117**; Volume 2 is 58 pages, ISBN **9791190821292**; Volume 4 is 64 pages, ISBN **9791190821841**; and Volume 6 is 55 pages, ISBN **9791190821896** ([RIDI Vol. 1](https://ridibooks.com/books/1745005096), [RIDI Vol. 2](https://ridibooks.com/books/1745005097), [RIDI Vol. 4](https://ridibooks.com/books/1745005099), [RIDI Vol. 6](https://ridibooks.com/books/1745005101)). Kyobo lists Volume 5 as a paid PDF from 도서출판 이음, released the same day, ISBN **9791190821889**; its rendered product page does not state the total page count ([Kyobo Vol. 5](https://ebook-product.kyobobook.co.kr/dig/epd/ebook/E000003606034)).

A product contents section on each edition includes chapter 3, **강규남 화보집** (“Kang Gyu-nam photobook”). This is shown on Kyobo’s exact product pages for Volumes 1, 5 and 6, and YES24’s exact product pages for Volumes 2 and 4 ([Kyobo Vol. 1](https://ebook-product.kyobobook.co.kr/dig/epd/ebook/E000003606030), [YES24 Vol. 2](https://www.yes24.com/Product/Goods/107445978), [YES24 Vol. 4](https://www.yes24.com/Product/Goods/107445975), [Kyobo Vol. 5](https://ebook-product.kyobobook.co.kr/dig/epd/ebook/E000003606034), [Kyobo Vol. 6](https://ebook-product.kyobobook.co.kr/dig/epd/ebook/E000003606035)). The K-Senior Model Association profile independently supports Kang’s senior-model identity ([association profile](https://jfs77081.wixsite.com/k-seniormodel)).

Before import, a live database lookup found no match for these five ISBNs and no Kang Gyu-nam work. The reviewed manifest passed with `Validated 5 import records`; the importer then reported `Catalog records and price observations committed; listing image provenance records: 0`. A repeatable-read database read-back confirmed **5 works, 5 digital editions, 5 featured-person credits and 0 media references** ([read-back JSON](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-13-db-readback.json)). No listing-price observations were added because this import only records bibliographic metadata. Volume 3 remains unimported because an individual exact product record with complete edition details was not verified ([RIDI series listing](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/common/wseriesitem.aspx?srid=1022568)).
## Seojin and Pyo Eunji leads need a stronger catalog link

Seojin’s Atelier sells *A Little Early Summer, A Lot More Seojin | Clear Blue Vol. 02* as a downloadable **162-page digital photobook** containing 39 photo series and 357 photographs. The product page gives no year, ISBN, or publisher, and the creator profile does not establish Seojin’s Korean nationality, location, or work as a Korean influencer/model ([Clear Blue Vol. 02](https://www.patreon.com/seojinsatelier/posts/little-early-lot-169117233), [Seojin’s Atelier profile](https://www.patreon.com/cw/seojinsatelier)). It is therefore a verified product lead but not yet a verified Korean-influencer record. The product page limits the file to personal use and bars redistribution, resale, and commercial use, so it also supplies no basis for copying artwork to R2 ([Clear Blue Vol. 02 terms](https://www.patreon.com/seojinsatelier/posts/little-early-lot-169117233)).

Pyo Eunji’s official store offers a paid C106 set containing three physical B5 books: *Ping Pong: Mini-Skirt Delivery Diary* (62 pages), *The Side of Me That No One Sees* (56 pages), and *Beyond the Secret Curtain* (56 pages). The store calls them “new books,” not photobooks, and does not identify Pyo as their author or photographed subject ([official C106 set](https://pyoeunji.com/en/products/c106set)). Shueisha independently identifies Pyo as Korean-born and working as a model and YouTuber, but that does not resolve her relationship to the books ([Weekly Playboy profile](https://wpb.shueisha.co.jp/gravure/20220821-117087/)). One resale listing labels *The Side of Me That No One Sees* a photo book and says Pyo signed it; that seller description is only a lead, not authoritative genre or subject verification ([eBay resale listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/397121672933)). The store’s site terms reserve image rights and prohibit unauthorized reproduction ([Pyo Eunji site copyright page](https://pyoeunji.com/ko/pages/about)).

## Print and storefront leads remain unresolved

Pyo’s C108 announcement and a Cupick event listing point to another set of three books, but they do not name the titles or establish their genre ([Pyo’s C108 announcement](https://pyoeunji.com/en/blogs/blogs), [Cupick event listing](https://cupick.jp/products/pyoeunji-birthday-event)). The previous research still has stronger-looking print leads that lack product-level or identity evidence: Crazy Giant’s *Sujeong* volumes and Min Hanna’s *Flower Blooms* lack direct individual edition records; Kim Min-ji’s work lacks a reliable identity match; and Lee So-young and UNEED listings have unresolved edition/date variants ([Crazy Giant Sujeong report](https://crazygiant.co.kr/giantgirl/?bmode=view&idx=175104830), [Xports on Min Hanna](https://www.xportsnews.com/article/1497472?ac=law), [Youngpoong Kim Min-ji listing](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202308233955929488), [Kyobo Lee So-young](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000001980674), [Aladin UNEED](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=212290261)).

Other digital-storefront candidates also need verification before they can be attributed to a unique Korean creator. BimilStory’s *Yeonjju Vol.01* post calls the title a photobook but provides no direct paid-download edition details; its *Eunsu Vol.01* PDF/JPG product is private and no longer for sale, and Eunsu’s identity is unresolved ([Yeonjju post](https://www.patreon.com/bimilstory/posts/yeonjju-vol-01-153790186), [Eunsu product page](https://ctee.kr/item/store/33737)). RIDI and Aladin list monthly *New Face Senior* issues, but the surfaced records do not establish the individual models depicted ([RIDI March 2022 issue](https://ridibooks.com/books/1745005650)). These are useful next research targets, not additions made in this pass.

## Conclusion

The strongest new outcome is the successful import of five additional digital photobooks with verified featured-subject evidence. Seojin’s Korean creator identity, Pyo Eunji’s C106 book genres and credits, and volume 3’s exact edition details remain open research leads. Exact-image reuse permission is still absent, so this pass made no R2 uploads.
