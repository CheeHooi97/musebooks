# Add Two Verified Korean Photobooks to MuseBooks

Two more Korean influencer photobook products have cleared title and creator checks and are now published in MuseBooks as metadata-only records: MAXQ *Secret B* Vol. 11 featuring Jeong Hanna and *Secret B + MAXQ Compilation* Vol. 02 featuring Yoon Na-gyeom. Aladin and RIDI identify the products and formats, while contemporaneous Korean press supports each creator’s influencer profile. Live database readbacks confirm one work and one digital edition for each; their scoped audits found no integrity failures. The compilation description says it includes selected and unreleased Secret B and MAXQ images, so its image overlap with Yoon’s previously cataloged Vol. 9 remains unreviewed. This incremental research set now contains nine additions—two physical books and seven digital books—without establishing exhaustive coverage of Korean creators. No cover or PDF was copied to R2 because exact-image reuse permission remains unconfirmed.

## Two digital products now clear the influencer bar

Aladin’s Secret B series catalog lists the exact Vol. 11 title for Jeong Hanna as a PDF e-book dated August 2022. Sports Seoul reported the Vol. 11 shoot, and Maeil Business explicitly described Hanna as a fitness influencer. The new MuseBooks record therefore uses month-level release precision; the exact day, page count, and ISBN were not verified. Its readback shows a published work with a featured influencer credit and one Korean digital edition. The scoped audit reports one work and one edition, no listings or media references, and zero integrity findings. ## Metadata is in PostgreSQL; R2 stays rights-gated

Both new records are published, with exact title, creator credit, format, publisher, and the available release metadata preserved in PostgreSQL. The public-catalog verifier passed this turn and reported 345 physical and 5,066 digital works in total; those are aggregate catalog counts, not the number of additions in this report. The missing data is explicit: Hanna’s pagination, ISBN, and exact release day are unknown; the compilation has no ISBN or captured retail listing, and its image-level overlap remains unchecked. ([Jeong Hanna audit](../docs/korean-secret-b-11-jeong-hanna-db-verification.json); [Yoon Na-gyeom audit](../docs/korean-secret-b-maxq-vol02-yoon-na-gyeom-db-verification.json))

Neither covers nor paid PDFs were transferred to R2. A retailer’s public product page or image URL does not grant permission to mirror the asset; the catalog’s source guidance requires documented reuse rights. Until the publisher or creator grants those rights, the records remain useful bibliographic entries with missing-cover gaps rather than image-complete entries. ([Korean photobook source guide](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md))

## Conclusion

These additions show that Korean fitness and creator photobooks are discoverable through retailer series catalogs cross-checked against publisher and press coverage. For this catalog, exact product evidence supports bibliographic records, while same-person compilations need separate image-level review before the catalog treats them as unique photographic content. The remaining R2 gap is a licensing question distinct from the metadata work: fuller asset coverage requires documented permission for each exact image or file.
