## Jung Da-yeon’s print edition is verified, but its ISBN and date are not

Youngpoong lists **임팩트 정다연 화보집** as a 204-page physical book from Impact Crew. Its product record labels **9772635471005** as an ISBN-13, but that prefix is not valid for an ISBN-13: the International ISBN Agency specifies the ISBN-13 prefix element as 978 or 979. The same 977-prefixed structure is used for EAN barcodes associated with serial publications, but that alone does not establish an ISSN or identify a serial title. The catalog therefore leaves ISBN blank instead of repeating the retailer’s mislabeled field. ([Youngpoong product record](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202308233114560575), [International ISBN Agency](https://www.isbn-international.org/index.php/content/what-isbn/10), [ISSN International Centre](https://www.issn.org/understanding-the-issn/issn-uses/identification-with-the-ean-13-barcode/))

The publication date is also left blank. Youngpoong gives 2019-08-08 and Kyobo lists a related Impact Crew record on 2019-08-12, while contemporary reporting describes online presales beginning in March and an April report uses ambiguous wording about publication and sellout. These entries could reflect separate preorder and retail dates, but the available evidence does not establish the actual release or fulfillment date. Sports Seoul and Wikitree link Jung Da-yeon to her fitness-model work and social account, supporting a featured-model credit without suggesting she authored or photographed the book. ([Kyobo Impact Crew listing](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000000330301), [Newstown preorder report](https://www.newstown.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=405209), [Seoul News report](https://m.go.seoul.co.kr/news/twig/entertainments/2019/04/24/20190424500112?cp=go), [Sports Seoul profile](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/749739), [Wikitree profile](https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/453827))

## LOVEYS Vol.2 and Vol.3 are separate creator-led digital issues

RINI BOOKS sells **LOVEYS Vol.2 – 소녀, 그리고 설레임** and **LOVEYS Vol.3 – Winter Trip** on separate Gumroad pages with their own PDF and JPG files. Vol.2 states 43 pages and calls itself a November issue, but provides no reliable year or exact release date; Vol.3 provides no page count or date. The two products are best represented as separate digital works in the same series rather than as one bundle. ([LOVEYS Vol.2 listing](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_02), [LOVEYS Vol.3 listing](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_03))

The creator writes in first person as Seorin on the product pages and identifies LOVEYS as her monthly photo diary in an earlier Patreon post. Her creator-controlled Patreon account uses the display name YooSeorin, describes LOVEYS and RINIQ as her magazines, and categorizes RINIQ as Korean glamour/model content with “Korean Model” tags. This supports crediting the public creator persona **Seorin/YooSeorin**; it does not verify a legal name or citizenship, so neither is inferred in the catalog. ([YooSeorin publishing guide](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/2026nyeon-balmae-147382927?l=de), [LOVEYS Vol.1 creator post](https://www.patreon.com/posts/loveys-vol-1-141787962), [RINIQ Vol.6 creator post](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/riniq-vol-6-ver-157047855))
