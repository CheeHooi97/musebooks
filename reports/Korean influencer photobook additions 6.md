# Korean Influencer Photobook Addition 6

## Added to the database

*MAXQ Special Edition Vol. 3* is now cataloged as a 41-page digital PDF photobook released December 24, 2020 by MAXQ International. The retailer identifies it as an online photobook; MAXQ-sourced contemporary coverage names Jeong Hanna, Lee Ha-rin, Kang Ju-yeon, and Lee Seon-i as the four featured models. Jeong Hanna is already documented as a fitness influencer; the other three are recorded as models because this pass did not establish their separate creator activity. ([RIDI product](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000074), [Newstown report](https://www.newstown.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=473998), [Maeil Business on Hanna's influencer profile](https://www.mk.co.kr/news/entertain/10007223))
