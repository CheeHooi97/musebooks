# Clear Four Photobook Leads Before Import

Batch 18 surfaced three exact titles: a print-on-demand photobook for Kim Young Ju, the physical `캔디 화보집 NO.1 꾸뿌` issue, and Studio BOM’s digital Eundam title *Leather Office Girl*. A fourth exact Patreon listing names Gyuri, but its creator’s Korean identity is unverified. The two physical titles returned no duplicates in live-catalog checks; Eundam’s title and person lookup also returned no matches. Those checks establish only that matching catalog rows were not found, not that the records are clear to import. Retailer terms, unavailable or inconclusive alternate bibliographic sources, and unresolved format or identity questions keep these leads on hold. **No database rows or R2 images were added in batch 18**, and no source reviewed grants permission to copy a particular cover or image to R2.

## Two physical titles have live checks but lack a cleared source

Aladin lists **KIM YOUNG JU 김영주 화보집** as a physical print-on-demand book with ISBN `9791197931567`, while its author profile describes a fitness model and cultural planner whose book collects her modeling photographs. The Korean Publishers Association’s May 2023 new-title record corroborates the title, publisher and ISBN, but reports 133 pages against Aladin’s 134; the KPA PDF shows no reuse license. The cited material supports a model photobook, but does not establish online influencer reach, so keep the person classification at model. The read-only live lookup found **zero matching work or edition rows** for `김영주`, `YOUNG JU` or that ISBN. Aladin’s Article 20(2) prohibits using information obtained through its mall for profit without prior consent, so the retailer-derived record is not cleared for commercial catalog use. The National Library ISBN API could not resolve the discrepancy because its required `cert_key` was not configured; an authorized API response or publisher source is still needed. ([Aladin listing](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=307017330), [Aladin author profile](https://www.aladin.co.kr/author/wauthor_overview.aspx?AuthorSearch=%409123536), [KPA May 2023 new-title record](https://member.kpa21.or.kr/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/2023%EB%85%84-5%EC%9B%94%EC%9D%98-%EC%8B%A0%EA%B0%84.pdf), [Aladin terms, Article 20(2)](https://www.aladin.co.kr/m/join/mJoin_rules.aspx?rule=5), [National Library API guide](https://www.nl.go.kr/NL/contents/N31101030500.do); [batch 18 physical research and DB check](../research_notes/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2018/physical_titles.md))

## Eundam’s exact digital title remains behind Patreon’s terms

Studio BOM’s Patreon Shop lists **“[19 Photos] 2025 06(Jun) No.1 Eundam 은담 - Leather Office Girl”** as a one-time digital download, described as Eundam’s first June pictorial photobook and containing 19 images. Studio BOM’s profile identifies Oh Eundam as a Korean model, YouTuber and BJ, and a separate model profile links the Eundam name to influencer work. The live lookup found no matching title or person. However, no independent non-Patreon product source for this exact edition was found. Patreon’s terms limit access to creator creations to personal, non-commercial use, and its Security Policy prohibits crawling, scraping or indexing Patreon information; those terms do not clearly establish permission for this catalog’s commercial metadata use. The listing is therefore a discovery lead, not an import authorization, and it does not grant rights to host its photos or preview images.
