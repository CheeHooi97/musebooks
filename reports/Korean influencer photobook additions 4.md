# Catalog Two Newly Verified Korean Photobooks

Batch 138 added **two works with five physical editions** to the Korean catalog: Rustic Photobook 24SS, represented by three model-specific editions, and Kim Woo-hyun’s 2023 *WOO HYEON'S Nxde PhotoBook*, represented by two volumes. The repeatable-read snapshot confirms both works, all five editions, and four featured credits; the scoped audit reports no integrity failures. The Nxde project is separate from Kim’s already cataloged 2021 *우현-아이 화보집*. Where the campaigns omit publication details, the catalog leaves the release date, page count, ISBN, and publisher unknown. The records have no covers because permission to reuse the exact campaign images in R2 has not been confirmed. Yeo Si-yeon's Vol. 7 remains on hold pending evidence of her own creator activity; the Anessa/Hurricane Kimchi campaign was resolved and imported in [addition 7](Korean%20influencer%20photobook%20additions%207.md).

## Rustic Photobook 24SS contributes three versions to one work

Rustic Studio’s Wadiz campaign presents *러스틱 사진집 24SS* as a photo book with three model-specific versions: gymnast and influencer Kan Hyeon-bae, fitness model and influencer Kim Dong-yoon, and climbing influencer Shim Min-sup. The campaign ran December 6–20, 2023; that is the campaign period, not a verified publication date. It does not supply pagination, ISBN, publisher imprint, or a confirmed release date, so those fields remain blank. The import treats the three versions as editions of one work and does not claim their interior photographs are unique. ([Wadiz campaign](https://www.wadiz.io/web/campaign/detail/250208); [import manifest](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-138-import.json))

## Nxde is a distinct physical campaign product

Fanding’s 2023 campaign identifies Kim Woo-hyun’s *WOO HYEON'S Nxde PhotoBook*. Its delivery-enabled rewards separately offer EP 1: Mandarin and EP 2: Cyber Punk; a later creator-community post calls it a paper photobook. The importer records those as two physical editions under one work. The campaign’s August 16, 2023 reward due date is not treated as a publication or actual shipment date, and no page count, ISBN, publisher imprint, or exact release date was found. ([Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/funding/140/); [creator’s paper-photobook post](https://fanding.kr/ja/@woohye0n/community/42256/))

This work is distinct from the previously cataloged 2021 *우현-아이 화보집*, which YES24 lists under product 106517266 and publisher 우직스타즈. The 2021 book remains its own catalog record; Nxde has not been merged with it. Kim’s later *Extreme* project uses “first paper photobook with WOOZIC STARZ” wording, which the available sources do not reconcile with the 2023 paper-book evidence. ([YES24 2021 product](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/106517266); [Nxde campaign](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/funding/140/); [creator profile and posts](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/))

## One research lead remains on hold

The reviewed interview only documents an SNS ambassador appointment and a plan to start a YouTube channel; it does not establish that Yeo operated a channel or created social content. Hold the product until a source shows Yeo's own creator activity and a live-catalog duplicate check is completed. ([RIDI product 3563000117](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000117); [Sports Seoul interview via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/ksVuguwoWi?f=p))

Anessa and Hurricane Kimchi's 10th-anniversary photobook and poster campaign was later verified as a creator-led physical photobook project. The creator and catalog checks are resolved; actual shipment and exact-image reuse permission remain unverified. See [addition 7](Korean%20influencer%20photobook%20additions%207.md) and the [research note](../research_notes/Korean%20influencer%20photobook%20additions%207/hurricane_kimchi_drag10.md).

## Catalog verification passed; R2 rights remain unresolved

The batch manifest, repeatable-read database snapshot, and scoped audit are available for review. The snapshot contains **2 works, 5 physical editions, and 4 featured credits**; the audit reports **zero integrity failures**, zero linked listings, zero media references, and five missing covers. The appended scraper report records public-catalog verification at 358 physical and 5,087 digital works after the import. ([manifest](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-138-import.json); [database snapshot](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-138-db-readback.json); [scoped audit](../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-138-db-verification.json); [import report](../scraper/catalog-import-report.md))

No cover or promotional asset was copied to R2. Public campaign pages document product provenance, but the exact images lack confirmed reuse permission; the audit records no explicit media references and flags all five covers as missing. The next additions should resolve the remaining Yeo Si-yeon lead, while any R2 asset remains dependent on rights evidence for that specific image.

## Conclusion

Batch 138 expands the catalog with a verified crowdfunding photobook and a three-version creator title while preserving missing bibliographic fields instead of inferring them. The product records are saved, but this research pass is not exhaustive; the Yeo Si-yeon lead and image-rights question remain open.
