## 關關 has product and cover evidence but unclear origin

Pubu directly lists *關關寫真集2-Vol.02* as a 73-page PDF released September 11, 2025, with NT$699 DRM and NT$1,399 DRM-free options. Its cover reads “G-JAN 關關 II / Fitness Prince” and matches the product title; the image was retrieved successfully and hashed in the research notes. However, the listing gives only the alias 關關 and does not supply a full identity or reliable source tying this creator to Taiwan. Although targeted MuseBooks searches returned no match, a zero-result query is not a substitute for the missing origin evidence. **Keep on hold; do not upload or import on this evidence alone.** ([Pubu product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/604421); [title-matched cover](https://res1.pubu.tw/docs/512992/13071/QXSQbl_l.jpg); [MuseBooks item query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=604421); [MuseBooks title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E9%97%9C%E9%97%9C%E5%AF%AB%E7%9C%9F%E9%9B%862-Vol.02))
