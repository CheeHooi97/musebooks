# Taiwan photobook research adds verified editions

This research cycle added a newly verified physical photobook by Taiwan creator 瑀熙 (Yuci) to MuseBooks and R2, while a live database check prevented duplicate work for 良良’s two-volume ebook series. The new 瑀熙 record is **《只想瑀你．初體驗 miss you photobook》**, ISBN **9786269709908**, a 160-page paperback; its Kingstone gallery cover was uploaded and verified against the live signed media route. The immediately preceding catalog update added 彭彭’s fixed-layout EPUB as a second edition under her existing print work, with a distinct EISBN and cover. 《韓你菲》 remains a credible physical-book lead, but a direct seller page and an explicitly identified cover still cannot be verified, so it was not imported. Public catalog verification passed with 345 physical and 5,064 digital works at the time of this check; those are global totals that include concurrent catalog activity. The search remains active because Taiwan creator releases are spread across retailer, publisher, and social preorder pages, and this research did not establish an exhaustive list.

## 瑀熙’s 160-page paperback is now in the catalog

瑀熙 fits the creator scope through her documented Twitch game-streaming and YouTube lifestyle work; TVBS describes her as an influencer and YouTuber, and the Kingstone biography says she began streaming in 2015 and started her YouTube channel in 2019. The National Central Library deposit record identifies her as author of a Taiwan-published paperback, 《只想瑀你初體驗 = miss you photobook》. These sources support inclusion based on her creator career and Taiwan publication context without making an unsupported claim about nationality. ([TVBS profile](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/entertainment/2434263); [Kingstone listing](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2019910049946/); [National Central Library deposit list](https://nclfile.ncl.edu.tw/files/202307/27cfb872-5f11-473e-8173-f00dfe5d7ca7.pdf))

Kingstone and Sanmin independently identify the physical edition as a 水靈文創 paperback with **ISBN 9786269709908** and **160 pages**. Their release dates differ—2023-02-24 at Kingstone and 2023-03-06 at Sanmin—so the database leaves the date unset. The Kingstone product gallery links the cover image used for the import. ([Kingstone listing](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2019910049946/); [Sanmin listing](https://www.sanmin.com.tw/product/index/011520713); [Kingstone cover asset](https://cdn.kingstone.com.tw/book/images/product/20199/2019910049946/2019910049946b.jpg))

The record is saved as work `work-tw-1a8acb668fee685d62d0dcd5`, with the physical edition and cover in the live catalog. The R2 cover’s SHA-256 is **a8f527cf04233c3bfdbf21c6cc9a99432b00d3b0a24dd517ad2d9b46269ba5a1**; authenticated R2 readback and the public signed-media fetch matched. The import dry run returned `Validated 1 import records`; apply returned `Validated 1 import records` and `Catalog records and price observations committed; listing image provenance records: 0`. The current public-catalog verifier also passed its pagination and format checks, reporting 345 physical and 5,064 digital works. These totals are system-wide and should not be read as the number of Taiwan additions in this batch.

## 《韓你菲》 still lacks import-grade product evidence

Taiwan press reports identify 菲菲／李恩菲’s 《韓你菲》 as her first solo physical photobook and announced a 2026-09-02, 8 p.m. sale; the official CPBL Girls profile connects her to the Dragon Beauties. Those sources confirm a real title and Taiwan creator connection, but the scheduled sale is not evidence that a specific edition was actually offered or shipped. ([UDN report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294); [FTV report](https://health.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260); [CPBL Girls profile](https://cpblgirls.tw/front/4/RakutenGirlsmen/member.php?ID=Y3BibGdpcmxzJlJha3V0ZW5HaXJsc21lbg%3D%3D&PID=86))

A third-party Instagram snapshot records a preorder-link phrase in her bio, and a Shopee search result snippet showed an alleged NT$1,380 preorder, but neither exposes a recoverable product destination and seller identity. The press photos are credited promotional images, not images explicitly identified as the book cover. Because no direct publisher or retailer product page ties together the edition details, seller, and cover, 《韓你菲》 remains unimported. The next useful evidence is the actual official preorder destination or a seller’s item page with a clearly identified cover. ([Kolr profile snapshot](https://www.kolr.ai/influencer/a7c5f083-7e76-46ad-8b53-e6ca47e18ea9); [Shopee search results](https://shopee.tw/search?keyword=%E6%9D%8E%E6%81%A9%E8%8F%B2); [UDN report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294); [FTV report](https://health.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260))

## Conclusion

The additions show the value of edition-level research: a new creator’s paperback and an existing creator’s ebook can be added without merging formats or inventing unresolved release dates, while duplicate checks prevent already represented ebooks from being inserted twice. The most actionable remaining lead is 《韓你菲》, where a direct product record and identified cover would turn an announced title into a verifiable database edition; continuing retailer and publisher searches is still necessary before the broader discovery goal is complete.
