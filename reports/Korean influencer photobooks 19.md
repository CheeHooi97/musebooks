# Add One Verified Korean Photobook Record

This research round added one source-cleared physical photobook record to the live catalog: Kim Young Ju’s 2022 *photography booklet*, ISBN `9791197931567`. The National Library of Korea (NLK) open bibliographic record supplies the title, publisher, year, extent, and size; the database read-back confirms one work, one physical edition, one featured credit, and **zero media assets**. The available identity evidence establishes Kim Young Ju as a model, but does not establish social-media influencer status, so this is adjacent catalog progress rather than a confirmed influencer addition. Jeon Yoonyoung’s first-party digital ebook lead and the Candy issue remain unimported because source authorization or product classification is unresolved. A live cover review found no exact image with documented permission for both R2 storage and public delivery, and **no image was uploaded to R2 this turn**.

## NLK’s open record cleared one physical metadata import

The public NLK LOD record `KMO202332009` identifies *Kim Young Ju: photography booklet*, published by Nadaum in 2022, with ISBN `9791197931567`, 133 pages, and a 30 cm format. NLK documents its LOD dataset under KOGL Type 1 and CC0 1.0, which cleared reuse of these factual catalog fields for this import; that metadata license does not grant rights to the cover artwork ([NLK record](https://lod.nl.go.kr/resource/KMO202332009); [NLK LOD dataset and license](https://lod.nl.go.kr/home/dataset/datadownload.do)).

Before importing, the live duplicate scan found no matching rows for the ISBN, NLK record ID, title variants, person name, or aliases. The importer reported `Validated 1 import records`; its apply run reported `Catalog records and price observations committed; listing image provenance records: 0`. The read-back confirms work `korea-kim-young-ju-photography-booklet-2022`, physical edition `korea-kim-young-ju-photography-booklet-2022-physical`, one featured credit, and no media, listings, or price observations ([import manifest](../docs/korean-kim-young-ju-photography-booklet-2022-import.json); [database read-back](../docs/korean-kim-young-ju-photography-booklet-2022-db-readback.json)). Sports Seoul identifies Kim Young Ju as a fitness model and cultural planner, which supports the database’s `model` classification; the reviewed evidence does not establish influencer reach ([Sports Seoul, 28 February 2023](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/1202040)).

Jeon Yoonyoung’s first-party portfolio names *HEART IN LOVE*, describes it as a photobook, and offers a digital/ebook edition through Gumroad. The portfolio presents Jeon as a portrait photographer and does not establish Korean nationality or influencer status. It also gives no commercial catalog-data license, while Gumroad’s terms restrict commercial exploitation of the service and scraping or downloading data from its pages. ## Catalog provenance does not grant R2 image rights

A read-only scan of the live catalog counted 142 published Korean photobook works across 153 editions. Ten editions had a cover URL and there were 20 edition-media rows, but the scan found zero cover object keys and no R2-hosted or distinct stored copies. In the influencer-focused subset, eight editions cover DJ SODA, Kim Seolhwa, and Pyo Eunji; none had documented permission for the exact image, R2 storage, and public delivery. These figures describe the catalog snapshot and records reviewed, not a legal conclusion about every possible exception ([DJ SODA physical DB record](../docs/korean-photobook-dj-soda-candid-publisher-db-verification.json); [DJ SODA digital DB record](../docs/korean-photobook-dj-soda-candid-apple-db-verification.json); [Kim Seolhwa DB record](../docs/korean-photobook-kim-seolhwa-26-yes24-db-verification.json); [Pyo Eunji DB record](../docs/korean-photobook-pyo-eunji-rondo-db-verification.json)).

The reviewed policies do not supply a blanket R2 license. Shueisha’s FAQ says it does not grant individuals permission to use book covers; KADOKAWA’s conditional cover rules route Internet distribution through an inquiry and do not document title-specific approval; Pyo Eunji’s terms restrict unauthorized reproduction and public transmission. A retailer or publisher image URL establishes where a cover was observed, not permission to mirror that exact file ([Shueisha cover-use FAQ](https://faq.shueisha.co.jp/faq/show/28?back=front%2Fcategory%3Ashow&category_id=2&faq_id=17&site_domain=default); [KADOKAWA cover-use rules](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/support/b/library/); [Pyo Eunji official terms](https://pyoeunji.com/pages/terms)). The new NLK metadata record contains no media, and no image was copied or uploaded to R2 this turn.

## Conclusion

The source-cleared NLK record shows that an open bibliographic route can add useful Korean photobook metadata without importing a retailer’s image. The remaining creator-led candidates need separately verified identity and an authorized metadata source; each future R2 asset needs permission tied to the exact image and expressly covering storage and public delivery.
