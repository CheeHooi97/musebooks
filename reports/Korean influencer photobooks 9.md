# Add Two More Verified Korean Photobooks

This pass added two verified Korean creator photobooks to MuseBooks: **Yebin’s Picnic Photobook**, a paid digital web-pictorial bundle, and **아쿠아 AQUA**, a physical photobook featuring model and online creator Shin Jae-eun (신재은, Zenny). Yebin’s single checkout combines two 70-photo sets, while Aqua has matching bibliographic details from two booksellers and Zenny’s own photo-book list. Each addition is recorded as **one work, one edition, and one featured credit**; the scoped audits found no integrity failures or missing creator provenance. The public catalog verifier reported **361 physical works and 5,092 digital works** after import. Neither title has documented permission to reuse its cover or interior images, so both records have **zero media** and nothing was uploaded to R2. Other leads remain on hold where the evidence points to illustrated content or lacks an exact edition record.

## Yebin’s single listing supports one bundled digital edition

The Gumroad listing sells **Yebin’s Picnic Photobook** as one digital product containing “Picnic With Love” (70 photos and one video) and a Nezuko-themed set (70 photos and one making video). The two sets therefore belong to one work and edition in this record; the photo counts describe images, not pages. The listing gives no release date, publisher, ISBN, or page count. ([Gumroad product page](https://yeb00.gumroad.com/l/Yebin_Picnic))

The seller identity is supported by a matching creator trail: the official Yeniverse link page connects yeb00__ to a YouTube channel and shop, while Feednews links the same Instagram handle to Jeon Yebin (전예빈), an online-shop owner and model. HK01 also identifies Yebin as a Korean influencer/model and links that handle. These sources support a featured-creator credit, but the listing does not establish that she photographed or authored the sets. ([Yeniverse official Linktree](https://linktr.ee/yeb00__), [Feednews profile](https://feednews.co.kr/1409), [HK01 profile](https://www.hk01.com/熱爆話題/629326/韓國網紅超有成熟女人味-開網店自己做model-性感造型曬好身材))

The catalog record preserves the product facts and leaves unsupported bibliographic fields empty. No asset-specific reuse license was found; the purchase page is not permission to host the paid photographs or video in R2. ([Yebin import manifest](../docs/korean-yebin-picnic-photobook-import.json), [final database read-back](../docs/korean-yebin-picnic-photobook-db-readback-final.json), [scoped audit](../docs/korean-yebin-picnic-photobook-db-verification.json))

## Aqua’s print edition matches across two retailers

The physical edition **아쿠아 AQUA** is credited to the ROTTA × ZENNY collaboration and features 신재은, also known as Jae-Eun Shin or Zenny. YES24 and Aladin agree on **ISBN 9791188073146**, publisher **브라보 (bravo)**, release date **2018-01-26**, **168 pages**, and dimensions **183 × 257 mm**. Retailer weight values conflict, so weight was omitted. Zenny’s official About page identifies her by name and alias, identifies her as a model, and lists Aqua among her photo books. ([YES24 edition record](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/57954502), [Aladin edition record](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?itemid=129267562), [Zenny official About page](https://zenn.ee/en/about/))

The bookseller records establish the edition, and the first-party profile confirms the creator connection. They do not grant a license to reproduce the book’s cover or interior. Accordingly, the database has bibliographic metadata and one featured credit, but no copied media. ([Aqua import manifest](../docs/korean-zenny-aqua-photobook-import.json), [database read-back](../docs/korean-zenny-aqua-photobook-db-readback.json), [scoped audit](../docs/korean-zenny-aqua-photobook-db-verification.json))

## Read-backs confirm both records and leave R2 empty

The read-backs show one work, one edition, one featured credit, no listings, and no media for each title. Both audits report **zero integrity failures** and **zero people without verification sources**. They also record coverage gaps—such as no captured listings or cover images—rather than treating missing data as verified metadata. The public catalog verifier completed with totals of **361 physical** and **5,092 digital** works. ([Yebin read-back and audit](../docs/korean-yebin-picnic-photobook-db-readback-final.json), [Aqua read-back and audit](../docs/korean-zenny-aqua-photobook-db-readback.json))

## Three adjacent leads do not yet qualify

Tumblbug’s completed ISEGYE IDOL project included a special photobook reward, but the available product reporting describes the book as illustrations of webtoon characters rather than photographs of the creators. It stays out of a photographic photobook set unless catalog scope explicitly includes illustrated avatar books. ([Tumblbug project](https://tumblbug.com/beyondthedimension_isedol), [News1 campaign coverage](https://www.news1.kr/amp/it-science/general-it/5612728), [I Love Character product report](https://www.ilovecharacter.com/news/newsview.php?ncode=1065597047851664))

Zenny’s official list also names **Zenny in Philippines** and **Oasis**, but the research found no exact publisher or bookseller edition for either. The former has a secondary announcement of a limited edition, which is a promising lead, but it still lacks a verified edition identifier. These titles need stronger bibliographic evidence before import. ([Zenny official About page](https://zenn.ee/en/about/), [secondary Zenny in Philippines announcement](https://moviediary.tistory.com/1925))

## Conclusion

The two additions show that an influencer-focused catalog can capture both informal creator-store releases and conventional print editions when the product and featured-person evidence are clear. Rights remain a separate gate: complete metadata does not authorize copying a commercial cover or paid content to R2. The next useful work is to identify exact retail or publisher records for creator-listed titles and obtain asset-specific reuse permission where R2 hosting is needed.
