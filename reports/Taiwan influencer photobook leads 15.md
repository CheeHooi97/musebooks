## Conclusion

Round 15 shows that storefront coverage and catalog coverage can diverge: 彭彭’s ebook is already stored in MuseBooks even though the local Markdown roster still lists only print. Future sweeps should compare each new retailer hit against both the live API and the working catalog, then reserve imports for products with a substantiated edition and cover trail. That separates true additions from data-maintenance opportunities without losing credible preorders that need another look.
