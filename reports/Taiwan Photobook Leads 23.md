# Round 23 Adds Verified Taiwan Photobook

Round 23 added one new Taiwan photobook to MuseBooks: 晏人物’s physical group anthology 《Loop：晏人物攝影集》, which features Taiwan Instagram creator 黃景. The campaign specifies a 128-page, 18 × 25.6 cm book, and its December 2024 shipping update shows the finished, title-bearing copy after a cover-jacket reprint. The catalog now has work `work-tw-9e5c7c9e51ab01a6e2fcdfba` and physical edition `edition-86f943f3a51fe9bd8866e257`; R2 and the live API cover both passed readback checks. The cover stored in R2 comes from an official shipping product photograph, not a standalone flat scan, and the work is a photographer-led group book rather than 黃景’s personal photobook. Other new leads remain duplicates or holds pending product, identity, fulfillment, or cover evidence.

## Official shipment evidence confirms the finished book

Zeczec’s campaign describes 《Loop：晏人物攝影集》 as a limited physical photobook add-on, separate from the project calendar, with 128 pages, lay-flat binding, and dimensions of 18 × 25.6 cm. It names 黃景 among the seven featured models. The project’s December 13, 2024 update says the shipment had begun and explains that the original jacket was reprinted to correct color and finishing problems; its photograph shows the finished book with “Loop” visible on the physical cover. ([Official campaign and product details](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/loop-2005), [shipping and jacket update](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/loop-2005/updates/48979), [official product photograph](https://assets.zeczec.com/asset_913856_image_big.jpg?1733981411=))

Taiwan People News identifies 黃景 as a Kaohsiung Instagram creator and reported nearly 100,000 Instagram followers at the time of publication. Together with the campaign’s model list, that connects a Taiwan influencer to this specific anthology. This does not establish the origin of every other model in the seven-person group, so the import records 黃景 as the verified featured creator rather than treating the whole roster as Taiwan-origin. ([Taiwan People News profile of 黃景](https://www.peponews.tw/2020/01/07/66692), [official campaign model list](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/loop-2005))

The cover source is an official product-set photograph. The original Zeczec image downloaded at 572,658 bytes; the R2 asset is a 228,714-byte WebP based on the visible finished copy. Its angled perspective means it should be understood as a shipping product photo used for the catalog thumbnail, not a flat standalone cover scan. The original image and shipping update remain attached as provenance in the import manifest. ([Official update](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/loop-2005/updates/48979), [original product photograph](https://assets.zeczec.com/asset_913856_image_big.jpg?1733981411=))

## R2 and the live catalog confirm the import

The import manifest validated one record and the apply operation reported that catalog records and price observations were committed. R2 returned HTTP 200 for both PUT and HEAD; an authenticated GET returned 228,714 bytes with SHA-256 `cc48f54048b1f065652c6d15355535dec805815c4f66edd4a8dc1bbf1f1dbc61`. A live title query returned exactly one work, `work-tw-9e5c7c9e51ab01a6e2fcdfba`, with physical edition `edition-86f943f3a51fe9bd8866e257`, 128 pages, and release date December 13, 2024. The API’s nested origin record reports Taiwan (`work.origin.code: TW`); a signed cover readback returned HTTP 200 and the same byte count and hash as the R2 object. ([Live MuseBooks title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=Loop%EF%BC%9A%E6%99%8F%E4%BA%BA%E7%89%A9%E6%94%9D%E5%BD%B1%E9%9B%86&pageSize=50))

The recorded proof is available in the [import manifest](../docs/taiwan-loop-yan-photobook-import.json), [database readback](../docs/taiwan-loop-yan-photobook-db-readback.json), and [database/R2 verification results](../docs/taiwan-loop-yan-photobook-db-verification.json). The work is now present in the catalog and its cover is retrievable from the signed API URL; this verifies the requested database and R2 write for this title.

## Conclusion

The import adds a shared photobook featuring a verified Taiwan influencer without mislabeling it as a personal book or presenting a product photograph as flat cover artwork. The strongest unresolved physical lead is Q尼’s booklet, while the next digital recheck should wait for *FOUR*’s finished PDF and cover; the next import should follow only after those exact editions can be matched to reliable creator and product evidence.
