# Round 24 Flags Holds and Cover Risks

Round 24 made no catalog, database, or R2 writes: the new leads either lacked final-edition evidence or already existed in MuseBooks. The strongest fresh physical lead is 阿布吳玳慈’s 《吳玳慈．再jian》, which had a successful crowdfunding campaign and a planned physical and digital release, but still lacks a final cover and fulfillment confirmation. The new digital lead, *PASSION：人態好攝影集4（數位特別版）*, is a verifiable ebook, yet its featured subjects’ Taiwan-origin influencer identities remain unclear and its seller image is an age-restriction placeholder. The physical storefront checks mostly matched existing works. They also exposed a cover mismatch for 瑟七’s already cataloged 《微澀・微瑟》: the live work cover is digital-specific, while the physical edition has a different design. Round 23’s verified addition, 晏人物’s group book 《Loop：晏人物攝影集》 featuring Taiwan creator 黃景, remains the latest successful R2/database import.

## 《再jian》 remains a campaign lead, not a verified delivery

Zeczec identifies 《吳玳慈．再jian》寫真書 as 阿布吳玳慈’s first personal fashion photobook and explicitly offers physical and digital versions. Its campaign closed successfully on April 30, 2023, raising NT$202,200 from 122 backers; the one-book reward was backed 58 times, and the page promised signed-book dispatch from May 20 onward. Those details establish that the title was sold and that shipment was planned, but the campaign page and focused follow-up still do not confirm that a finished copy reached supporters or specify the digital file format and delivery. ([Official Zeczec campaign and reward](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/aboo-tzaijianfashionshow))

The creator fit is plausible: Chinese Culture University’s profile describes 吳玳慈’s modeling-to-acting path and attention from viral TikTok/runway clips, while Shih Hsin University and TVBS document her public career in Taiwan. None of those sources explicitly establishes her birthplace or nationality, so the report does not treat Taiwan origin as a proven biographical fact. More decisively, the only located campaign image is a 600×200 promotional reward banner with a portrait, not a standalone final cover or photograph of the printed book. The live title query also returned no MuseBooks match. Keep **HOLD** until a title-matched final cover and credible fulfillment evidence are found; confirm the digital file and metadata if that edition is to be cataloged. ([Chinese Culture University profile](https://joumedia.pccu.edu.tw/article.php?id=298), [Shih Hsin University profile](https://www.shu.edu.tw/Spotlight.aspx?from=06&sID=30178), [Shih Hsin program notice](https://vci.wp.shu.edu.tw/?page_id=280), [TVBS profile](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/life/85405), [campaign reward banner](https://assets.zeczec.com/projects/14534/options/99881_banner.jpg?1679421950=), [MuseBooks title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E5%90%B3%E7%8E%B3%E6%85%88%EF%BC%8E%E5%86%8Djian&pageSize=50))

Readmoo lists *PASSION：人態好攝影集4（數位特別版）* as a fixed-layout EPUB released June 6, 2025, at NT$490, with ISBN 9786264157292 and EISBN 9786264158145. Google Play Books independently describes a 162-page Taiwan Kadokawa ebook and says its layout differs substantially from print. The product names the subjects only as Bin (`@binbincoach`) and Ray (`@ray._.tuntun`), described as fitness coaches; the sources reviewed do not firmly connect both handles to named people who are Taiwan-origin influencers. ([Readmoo ebook listing](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=137751), [Google Play Books product](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=9P5fEQAAQBAJ))

The National Central Library record confirms a Taipei paperback edition but does not resolve the subjects’ identities or the ebook cover. Readmoo’s direct image returned an 18+ warning graphic, not an inspectable final cover, and live MuseBooks searches for the ebook title and EISBN returned no work. A Taiwan publisher, photographer, or seller alone does not establish that the featured models meet the requested creator-origin scope. Keep **HOLD** until primary evidence links both identities and origin, and a final digital cover is accessible. No correction was made: a future update should first obtain a clean, final publisher physical-cover asset and verify how MuseBooks associates covers with editions. ## Round 23’s 《Loop》 remains the latest verified addition

The most recent successful import remains 晏人物’s physical group anthology 《Loop：晏人物攝影集》, featuring Taiwan Instagram creator 黃景. Zeczec specifies 128 pages and 18 × 25.6 cm; its December 2024 shipment update documents the finished, title-bearing copy after a jacket reprint. The catalog work is `work-tw-9e5c7c9e51ab01a6e2fcdfba` and its physical edition is `edition-86f943f3a51fe9bd8866e257`. Round 23 recorded successful database import and R2 PUT/HEAD/GET plus signed API cover readback, with the same SHA-256 for the stored image. As the earlier report explains, the cover is an official shipping product photograph showing the angled book, not a flat standalone scan; only 黃景’s Taiwan-origin influencer evidence was checked and attached to this group title. ([Official Zeczec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/loop-2005), [finished-book shipping update](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/loop-2005/updates/48979), [Taiwan creator profile](https://www.peponews.tw/2020/01/07/66692), [MuseBooks live title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=Loop%EF%BC%9A%E6%99%8F%E4%BA%BA%E7%89%A9%E6%94%9D%E5%BD%B1%E9%9B%86&pageSize=50), [Round 23 verification report](Taiwan%20Photobook%20Leads%2023.md))

## Conclusion

Round 24 sharpened the catalog’s review queue rather than expanding it. The next import should wait for title-matched final artwork and fulfillment evidence for 《再jian》, or verified subject identities/origin and a real ebook cover for *PASSION*. Separately, 瑟七’s mismatch deserves an edition-aware cover review based on clean publisher artwork; it is a quality correction to an existing record, not a new-book import. Until those specific evidence gaps close, leaving R2 and the database unchanged avoids treating campaign promises, placeholder images, or duplicate listings as completed editions.
