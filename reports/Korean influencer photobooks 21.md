# Track New Korean Creator Photobook Leads

This round found two plausible Korean creator photobook leads—a 2024 physical group book from the SOOP Streamer Awards and a reported 2020 digital photobook by streamer Ayul—but neither cleared the evidence needed for a database import. The SOOP book is described as a roughly 100-page hardcover with 65 creator participants, yet no exact open-licensed bibliographic record or commercial metadata-use grant was identified. Ayul’s photobook is reported in a 2020 article and a creator-facing Linktree, but her nationality, exact edition identity, and authorized metadata source remain unverified. Five read-only SOOP catalog title searches and an Ayul name/title duplicate query returned zero matches; those results do not establish catalog absence beyond the terms checked. Magarin’s *Secret B* No. 4 is already in the catalog. No import, database write, image download, R2 upload, or external contact occurred this round, and no exact cover image was cleared for R2 storage and public display.

## SOOP confirms a group book, not reusable catalog metadata

SOOP’s ESG page says proceeds from the 2024 Streamer Awards photobook sale were donated, while Kuki News reports that the limited SOOPTORE release went on sale in January 2025 and involved 65 creators, including Gamst, Isegye Idol, Woowakgood, and UjeongIng. The same report describes a hard-cover photo album of about 100 pages with portraits, behind-the-scenes and event images, and a cover buyers could select from participating streamers’ photographs. SOOP’s event program and a Newsis profile support the event and at least one named Korean streamer’s creator identity, but the reviewed sources do not enumerate all 65 participants or establish that every participant is Korean ([SOOP ESG 2024](https://corp.sooplive.com/esg/2024/index.php?page=sharing); [Kuki News, 12 March 2025](https://m.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503120038); [SOOP event program](https://2024award.sooplive.co.kr/?page=program); [Newsis profile of Gamst](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20241105_0002946977)).

The live read-only catalog searches used `SOOP`, `2024 SOOP Streamer Awards`, `Streamer Awards Photobook`, and two Korean title variants; all five returned `total=0` for `publicationType=photobook`. This establishes that those queries found no match, not that no matching record exists under another title or alias. Searches of NLK/LOD discovery surfaces did not identify an exact open-licensed record, and no source granting commercial reuse of the metadata was found. The SOOP and press pages therefore remain discovery evidence rather than an authorized metadata source for import; the project register requires a source with reuse terms compatible with the catalog’s intended use ([National Library of Korea LOD](https://lod.nl.go.kr/); [project source-policy register](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)). No ISBN, stable product ID, full participant list, imprint, or complete bibliographic record was verified.

## Ayul’s digital photobook lacks an exact edition link

A 2020 report describes “BJ Ayul” as a popular PandaTV streamer and says her debut was published as a digital photobook. Ayul’s public Linktree labels a release “Ayul 1st Sexy photo album” and links to Puremedia, while Puremedia’s current product page separately lists “Ayul / 아율 Story e-book / PURE MEDIA Vol. 51” and a Vol. 28 item. The sources do not connect either current volume to the reported 2020 debut, and the report and product-facing account do not establish Ayul’s nationality or provide a complete edition record such as a stable product identifier, ISBN, or page count ([NewsCulture report reproduced by Ppomppu](https://m.ppomppu.co.kr/new/bbs_view.php?id=news_broadcast&no=128244); [Ayul Linktree](https://linktr.ee/store_ayul); [Puremedia product listing](https://puremedia.co.kr/mall/m_mall_list.php?ps_brand=&ps_company=&ps_ctid=01010000&ps_line=high&ps_max_money=&ps_min_money=&ps_page=1&ps_search=)).

The read-only duplicate query covering Ayul/아율, “Ayul 1st,” and Puremedia Vol. 51 title fields returned `Read-only candidate duplicate rows: 0`. That is a narrow result for the queried name and title variants, not proof that all aliases or editions are absent. No exact commercial-reuse-licensed metadata record or written metadata grant was located, so Ayul remains a lead rather than an import candidate. A separate Puremedia product detail page restricts reproduction and republication of its web-pictorial images, but it is not an Ayul-specific license statement; no exact Ayul cover or preview image had permission documented for R2 storage and public delivery ([Puremedia e-book detail page](https://puremedia.co.kr/mall/m_mall_detail.php?ps_ctid=01000000&ps_goid=174&ps_page=2&ps_page_=&ps_page_=4); [project source-policy register](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)).

## Magarin remains a duplicate, and the broader leads stay held

Magarin’s physical *Secret B* No. 4 already exists in MuseBooks as work `korea-magarin-secret-b-4-2024` and edition `korea-magarin-secret-b-4-2024-print-spomax`; a fresh repeatable-read, read-only verification recorded one work, one edition, one linked listing, and no media reference. The official MAXQ/Spomax product page and a Sports Seoul profile support the product and influencer-award lead, but the bookseller listing’s serial-style code is not a verified ISBN. No exact NLK record was identified; the prior NLK endpoint searches were inconclusive, with a narrower title-term query timing out after 25 seconds. The existing duplicate and unresolved open-metadata source make a second record inappropriate ([existing Magarin manifest](../docs/korean-photobook-magarin-secret-b-4-import.json); [read-only database verification](../docs/korean-photobook-magarin-secret-b-4-db-verification.json); [Spomax product page](https://spomax.kr/product/%EC%8B%9C%ED%81%AC%EB%A6%BF-b-4%ED%98%B8-%EB%A8%B8%EC%8A%AC%ED%80%B8-%EB%A7%88%EA%B0%80%EB%A6%B0-%ED%99%94%EB%B3%B4%EC%A7%91/5221/); [Sports Seoul profile via Nate](https://news.nate.com/view/20251208n02046); [NLK LOD service](https://lod.nl.go.kr/sparql)).

The previous round’s other digital leads also remain unresolved: MAXQ described *Secret B* No. 1 as digital-first, but the existing catalog contains its physical edition and no separately identified digital edition; STOVE lists a downloadable cosplay photobook, but its credited cosplayer’s Korean identity and influencer status were not established. These are not new imports, and their public product pages do not themselves grant commercial metadata reuse ([round 20 report](Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2020.md); [STOVE product page](https://store.onstove.com/ko/games/100488); [MAXQ announcement](https://maxq.kr/articles/sikeurit-b-ryusebibakeunhye-hwabojip-yeyakpanmae-opeun)).

## R2 still needs permission for each exact image

No exact cover or preview image tied to the SOOP or Ayul candidates was found with a rights-holder grant covering both storage in R2 and public delivery from MuseBooks. SOOP’s customizable cover makes the exact variant especially important, and the reviewed sources do not identify the rights holders for all portraits and cover images. Puremedia’s listing and its restrictive general product page do not grant reuse of an Ayul asset. The project register treats source URLs and provenance as distinct from permission to store and serve an image ([Kuki News](https://m.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503120038); [Puremedia product listing](https://puremedia.co.kr/mall/m_mall_list.php?ps_brand=&ps_company=&ps_ctid=01010000&ps_line=high&ps_max_money=&ps_min_money=&ps_page=1&ps_search=); [project source-policy register](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)).

As of the prior read-only scan, 10 published Korean photobook editions had cover references and zero R2 object keys; the broader earlier audit covered 153 editions, including eight influencer-focused covers without documented exact-image permission for public R2 delivery. No R2 writes occurred in this round, so those earlier findings were not changed by this research pass ([round 20 image-rights audit](../research_notes/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2020/image_rights.md); [round 19 R2 audit](../research_notes/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2019/r2_rights.md)).

## Conclusion

SOOP is the strongest newly verified product lead because official and press sources establish a real physical creator-group book, while Ayul is a plausible digital lead whose reported title is not yet tied to the current publisher listings. Neither reaches the import bar: exact bibliographic identity and an authorized metadata source are still needed, followed by a duplicate check. Image permissions remain a separate requirement; no cover should be staged in R2 until a license identifies the exact asset and permits storage and public display.
