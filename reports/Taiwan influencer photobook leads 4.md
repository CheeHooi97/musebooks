# Taiwan Photobooks Gain Verified New Editions

This round added **two Taiwan creator photobooks as three format-specific editions** in MuseBooks, with cover files saved to R2: 廖心恩’s existing 《心存感恩：心恩做最好的自己真愛寫真》 work now includes a verified paperback, and 黃氏兄弟’s 《有你的夏天：黃氏兄弟寫真書II》 now has both digital and paperback editions. The Huang Brothers’ digital EISBN **9789865249809** and print ISBN **9789865249793** remain distinct, while the 廖心恩 record preserves a shared identifier across its Pubu digital and library-confirmed paper formats and flags the ambiguity. Live work-record reads show the saved editions, and signed public cover downloads matched the uploaded R2 hashes. The catalog verifier passed with **347 physical works and 5,067 digital works** in the global catalog. 《韓你菲》 remains a credible release announcement, but its direct seller page and final cover are still unverified, so it was not imported.

## Conclusion

The round’s strongest gains came from checking formats at the edition level: it uncovered a physical book hidden behind an existing digital work, and paired a verified EPUB with its separately identified print edition. Import dry runs and applies validated the catalog records, and the public catalog verifier reported **347 physical works and 5,067 digital works**; these are system-wide totals, not counts of Taiwan additions. The discovery effort remains open because the Taiwan creator market includes retailer listings and social preorders that are difficult to resolve into stable product records, with 《韓你菲》 the clearest remaining lead.
