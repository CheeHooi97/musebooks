## Conclusion

Round 10 contributes one new physical work and one metadata repair. The Huang Brothers record has a strong ISBN-to-cover match across publisher, library, creator, and item-photo evidence, with the ended resale listing as its main limitation. The unresolved holds show the practical threshold for future catalog work: a campaign announcement or format-conflicted ebook listing is not enough without a final item record and a confirmed cover. This targeted pass is not an exhaustive census of Taiwan creator photobooks.
