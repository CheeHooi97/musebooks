# Two JTWINS Ebooks Join Taiwan Catalog

The clearest import-ready discovery in this update is a pair of JTWINS digital photobooks. **Both are now saved in MuseBooks as separate works and editions, with their covers in R2**; live readbacks confirmed two works, two editions, and featured credits for 夏恩 and 夏得 on each. Taiwan's National Central Library records distinct EISBNs for the two Taiwan KADOKAWA EPUBs, and an interview supports the creators' Taiwan connection. BROMANCE's precise October 2023 release day remains disputed, so its database date retains month precision. 李恩菲's 《韓你菲》 is a credible new physical-book lead but still lacks a verifiable product page and cover asset; several male-model and drama-book leads also remain outside the import set because the available sources do not establish Taiwan eligibility or an individual influencer subject. This is an incremental catalog update, not an exhaustive inventory.

## Separate EISBNs establish two JTWINS photobooks

The National Central Library's Taiwan ISBN deposit lists two October 2023 EPUB photobooks by JTWINS: 《JTWINS夏恩夏得數位寫真（1）BROTHER》, EISBN **9786263780866**, and 《JTWINS夏恩夏得數位寫真（2）BROMANCE》, EISBN **9786263780873**. The distinct subtitles and identifiers match separate retailer records, so the pair belongs in two work and edition records rather than one combined title. The Pubu offer is a bundle containing these same two volumes, not a third photobook. ([National Central Library](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1697771178.pdf); [Pubu bundle](https://www.pubu.com.tw/combo/668074))

Readmoo identifies BROTHER as a fixed-layout EPUB published by Taiwan KADOKAWA on **2023-10-20**; its page does not establish a page count. Eslite identifies BROMANCE as a **133-page** fixed-layout EPUB from Taiwan KADOKAWA. The creators' Taiwan connection is supported by a ManGu interview in which 夏恩 says he is from Taiwan and identifies 夏得 as his twin; Mirror Media also reports their entry into Taiwan's entertainment industry. ([Readmoo BROTHER](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=135375); [Eslite BROMANCE](https://www.eslite.com/product/10072302132683100269003); [ManGu interview](https://www.atmangu.com/uploads/article/zwh4MAjsWCuweHmlCIIBdprehgeGuFYC.pdf); [Mirror Media profile](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20181012ent004))

## Covers and records are verified; BROMANCE stays month-dated

The Readmoo BROTHER cover and PChome/Pubu BROMANCE cover were visually matched to their respective titles, uploaded to R2, and read back with authenticated SHA-256 matches. The live database readbacks show **two distinct published photobook works, two digital editions, and both creators credited on each**. They show no linked retailer listings or price observations, so this import records the bibliographic editions and covers but does not assert a current offer or price. ([Readmoo BROTHER and cover](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=135375); [PChome/Pubu BROMANCE and cover](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBS65-D900HY8M4/))

BROTHER's exact release day is corroborated by Readmoo. For BROMANCE, the National Central Library establishes October 2023, but retailer pages conflict: Kingstone says October 20, Eslite October 19, and PChome/HyRead October 1. The imported edition therefore keeps **October 2023 at month precision** rather than choosing one seller's date. Eslite supplies the page count and the National Central Library supplies the EISBN; the retailer disagreement affects only the day. ([National Central Library](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1697771178.pdf); [Kingstone BROMANCE](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2800000201668/); [Eslite BROMANCE](https://www.eslite.com/product/10072302132683100269003); [PChome/HyRead BROMANCE](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBR5E-D900IOXBZ))

## 《韓你菲》 is confirmed but not catalog-ready

民視 and UDN identify 《韓你菲》 as 菲菲／李恩菲's first solo physical photobook, photographed in Busan, and report a planned sales launch at 20:00 on **2026-09-02**. A later report repeats that it was released that day, while an indexed snapshot of her Instagram bio mentions a preorder link. None of these sources yields a retrievable seller destination, publisher record, ISBN, page count, dimensions, price, or availability. The promotional photo credits identify 杰斯汀整合行銷 as the image provider; they do not establish that the image is the book jacket. Accordingly, 《韓你菲》 remains a confirmed existence lead rather than an imported edition, and its reported launch date is not treated as verified fulfillment. ([民視 report](https://finance.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260); [UDN report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294); [GamerSky follow-up](https://www.gamersky.com/news/202609/2201785.shtml); [Kolr snapshot of @aviva_1028](https://www.kolr.ai/influencer/a7c5f083-7e76-46ad-8b53-e6ca47e18ea9))

The Taiwan eligibility signal is strong: the Rakuten Girls profile identifies 菲菲／Aviva 李恩菲, and Taiwan coverage describes her cheerleading work. The repository already has her earlier solo digital releases and the group book 《bestie閨蜜》, but no 《韓你菲》 entry. The missing evidence is product-level, not a lack of a plausible Taiwan connection. ([Rakuten Girls profile](https://cpblgirls.tw/front/4/RakutenGirlsmen/member.php?ID=Y3BibGdpcmxzJlJha3V0ZW5HaXJsc21lbg%3D%3D&PID=86); [民視 cheerleader profile](https://www.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026424W0371))

## Pubu discovery still needs creator-level origin checks

Pubu availability by itself does not establish that a photobook subject is a Taiwan creator. 彭果 remains unresolved because the cited Hunan University page concerns a possibly same-name student selected for China's national bodybuilding team, and no source connects that student conclusively to the Pubu model. Devil-Lee's promoter posts and Logan奶狼宇's Xiaohongshu-related reference do not establish either person's Taiwan origin or current Taiwan base. 夏勝傑 and Gabriel remain excluded on the previously reported Hangzhou and Shanghai connections, respectively; the evidence reviewed did not establish a qualifying Taiwan affiliation. ([Hunan University record](https://tyxy.hncu.edu.cn/info/2017/6437.htm); [Devil-Lee promoter timeline](https://www.sina.cn/media/1212848862); [Logan reference](https://www.ktyrw.com/jirou/2025/0420/2658.html); [Pubu male-photo category](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook-category/male-photo-books-402))

The Pubu drama titles checked are ensemble publications rather than books centered on an individual Taiwan influencer. For example, 《向流星許願的我們 戲劇寫真書》 and 《靈魂約定 戲劇寫真書》 describe cast or drama imagery, which does not establish a specific influencer as the book's subject. This was a sample of a changing category, not an exhaustive review of every drama photobook. Another real digital listing, 李昊禹's 《個人寫真書昇肌勃勃》, has product metadata, but the available search did not resolve the subject's identity or establish a Taiwan origin/current base; that title remains excluded until a subject-specific source is found. ([Pubu 《向流星許願的我們》](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/673736); [Pubu 《靈魂約定》](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/605898); [Pubu 李昊禹 listing](https://www.pubu.com.tw/magazine/686431))

## Conclusion

This update shows two separate checks are needed before a catalog addition: bibliographic certainty and creator eligibility. JTWINS cleared both and is now represented by two R2-backed database records; 《韓你菲》 has strong evidence of existence and Taiwan relevance but still lacks the seller and cover trail needed for a reliable edition. The most productive next step is to recover the creator's actual preorder destination and continue resolving male-model identities from direct profiles, while checking drama titles only when an individual Taiwan influencer is explicitly the subject.
