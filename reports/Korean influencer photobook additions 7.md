# Korean creator photobook addition 7

## Added to the catalog

The successful Tumblbug campaign **드랙퀸 아네싸와 허리케인 김치의 10주년 포토북 & 포스터** explicitly planned a commemorative photobook and poster for Anessa and Hurricane Kimchi. It raised KRW 2,222,000 from 60 backers and reached 111% of its goal. Heezy Yang's official bio identifies them as a Seoul-born Korean artist and activist, active as drag queen Hurricane Kimchi, who performs, writes and releases original songs, and produces music videos. This supports cataloging a Korean creator-led photobook; the sources do not label either person a social-media influencer. ([Tumblbug campaign](https://tumblbug.com/drag10), [official artist bio](https://heezyyang.wixsite.com/heezyyang), [official music and social links](https://heezyyang.wixsite.com/heezyyang/music))

The campaign says the book and poster imagery were ready for production, but the reviewed page does not verify actual shipment. The edition is therefore recorded as physical, with no publication date, page count, ISBN, or publisher imprint inferred. The campaign dates are not used as a release date. The exact campaign cover was not copied to R2 because reuse permission for that image is undocumented.
