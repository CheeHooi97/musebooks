# Verify Ran’s Book Before Cataloging It

Ran’s 2023 physical photobook is now tied to an exact National Diet Library (NDL) bibliographic record, but this round still does **not** clear it for MuseBooks import or R2. The NDL record identifies ISBN `978-4-04-737-435-5`, while its own pagination (109 pages) conflicts with KADOKAWA’s 112-page listing. NDL’s public license applies to NDL-created information and excludes third-party bibliographic metadata; the record attributes its descriptive summary to JPRO, and the provenance of every other field is not stated. KADOKAWA’s cover exception is narrowly for introducing books and directs commercial or internet use to an inquiry, which is not approval. A scoped read-only duplicate query returned no rows, but that result does not prove there is no record under other variants. No database write, contact, image download, or R2 operation occurred; this closes the current research round at the user’s request.

## NDL confirms the edition but conflicts on pagination

The NDL Search record identifies *蘭 : らんファースト写真集* as a 2023.3 KADOKAWA paper book, ISBN `978-4-04-737-435-5`, 109 pages and 26 cm, with photographer 山内洋枝 and subject らん ラン. Its bibliographic ID is `032712353`. KADOKAWA’s own product page gives the same ISBN and a 2023-03-29 release date, but describes a B5 book of 112 pages. These are source-specific values; the round did not establish which page count should be treated as authoritative. ([NDL Search record](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I032712353); [KADOKAWA product record](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/322301000031))

The creator and book match is supported by the prior round’s independent profile and release sources: Pop’n’Roll described Ran as Korea-born and an influencer/talent in its 2023 announcement, and ORICON identified her as a Korean model, TikToker, and influencer later that year. Those sources establish historical descriptions, not current follower totals. ([Pop’n’Roll, 29 March 2023](https://popnroll.tv/articles/31921); [ORICON, 18 December 2023](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2306799/full/))

## NDL’s license does not cover every displayed field

NDL’s terms place eligible NDL-created information under Japan’s Public Data License 1.0, but explicitly exclude third-party-created bibliographic metadata from that general grant. The record itself attributes its descriptive summary to the Japan Publication Registry Office (JPRO); it does not provide field-by-field provenance for the remaining title and edition data. PDL 1.0 permits commercial use only for content actually covered by the license, with attribution and subject to exclusions. Accordingly, the NDL page is useful bibliographic evidence, but this review could not establish that every field needed for a commercial MuseBooks record is licensed. ([NDL Terms of Use](https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/sitepolicy/terms); [Digital Agency, PDL 1.0](https://www.digital.go.jp/en/resources/open_data/public_data_license_v1.0); [NDL Search record](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I032712353))

JPO Books’ terms separately limit use of its provided information to noncommercial purposes and restrict reproduction, repurposing, and sale without rightsholder permission. Those terms do not automatically govern every NDL field, but they do not supply a commercial reuse route for the JPRO-attributed summary. KADOKAWA’s product page and works-use policy likewise did not state an explicit commercial license for the factual metadata reviewed here. This is a project-policy hold for lack of a documented grant, not a legal conclusion about the copyright status of bare facts. ([JPO Books terms](https://www.books.or.jp/books-terms); [KADOKAWA works-use policy](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/copyrightpolicy/); [project source-use register](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md))

## The cover policy and nearby lead do not clear

NDL labels the displayed cover as a book image and notes that covers can differ by holding library; NDL’s terms identify JPRO-provided book images as third-party content. KADOKAWA’s no-application cover-use exception is limited to introducing its books under stated conditions. It directs commercial uses and internet distribution to its corporate/media inquiry route. That route is an application channel, not permission for MuseBooks to store, transform, and publicly serve this exact cover from R2. No exact-image authorization was found, and no image was downloaded or uploaded. ([NDL Search record](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I032712353); [NDL Terms of Use](https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/sitepolicy/terms); [KADOKAWA cover-use policy](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/support/b/media/))

The other near miss in this round was *CLOE Magazine Vol. 2: Hye Rin’s First Photobook*, listed by Apple Books as an 87-page English e-book released in 2021. The accessible listing’s description calls Hye Rin a Korean model, but the direct product page could not be inspected and the identity or influencer status was not independently established. Apple’s terms restrict service and content use to personal, noncommercial purposes and restrict scraping or copying; no separate metadata or image license was found. This remains a lead rather than an import candidate. ([Apple Books listing](https://books.apple.com/gb/book/cloe-magazine-vol-2-hye-rins-first-photobook/id1549141595); [Apple Media Services Terms, §F](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/))

Earlier SOOP and Ayul leads also remain unresolved: the SOOP group book lacks an exact bibliographic record and complete participant credits, while the reported Ayul digital debut could not be mapped to a current Puremedia edition. Their prior report documents the evidence and limitations; neither was imported in this round. ([SOOP ESG 2024](https://corp.sooplive.com/esg/2024/index.php?page=sharing); [Kuki News, 12 March 2025](https://m.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503120038); [2020 Ayul photobook report](https://m.ppomppu.co.kr/new/bbs_view.php?id=news_broadcast&no=128244); [Puremedia Ayul category](https://puremedia.kr/mall/m_mall_list.php?ps_ctid=01011300))

## The duplicate check stayed narrow and read-only

A PostgreSQL transaction ran with `READ ONLY` and repeatable-read isolation, checking the ISBN `9784047374355`, Japanese title variants, the English title “Ran First Photobook,” and a matching slug. Its output showed `Read-only exact-title/ISBN candidate rows:` followed by `null`, because the temporary Go check serialized a nil result slice as JSON `null`; the query returned no rows for the searched terms. This supports only a scoped duplicate result, not an absolute claim that no matching work exists under another name or identifier. No database write, external contact, media staging, cover download, or R2 copy occurred. ([NDL Search record](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I032712353); [project source-use register](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md))

The research goal is not fully achieved: Ran’s edition is identified, but the current evidence does not clear the project’s commercial metadata source gate or exact-cover rights gate. Since the user asked to finish this round and stop, this report closes the current pass without further discovery or follow-up. No catalog or R2 changes were made.
