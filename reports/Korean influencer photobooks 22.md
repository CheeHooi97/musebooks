# Track New Korean Creator Photobook Leads

Round 22 surfaced a strong new match: Korean influencer and model Ran’s 2023 physical photobook, *蘭 らんファースト写真集* (ISBN 9784047374355). KADOKAWA supplies exact edition facts, while independent profiles support Ran’s Korean identity and influencer status, but the reviewed sources do not grant metadata reuse for MuseBooks’ commercial catalog. A read-only exact-title/ISBN duplicate check returned zero rows, which applies only to the fields and variants queried. The SOOP 2024 Streamer Awards group photobook is also confirmed, but its exact bibliographic record remains incomplete. Ayul’s reported digital photobook still cannot be matched to either of two current Puremedia e-book listings. No database import or image operation occurred: metadata authorization remains unresolved for the candidates, and no exact cover image has permission documented for R2 storage and public delivery.

## Ran’s exact book clears discovery, not reuse

KADOKAWA identifies *蘭 らんファースト写真集* as a B5 physical book released on 29 March 2023, with 112 pages and ISBN 9784047374355 ([KADOKAWA product record](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/322301000031)). Pop’n’Roll describes Ran as Korea-born, an influencer and talent, and connects the book to the publisher; ORICON independently identifies Ran as a Korean model, TikToker, and popular influencer. The follower totals in those articles—2.3 million in March and 2.4 million in December 2023—are historical figures, not current counts ([Pop’n’Roll, 29 March 2023](https://popnroll.tv/articles/31921); [ORICON, 18 December 2023](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2306799/full/)). This is the strongest new subject-and-format match in the round: an identifiable physical photobook by a Korean influencer/model, published in Japan.

The live catalog lookup used a read-only, repeatable-read transaction and searched ISBN `9784047374355`, the Japanese title variants, “Ran First Photobook,” and a matching slug; it returned `Read-only exact-title/ISBN candidate rows: 0` and `[]`. That result means only that those identifiers and queried text did not return a duplicate. The KADOKAWA product page provides the facts but does not state a commercial metadata-reuse license. Its copyright policy directs publication-use requests to a separate inquiry, and its limited cover guidance applies to library book-introduction use rather than MuseBooks’ commercial public catalog ([KADOKAWA copyright policy](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/copyrightpolicy/); [KADOKAWA book-cover guidance](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/support/b/library/)). The project’s source-use register requires a compatible authorization for the intended reuse, so the candidate was not imported on the basis of the storefront alone ([project source-use register](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)).

## SOOP confirms a creator-group book, not a complete edition

SOOP’s ESG page confirms the sale of a 2024 Streamer Awards photobook as merchandise, with proceeds donated. Korean press identifies it as “2024 SOOP 스트리머 대상 포토북,” reports a limited SOOPTORE release from January 2025 with 65 streamers, and describes a hardcover of about 100 pages whose cover could feature a selected streamer. The official event page also points buyers to SOOPTORE for event merchandise ([SOOP ESG 2024](https://corp.sooplive.com/esg/2024/index.php?page=sharing); [Kuki News, 12 March 2025](https://m.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503120038); [SOOP event information](https://2024award.sooplive.co.kr/?page=information)). The store route visible in the reviewed page did not resolve to an identifiable book listing, and the “about 100 pages” description is not exact pagination.

Read-only catalog searches for the exact Korean and English book titles each returned `total=0`; this is not evidence that the work is absent under all aliases. The reviewed sources do not provide an ISBN, stable SOOPTORE item ID, exact imprint, exact page count, or complete participant list. The named examples establish a creator-group subject, but they do not verify all 65 participants’ identities or nationalities. SOOPTORE’s terms restrict commercial reuse or third-party provision of certain mall-owned information without approval, and no positive metadata license was located ([SOOPTORE terms](https://sooptore.sooplive.com/terms/use)). This remains a well-supported discovery lead, not a record ready to import.

## Ayul’s digital listing still lacks an edition link

A 2020 report says BJ Ayul, associated with PandaTV, released a debut digital photobook, but it supplies no stable product identifier, page count, or full edition data ([NewsCulture report reproduced by Ppomppu](https://m.ppomppu.co.kr/new/bbs_view.php?id=news_broadcast&no=128244)). Ayul’s Linktree calls a Puremedia product “Ayul 1st Sexy photo album,” while Puremedia currently lists separate Ayul “Story e-book” products as Vol. 28 and Vol. 51. The available listings do not connect either volume to the 2020 report, and the accessible pages do not show an individual product identifier, date, or pagination ([Ayul Linktree](https://linktr.ee/store_ayul); [Puremedia Ayul category](https://puremedia.kr/mall/m_mall_list.php?ps_ctid=01011300)). The evidence associates the creator with Korean-language pages and a Korean streaming platform, but does not establish her nationality or prove that she is a Korean national.

A fresh read-only, repeatable-read query found no matching person, work, or edition rows for the Ayul name and product variants searched. As with the Ran and SOOP checks, that zero-row result is scoped to the query and does not prove absence under other identifiers. Puremedia’s terms restrict commercial use or third-party provision of certain mall-owned information without prior consent, and no compatible metadata license or exact mapping to a listed volume was found ([Puremedia terms, §22](https://puremedia.kr/mall/m_page.php?ps_pname=page_member2)). The report therefore remains a lead rather than a catalog import candidate.

## R2 requires permission for each exact image

None of this round’s sources grants permission for an exact cover or preview image to be stored in R2 and publicly delivered by MuseBooks. KADOKAWA’s cover guidance is limited to specified library uses; the SOOP book has selectable cover variants and no identified asset license; Puremedia’s category listing likewise does not grant rights to rehost an Ayul image. A product page or image URL establishes provenance, not permission for storage, public delivery, or resizing ([KADOKAWA cover guidance](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/support/b/library/); [Kuki News](https://m.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503120038); [Puremedia Ayul category](https://puremedia.kr/mall/m_mall_list.php?ps_ctid=01011300); [project source-use register](../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)).

No database writes, media downloads, image staging, R2 uploads, or external contacts occurred in this round. The earlier catalog and image-rights audits are unchanged by these read-only checks. An eventual import needs an authorized factual-metadata source and a fresh duplicate check; an eventual R2 upload needs a separate grant that identifies the exact asset and covers the intended storage and public delivery.

## Conclusion

Ran’s photobook is now the clearest new catalog lead because the person, physical format, and exact edition are independently supported. The unresolved issue is source authorization for metadata reuse, not book identification. SOOP and Ayul need more edition-level evidence before they can be represented accurately, while image rights remain a separate decision for each exact cover. The next useful step is to locate an open-licensed record for Ran’s edition or an explicit metadata grant, then verify cover permission independently.
