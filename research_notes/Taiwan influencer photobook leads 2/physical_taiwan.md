# 2025–2026 Taiwan Physical Photobook Leads

## Import-ready candidate: 博文 TTENG《Play Bass Boy》

### Takeaway

One previously uncatalogued, product-level verified physical photobook qualifies as a Taiwan creator/artist lead: 博文 TTENG’s *Play Bass Boy*. Its Taiwan connection is as a bassist and creative member of Taiwanese rock band TRASH; its official Taiwan publisher page and a Taiwan retailer provide the printed edition details. It is an artist/creator case rather than a cheerleader or conventional social-media influencer.

### Cited Findings

- Warner Music Taiwan identifies 博文 as TRASH bassist; a Taiwan music profile describes TRASH as a Taiwanese band. [Warner artist page](https://www.warnermusic.com.tw/pages/-trash); [MyPlus profile](https://www.myplus.com.tw/article-20210828/1672/1).
- The publisher’s Taiwan product page sells the physical *博文 TTENG 首部個人創作紀實《Play Bass Boy》聲景寫真輯*: a 92-page 170 × 260 mm photobook, NFC mini-album charm, and one random two-cut photo from four designs. It lists NT$890 and currently says sold out. The copy describes January 23 as the release/launch date. [Warner Music Taiwan product page](https://www.warnermusic.com.tw/products/%E5%8D%9A%E6%96%87-tteng-%E9%A6%96%E9%83%A8%E5%80%8B%E4%BA%BA%E5%89%B5%E4%BD%9C%E7%B4%80%E5%AF%A6%E3%80%8Aplay-bass-boy%E3%80%8B%E8%81%B2%E6%99%AF%E5%AF%AB%E7%9C%9F%E8%BC%AF).
- Eslite lists Warner International Music as publisher, publication date 2026-01-22, a live NT$998 product with an Add to Cart control, EAN/barcode 5026854399287, no ISBN, and item code 2683082245002. Its package dimensions are 18 × 27 × 1 cm. [Eslite product page](https://www.eslite.com/product/10032019212683082245002).
- The Eslite cover asset URL is [mainCoverImage1.jpg](https://s2.eslite.com/unsafe/fit-in/x900/s.eslite.com/b2b/newItem/2026/01/21/2521_141957432_728_mainCoverImage1.jpg). The publisher page warns that product images are indicative mockups, so use the Eslite main-cover asset as the retailer cover source.
- I checked the current [Taiwan photobook catalog](../../scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md) and [import report](../../scraper/catalog-import-report.md); neither contains *Play Bass Boy*.

### Inferences

- The product is a physical photo book even though it is sold as a soundscape package with an NFC music accessory; preserve the EAN as a barcode and leave ISBN blank.
- Treat 2026-01-22 (Eslite bibliographic date) and 2026-01-23 (Warner’s stated launch date) as a one-day source discrepancy. Keep the source’s 170 × 260 mm book dimensions separate from Eslite’s package dimensions. Current price/availability also differs: Eslite shows NT$998 and add-to-cart, while Warner lists NT$890 and sold out.

### Gaps

- The publisher does not state an ISBN; Eslite also has no ISBN field. No additional print-run or binding information was found.

## Unresolved leads and media-only announcements

### Takeaway

Two leads remain outside the import-ready set: 顏尼克《顏》 has a confirmed completed crowdfunding photobook but no adequately verified individual Taiwan connection or final cover asset in this pass; 菲菲《韓你菲》 remains a media-announced title without a recovered product listing/cover source. Keep these separate from verified edition candidates.

### Cited Findings

- ZecZec describes 晏人物 x 顏尼克《顏》 as a physical, single-model photobook. The campaign reports 128 pages, 18 × 25.6 cm, planned April 2026 fulfilment and a 2026-04-20 shipping update; it does not display an ISBN/barcode in the material checked. [ZecZec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/NickYenHBD).
- The campaign describes the organizer as a Taiwan photography brand, but the evidence collected here does not establish 顏尼克’s own Taiwan origin/residence or an individual creator connection. Its campaign imagery was not verified as a final book cover; consequently it is not treated as import-ready for this Taiwan-subject task. [ZecZec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/NickYenHBD).
- News coverage confirms 菲菲／李恩菲’s physical solo photobook《韓你菲》was announced for a 2026-09-02 launch, but the previous catalog research did not recover a seller/publisher product URL or cover identified as the book cover. [民視 report](https://finance.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260); [UDN report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294); see the documented follow-up note in [catalog-import-report.md](../../scraper/catalog-import-report.md).

### Inferences

- Keep 顏尼克 and《韓你菲》as follow-up research leads, not fabricated or incomplete catalog editions. A product announcement alone does not verify identifiers, current availability, seller metadata, or cover provenance.

### Gaps

- For《顏》, an authoritative source tying the model to Taiwan and an identified final cover asset remain unverified. For《韓你菲》, a direct publisher/store listing and its printed-edition metadata, price/availability, and cover asset remain unverified.
