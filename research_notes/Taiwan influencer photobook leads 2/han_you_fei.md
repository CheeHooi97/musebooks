# 菲菲／李恩菲《韓你菲》

## Is the photobook and its physical format verified?

### Takeaway
Two Taiwan news outlets identify 《韓你菲》 as 菲菲／李恩菲’s first solo physical photobook and report a planned sales start of September 2, 2026 at 20:00. This verifies the work’s announced existence and format, but the sources do not independently confirm completed sales or current availability.

### Cited Findings
- UDN reported on August 31, 2026 that 李恩菲’s first personal physical photobook is titled 《韓你菲》, was photographed in Busan, and was scheduled to go on sale at 20:00 on September 2; it directed readers to her official social accounts for sales details. [UDN report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294)
- 民視 also described 《韓你菲》 as her first personal physical photobook and gave the same announced sale time and date. Its report credits the supplied photo to 杰斯汀整合行銷. [民視 report](https://health.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260)
- ENews repeated the physical-format and sale-announcement details and credited its images to 杰斯汀整合行銷. These reports appear to cover the same announcement, so they support existence but are not proof of a separate publisher or live seller listing. [ENews report](https://enews.tw/Article/1264654)

### Inferences
- The announced September 2 sale date has passed, but no source reviewed here verifies whether sales actually opened, whether the book remains available, or whether orders were fulfilled.

### Gaps
- No publisher catalog, ISBN record, or seller product page was located that independently establishes release completion or current availability. The available reports point readers to official social media for sales details instead of linking a product page. [UDN report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294); [民視 report](https://health.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260)
