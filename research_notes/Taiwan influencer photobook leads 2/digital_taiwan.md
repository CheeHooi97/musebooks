### Takeaway

The strongest new database lead is the digital edition of 彭彭's *彭然心動：彭彭寫真〖電子書祕藏版〗*. The existing catalog already has the matching physical work, so this is an edition-level addition rather than a new work.

[Lioneers official profile](https://www.lioneers.tw/pages/musegirls-1), [Rakuten Girls profile](https://cpblgirls.tw/front/4/RakutenGirlsmen/member.php?ID=Y3BibGdpcmxzJlJha3V0ZW5HaXJsc21lbg%3D%3D&PID=158)

## Digital edition metadata, image, and prices

### Takeaway

The digital edition is currently purchasable with EISBN 9786264448314 as a fixed-layout EPUB. Its product cover has a direct, recoverable image URL; retailer dates and short-term discounts differ, and no ebook page count was found.

### Cited Findings

- Books.com specifies EISBN 9786264448314, EPUB fixed layout, Taiwan as publication territory, and a 139.1 MB file; it separately lists 9786264448420 as the print ISBN. The listing describes the e-book edition as adding 40 photographs. [Books.com e-book product](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050359832)
- BOOK☆WALKER has an active product page for the e-book, labels the format fixed-layout EPUB, shows EISBN 9786264448314, and lists NT$476. [BOOK☆WALKER product 311413](https://www.bookwalker.com.tw/product/311413)
- Readmoo lists NT$476 and a promotional NT$419 through 2026-11-30; TAAZE lists NT$476; Books.com lists NT$476. [Readmoo product](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=175313), [TAAZE e-book product](https://www.taaze.tw/products/14100142649.html), [Books.com e-book product](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050359832)
- Momo's page shows a temporary NT$418 price for 2026-10-01–10-31 against its NT$476 promotional price, confirming that observed checkout discounts can vary by store and date. [momoBOOK product 15774121](https://www.momoshop.com.tw/product/15774121?Area=DgrpCategory&ctype=B&mdiv=4003200047-bt_9_002_01&str_category_code=4003200047)

### Inferences

- Use EISBN 9786264448314 for the digital edition and keep print ISBN 9786264448420 on the physical edition. The sources explicitly show both identifiers and distinguish them by format. [Books.com e-book product](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050359832), [BOOK☆WALKER product 311413](https://www.bookwalker.com.tw/product/311413)
- Capture NT$476 as the listed retailer ask if a single standard price is required; record discounts separately with observation date and retailer because Readmoo and momo show time-limited lower prices. [Readmoo product](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=175313), [momoBOOK product 15774121](https://www.momoshop.com.tw/product/15774121?Area=DgrpCategory&ctype=B&mdiv=4003200047-bt_9_002_01&str_category_code=4003200047)

### Gaps

- Books.com and the matching physical listing date the publication 2026-09-11, while BOOK☆WALKER's digital product page/search record gives 2026-10-12. Treat these as retailer-specific publication/release dates unless an authoritative publisher notice clarifies whether the digital release was delayed. [Books.com e-book product](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050359832), [BOOK☆WALKER product 311413](https://www.bookwalker.com.tw/product/311413), [Sanmin print listing](https://www.sanmin.com.tw/product/index/015778708)

The 144-page figure belongs to the print edition, so it should not be copied onto the EPUB without product-level confirmation; the e-book adds 40 images. [Books.com e-book product](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050359832), [Sanmin print listing](https://www.sanmin.com.tw/product/index/015778708)
