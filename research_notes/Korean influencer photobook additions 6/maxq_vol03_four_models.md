# MAXQ Special Edition Vol. 3 — four models

## Finding

RIDI product [3563000074](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000074) is *MAXQ Special Edition 3호*, released 2020-12-24 as a 41-page PDF e-book by MAXQ International. RIDI's description calls this series an online photobook selecting MAXQ pictorials not previously released online.

Newstown's contemporaneous report, using MAXQ-supplied imagery, names Jeong Hanna (정한나), Lee Ha-rin (이하린), Kang Ju-yeon (강주연), and Lee Seon-i (이선이) as the four models for the matching “Muscle Mania Four Beauties of Fantasy Island” concept; it also says the four were selected for the MAXQ Special Edition Vol. 3. Maeil Business separately identifies Jeong Hanna as a fitness influencer. The other three are recorded as models because the sources reviewed here do not establish their independent creator status.

## Database check and import

A repeatable-read pre-import query found no edition with the exact RIDI metadata URL and no work with a matching Vol. 3 title. Jeong Hanna's existing influencer person record was reused; exact-name checks found no existing rows for the other three models. The import adds one digital photobook work, one PDF edition, and four featured credits. It does not assert that the photographs are unique relative to the models' other MAXQ appearances.

## Image and catalog limits

The retailer's cover and the paid PDF are not copied to R2. No source located in this pass grants reuse rights for the exact cover image. No retailer price/availability observation or listing was added. The identity/photo overlap between this compilation and other MAXQ products remains unreviewed.
