# Kim Woo-hyun / Woo Hyeon Fanding photobooks

## Which candidate photobooks, formats, dates, specifications, and statuses can be verified?

### Takeaway

Fanding verifies a successful 2023 campaign titled *WOO HYEON'S Nxde PhotoBook* and creator posts verify a successful 2025 *Extreme* printed-photobook project. The public text does not establish the 2023 book's physical/digital medium or either title's bibliographic specifications; the 2025 delivery date was revised and the final shipment date is not stated in the accessible feed.

### Cited Findings

- The 2023 Fanding campaign is titled *WOO HYEON'S Nxde PhotoBook*, identifies the creator as “우현 woohyeon / 예술가 김우현,” and describes it as “모델 우현의 처음이자 마지막 누드 화보집” (the model Woo Hyeon's first and last nude photobook). It is marked successful and ended, with 354% funding, ₩106,461,000 raised against a ₩30,000,000 goal, and 358 participants. [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/funding/140/)
- Fanding lists the 2023 funding period as 2023-04-15 through 2023-06-15 and its “Reward Due date” as 2023-08-16 15:00. The campaign is categorized as Photography. This is a promised reward date, not independently verified proof that parcels were shipped or received on that day. [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/funding/140/)
- The campaign's accessible text does not state page count, dimensions, ISBN, publisher/imprint, edition statement, or whether the book reward is paper or digital; its four detailed project sheets are embedded as images rather than extractable text on the public page. [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/funding/140/)
- On 2025-03-14 the creator's Fanding feed advertised “DJ YOR 'EXTREME' 오키나와 종이 화보집 런칭” (an Okinawa printed photobook launch); on 2025-04-15 a post said the first paper photobook project *Extreme* with WOOZIC STARZ had ended successfully. [Fanding creator feed](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/)
- The same creator feed lists a 2025-04-18 post announcing a changed photobook delivery schedule and fan-meeting information, followed by a 2025-05-20 post about photobook shipping and a fan-meeting schedule. The visible feed does not disclose the revised dispatch date or confirm a specific date of actual shipment. [Fanding creator feed](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/)
- A June 2023 Asian Candy Blog post called a Woo Hyeon adult photobook released worldwide the “Extreme Photobook” and mentioned “Volume 1.” This is a weak, third-party adult-content blog source rather than a publisher catalog, but it creates a material title/identity overlap with the 2025 *Extreme* project. [Asian Candy Blog](https://cmnetasia.wixsite.com/asiancandy/post/congratulations-leehee-s-11th-anniversary)
- The Fanding creator's community feed also contains a 2023 photobook review that discusses a *Mandarin* photobook, while a 2025 Korean profile article identifies Kim Woo-hyun as an influencer/model and reports on her final 2023 MAXIM shoot. These do not establish whether *Mandarin*, *Cyber Punk*, *Nxde*, and the 2023 *Extreme Photobook* are separate books, names for the same project, or different versions. [Fanding review](https://fanding.kr/ja/%40woohye0n/community/42257/), [Star Daily News via Nate](https://news.nate.com/view/20230425n22610)

### Inferences

- The 2023 Fanding campaign is strong evidence that a completed photobook project existed, but the `reward due date` alone does not let us assign it the catalog's physical or digital format. Treating it as a print book would be an unsupported inference. [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/funding/140/)
- The 2025 *Extreme* project is explicitly a printed/paper photobook and was declared successfully completed; March 14 is a launch announcement, April 15 is the completion announcement, and the April/May posts show subsequent schedule/shipping communications. These sources support recording a physical book/project, but not an exact publication or confirmed ship date. [Fanding creator feed](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/)
- The 2025 *Extreme* may be a newly printed edition of the 2023 worldwide *Extreme Photobook*, but the sources do not prove that the photographed contents match. It should not yet be added as an unrelated new work or merged into the 2023 title without content/edition comparison. [Asian Candy Blog](https://cmnetasia.wixsite.com/asiancandy/post/congratulations-leehee-s-11th-anniversary), [Fanding creator feed](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/)
- A second-party Korean article describes Kim Woo-hyun as a “first-generation influencer” and sexy model, and traces her public profile to the Musinsa denim campaign, fashion modeling, YouTube, and MAXIM; MAXIM's own 2018 article also described her as an SNS star who gained fame through Instagram. This adequately establishes that the creator fits the influencer/model scope, independently of the Fanding campaign copy. [Star Daily News via Nate](https://news.nate.com/view/20230425n22610), [MAXIM Korea](https://www.maximkorea.net/cms/contents_view.php?contents_cate2=7&contents_uid=12411)

### Gaps

- No readable primary-source reward breakdown or product sheet was retrieved for the 2023 *Nxde PhotoBook*; medium, dimensions, pagination, publisher, and release-versus-shipping date remain unknown.
- The 2025 feed is not a standalone product/campaign record with pledge tiers, specs, or a bibliographic imprint; the actual ship date and product dimensions/page count remain unknown.
- The relation among the names *Nxde PhotoBook*, *Mandarin*, *Cyber Punk*, and *Extreme Photobook* has not been resolved. The 2025 *Extreme* could be a new photo set or a physical edition of an earlier work.

## Does the evidence support creating metadata-only catalog records now?

### Takeaway

The creator's eligibility and the existence/success of both projects are well-supported, but neither candidate is safely import-ready as a distinct physical/digital work without resolving format and title/edition identity. Recommend holding both for catalog writes; 2025 is the stronger follow-up candidate because print format is explicit, and can be staged as a physical edition once the possible 2023 *Extreme Photobook* overlap is checked.
