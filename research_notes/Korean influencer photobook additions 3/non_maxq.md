# Korean Influencer Photobooks Outside MAXQ Secret B / Special Edition

## Verified candidates

### Takeaway

Pyo Eunji (표은지) is a strong Korean creator match with several exact, non-MAXQ photo-book products: a creator-store print book, a publisher-issued print/e-book title, and publisher-branded or Cupick digital photo books. Kim Woo-hyun (우현) also has a successful creator-led Fanding photo-book campaign; her older MAXIM book appears already in MuseBooks, so only the newer work should be considered after a live duplicate check.

### Cited Findings

- Pyo Eunji's official profile describes her as a Korean gravure model and YouTuber and documents Instagram/YouTube audience figures as of 2022-01-11. The same official biography lists multiple earlier solo books and limited photobooks, but without enough product specifications to import those older leads yet. [Official profile](https://pyoeunji.com/pages/profile)
- Her official store lists the Japan Official Fan Club exclusive first-edition photo book, says it was released with her Japan activities in 2022, and gives 168 pages, A4 size, and ¥4,800. The page offers a limited restock and international shipping. [Official store listing](https://pyoeunji.com/en/products/restock-first-edition-pyo-eunji-photo-book-japan-official-fan-club-only)
- KADOKAWA identifies the print book *ピョ・ウンジ1st写真集 輪舞曲* (Rondo), published 2023-08-10, A4, 128 pages, ISBN 9784048976268; it also lists an electronic edition of the same title. KADOKAWA describes Pyo as a Korean YouTuber and model. Treat the electronic version as an edition of the same work, rather than a second work. [KADOKAWA book record](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/322303002115/)
- Cupick's product page identifies *EX Taishu Digital Photobook Pyo Eunji “Tokyo Life — Dokyo Saenghwal —”* as a 75-page digital photo book, with Futabasha as publisher and EX Taishu as series. Its product page says it opens in browser after purchase and labels Pyo a Korean YouTuber/model. [Cupick product page](https://cupick.jp/en/products/pyoeunji-tokyoseikatsu)
- Cupick lists *Sunshine* as an 81-page digital photo book and identifies it as a Cupick original featuring Pyo; its description also identifies her as a YouTuber. [Cupick product page](https://cupick.jp/ko/products/pyoeunji-likesunshine)

Cupick's listing calls her a Korean influencer and YouTuber; a publisher-side listing was not located in this pass. [Cupick product page](https://cupick.jp/en/products/pyoeunji-amaryllis)

A secondary marketplace listing gives the print book's title and 68-page B5 specifications. The text supports related print/digital editions with some different images; compare creator-authorized product metadata before modeling the relationship. [Cupick deluxe listing](https://cupick.jp/en/products/pyoeunji-mouhitorinowatashi-delux); [Ruten print listing](https://www.ruten.com.tw/item/22508790027822/)
- Fanding's direct creator page identifies *WOO HYEON'S Nxde PhotoBook* as a successful photography campaign for model Woo Hyeon / Kim Woo-hyun: it reached 354% of goal with 358 participants, ran 2023-04-15 through 2023-06-15, and listed rewards for dispatch on 2023-08-16. The campaign describes it as her nude photo book, but the reviewed page does not specify page count or binding. [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/%40woohye0n/funding/140/)
- The same creator's Fanding page says her first printed photobook *Extreme*, made with WOOZIC STARZ, completed successfully in April 2025 and later shows delivery notices. The exact format is explicit in the creator's phrase “첫 종이화보집” (“first printed photo book”). [Creator Fanding page](https://fanding.kr/en/%40woohye0n/)
- MAXIM's official report calls Kim Woo-hyun a prominent SNS star whose fame came from Instagram influence and describes its 160-page *MAXIM B-SIDE—Woo-hyeon* book, released 2018-12-05. MuseBooks already has a public catalog page for this MAXIM work, so it is a duplicate-check reference rather than a new addition. [MAXIM report](https://www.maximkorea.net/cms/contents_view.php?contents_cate2=7&contents_uid=12411); [MuseBooks catalog page](https://musebooks.my/books/korea-maxim-bside-2018-4-kim-woo-hyun)

### Inferences

- Pyo Eunji's exact KADOKAWA and official-store products, plus the Cupick listings, are high-confidence metadata candidates if the live catalog confirms they are absent. Preserve product/edition distinctions: the KADOKAWA e-book matches its print title, and *Another Me Deluxe* is explicitly related to the print *Another Me*.
- Kim Woo-hyun's 2023 campaign and 2025 print-book announcement are new product candidates relative to the 2018 MAXIM book. A live DB check is still needed for titles and person aliases before import.
- Public product listings and images are evidence that books exist; they do not grant permission to copy covers or interiors into R2.

### Gaps

- No live MuseBooks duplicate lookup was performed for these products. The earlier reports checked for repeats only in their listed MAXQ/creator additions; none of the Pyo Eunji, Fanding Kim Woo-hyun, or 2018 MAXIM titles appears in those two reports.
- Page count, ISBN, and edition/binding details for Pyo's 2022 official fan-club book are provided only for pages and size; no ISBN or exact original publication day was found.
- *Tokyo Life*, *Sunshine*, *Bold Amaryllis*, and *Another Me Deluxe* have exact storefront evidence; publisher-side cross-checks remain desirable, particularly for Cupick Original and Wani Books metadata.
- The 68-page print *Another Me* evidence located here is from a secondary marketplace, not the creator's own store or Comiket organizer. The digital seller states that the print version was sold at C105, but the original campaign/product page was not found.
- Fanding's 2023 *Nxde PhotoBook* campaign provides exact campaign and shipping information, but it does not state physical specifications. The 2025 *Extreme* page establishes a printed book and campaign completion, but not pagination, ISBN, or full retail/campaign terms.
- No documentation granting R2 reuse rights for any exact book cover or image was found.
