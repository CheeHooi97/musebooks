# Pyo Eunji and additional Korean creator photobooks

## Independent records for the Cupick-discovered titles

### Takeaway
Independent catalog sources verify Tokyo Life and Bold Amaryllis as digital photobooks. Pyo Eunji’s own official store independently verifies a physical C105 booklet titled Another Me, but I found no qualifying publisher or retailer record for Sunshine or for a separate digital Another Me Deluxe Edition. Cupick pages and their product text were not used as bibliographic evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Rakuten Kobo lists EX Taishu Digital Photobook No. 32, Pyo Eunji “Tokyo Life — Dokyo Saenghwal,” as a Futabasha digital photo book released 2022-09-26, 76 pages, product ID 8942124843230; BookWalker independently lists the same title in Futabasha’s EX Taishu digital photobook series. [Rakuten Kobo](https://www.kobo.com/us/en/ebook/1xpXER34kjaxPRcqg2CQCA); [BookWalker series](https://bookwalker.jp/company/98/?np=0&order=rank&qnot%5B0%5D=KADOKAWA&qr15=1&qser=217745)
- Rakuten Kobo lists “Bold Amaryllis” as a Wani Books digital photobook by Pyo Eunji and photographer Hiroyuki Sato, released 2024-12-06, 80 pages, product ID 8909127751945. [Rakuten Kobo](https://books.rakuten.co.jp/rk/1203f5b17e723e96a497f1f2ed16084b/)
- Pyo Eunji’s official fan-club store identifies its C105 set as a first-circle publication set sold for the 2024-12-30 Tokyo event and itemizes DokiDoki (B5, 64 pages), PuniPuni (B5, 68 pages), and AnotherMe (B5, 68 pages). This independently verifies a physical AnotherMe booklet; it does not verify a distinct digital Deluxe Edition. [Official C105 product page](https://pyoeunji.com/ko/products/c105set2)
- Sponichi ran a photo-gallery page naming “Sunshine” as a Cupick-limited digital photobook, but it is media coverage rather than a publisher or retailer product record and does not meet the requested primary-source standard. [Sponichi photo gallery](https://www.idolreport.jp/gravure/yomimono/idol/gazo/20240530b00083000030000p.html)

### Inferences
- Tokyo Life and Bold Amaryllis meet the bibliographic evidence threshold for distinct digital photobook works, subject to the project’s normal live-catalog duplicate check.
- AnotherMe should be treated as a physical booklet within the C105 set unless a separate individual product record is found; the shared title alone does not establish that it is the same content as any digital product.

### Gaps
- No independent publisher or retailer product page was found for Sunshine. The cited Sponichi page is secondary coverage and should not substitute for an authorized listing.
- No independent publisher or retailer page was found for a separate digital Another Me Deluxe Edition. The official store verifies only the physical C105 booklet.
- A publisher/retailer record with exact release date, product identifier, and page count for Sunshine remains unavailable.

[Official profile](https://pyoeunji.com/ko/pages/profile); [KADOKAWA Rondo record](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/322303002115/)

### Inferences
- For C105 and C107, each booklet has its own title and page count, while the current source offers a bundle listing. Catalog each as a distinct physical work/edition only if that matches MuseBooks’ work model; retain the bundle as its retail package/source context.
- The 2026 weekly-playboy digital release and the 2026 DIGVII books are new-title candidates, not presumed absent from PostgreSQL. Check creator identity and product IDs against existing records before creating anything.
- “Omotenashi,” “Juicy,” “Kisekae Oneesan,” and “Time yo, Tomare” have clear publisher/retailer records and can be considered for metadata-only imports after duplicate checks.

### Gaps
- Individual ISBNs/product codes and standalone item pages were not located for the C105/C107 event booklets; the official fan-club bundle pages are the evidence available here.
- The official store page for PHOTO BOOK 2022 does not state a publication day; the 2021-12 to 2022-11 span describes its contents, not an exact release date.
- The official profile contains older named photo-book leads (“Bikini,” “Okinawa Bunny,” and an “Eunji” solo book) but this review did not find independent item-level publisher/retailer records for them. [Official profile](https://pyoeunji.com/ko/pages/profile)

## Import and image-rights boundaries

### Takeaway
This research supports metadata-only catalog candidates. It does not establish permission to copy any cover, sample, or product image into R2.

### Cited Findings
- The project source guide says a public product page is not a reuse license and instructs that image fields stay blank until permission for the exact asset is documented. [Project source guide](../../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)
- The product sources reviewed provide bibliographic and sales information, but none cited here grants MuseBooks permission to reproduce or host its images. [KADOKAWA](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/322303002115/); [Wani/Rakuten Kobo](https://books.rakuten.co.jp/rk/1203f5b17e723e96a497f1f2ed16084b/); [Pyo Eunji official store](https://pyoeunji.com/ko/collections/all)

### Inferences
- Keep source URLs and metadata provenance, leave R2 image fields empty, and do not download covers or samples until exact-asset reuse permission is documented.
- Before import, run the normal catalog duplicate checks for every product identifier and exact title, especially for differently formatted editions of Rondo or other already-known Pyo Eunji works.

### Gaps
- No exact cover/sample asset permission or license was found for the publisher, retailer, or official-store images reviewed.
- Database status was not checked for these newly discovered candidates in this research pass.
