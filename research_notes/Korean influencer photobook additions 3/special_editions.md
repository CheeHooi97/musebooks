# MAXQ Special Edition leads

## Exact product records and featured-creator evidence

### Takeaway
Both RIDI records are well-defined MAXQ digital photobooks. Vol. 1 is the clearest new candidate for the influencer catalog because it features three models and contemporary coverage verifies Choi Seolhwa as a YouTube fitness creator; creator evidence is not equally strong for Gong Minseo and Son Sohee. Vol. 10 is also eligible through Park Eunhye's established YouTube work, but Park already has three separate MAXQ books in the known catalog cohort.

### Cited Findings
- RIDI lists **MAXQ Special Edition 1호 / MAXQ Special Edition Vol. 1**, subtitle “머슬여제 3인방- 공민서, 손소희, 최설화,” by 맥스큐 편집부, published by ㈜맥스큐인터내셔널. Its listing records the e-book date as 2020-08-03, paper publication date as 2020-07-31, a 50-page/18.4 MB PDF, current e-book purchase price KRW 3,900 and 7-day rental KRW 2,900. The product description says Special Edition is an online-unpublished photo book selecting MAXQ photographs. [RIDI Vol. 1](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000054)
- Big Data News's July 2020 report calls Choi Seolhwa a fitness model and broadcaster, identifies her YouTube channel “최설화의 앞으로뒤태,” and says she posted fitness content on YouTube and Instagram. This is contemporary evidence that at least one of Vol. 1's named subjects was an active creator around the product's release. [Big Data News](https://www.thebigdata.co.kr/view.php?ud=202007161721581840992c130dbe_23)
- MAXQ's 2017 profile identifies Gong Minseo as a Musclemania winner and fitness model; it does not describe her as an influencer or document a creator channel. [MAXQ profile of Gong Minseo](https://maxq.kr/articles/meoseulmania-4gwanwang-gongminseo)
- New Daily's 2017 photo report identifies Gong Minseo, Son Sohee and Choi Seolhwa together as Road FC ring models at a Muscle & MAXQ magazine photo shoot. It establishes earlier MAXQ photography of the same trio, not that those particular frames appear in Special Edition Vol. 1. [New Daily](https://www.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2017/02/15/2017021500113.html)
- RIDI lists **MAXQ Special Edition 10호 / MAXQ Special Edition Vol. 10**, subject 박은혜, by 맥스큐 편집부 and publisher ㈜맥스큐인터내셔널. Its record gives a 2024-06-27 e-book date, 33-page/4.0 MB PDF, purchase price KRW 4,900 and 7-day rental KRW 3,900. The product description calls the line an online-unpublished book of selected MAXQ photos. [RIDI Vol. 10](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000201)
- Sports Seoul identifies Park Eunhye as the operator of YouTube channel 자몽튜브, with 60,000 subscribers reported in August 2020, and describes her as a model, Chinese-language instructor and YouTuber. This supports her creator eligibility independent of RIDI's product record. [Sports Seoul](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/948176)

### Inferences
- Vol. 1 meets the catalog's creator-photo-book inclusion bar through Choi Seolhwa, though the present evidence supports Gong and Son as featured fitness/ring models rather than independently verified influencers.
- The exact RIDI records distinguish Vol. 1 and Vol. 10 as separate numbered digital retail products. Recording each product separately is defensible at product-title level. It is not evidence that every photograph is unique or that any two products must be modeled as separate underlying photo works.

### Gaps
- No authorized interior preview was found that permits comparison of the Vol. 1 photographs against the trio's prior MAXQ shoot or other print issues. The “online-unpublished” description does not disclose whether any photo was previously printed.
- No authorized interior comparison was found for Vol. 10 against Park Eunhye's existing Secret B books. Product-level distinctness is verified; image overlap remains unknown.
- The cited sources do not establish creator/influencer status independently for Gong Minseo or Son Sohee.

## Catalog distinction, overlap, and strongest next candidate

### Takeaway
Vol. 1 is the cleaner next candidate from the two: the prior additions report lists no existing Vol. 1 import, and its three-subject package has contemporary evidence for at least one active creator. Vol. 10 is an exact additional product but less incremental because the catalog already includes Park's Secret B No. 1, Vol. 5, and Compilation Vol. 03. Neither the prior report nor this research proves that either exact title is absent from the live database; a read-only exact-title/product-ID duplicate query is still required before import.

### Inferences
- If the project tracks book products, either Special Edition is recordable as its own titled product, with any photo reuse represented through shared media or an edition/work relationship after comparison. If the project tracks unique photo sets as works, the currently unavailable interiors are insufficient to decide whether to create a new underlying work or associate the edition with existing Park/Choi works.
- Of Vol. 1 versus Vol. 10, Vol. 1 is the strongest report-level next import. Vol. 10 remains defensible if the importer records distinct product titles, but its photoset relationship should stay unresolved until review.
- An exact RIDI/product ID is available for both Vol. 1 and Vol. 10, so a live catalog duplicate check can use IDs 3563000054 and 3563000201 as well as titles.

### Gaps
- Live DB exact-title/product-ID duplicate query was not run in this researcher task.
- Neither product's paid PDF was inspected; image overlap with earlier MAXQ books is unknown.
- No image license or reuse permission was established. Public RIDI cover/product pages do not grant R2 mirroring rights; keep covers out of R2 unless the rights record changes.
