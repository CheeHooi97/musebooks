## Secret B Vol. 6 — Baek Seonghye

### Takeaway
Vol. 6 is a distinct, retailer-listed digital product featuring Baek Seonghye, whose influencer status is explicitly documented. It can be recorded as its own product/edition; whether it merits a separate work from Baek’s already cataloged Vol. 16 or the physical No. 3 cannot be settled without an interior comparison.

### Cited Findings
- Aladin lists *맥스큐 디지털화보집 Secret B Vol.6 백성혜* as an e-book PDF published by MAXQ International in November 2020 at ₩6,900; its listing does not expose a page count or exact day. ([Aladin e-book listing](https://www.aladin.co.kr/m/mbest.aspx?BestType=EBookBestseller&BranchType=9&CID=38820&InKids=0))
- RIDI’s current Vol. 14 page separately names “Secret B 6호-백성혜” and “Secret B 16호-백성혜 Special” in the Secret B series list; the page describes the series as digital-only photobooks. ([RIDI Secret B series listing](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000182))
- Aju Economy’s June 2022 coverage calls Baek an SNS-popular influencer and muscle-queen, and reports her MAXQ cover shoot; the article supports person identity/status but is about a different MAXQ Special Edition shoot, not Vol. 6. ([Aju Economy](https://www.ajunews.com/view/20220604233643912?photoIdx=0&term=0))

### Inferences
- Vol. 6 and Vol. 16 have separate issue numbers; Aladin dates Vol. 6 to November 2020, and RIDI’s separately listed Vol. 16 product is another digital item. Separate product/edition records preserve that evidence even if a future interior check links both to one bibliographic work. ([RIDI Vol. 16](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000203))
- Baek’s influencer evidence makes this the strongest of the four candidates for inclusion under an influencer criterion.

### Gaps
- Aladin did not provide pagination, ISBN/ECN, or an exact publication day; RIDI’s visible series index did not expose Vol. 6’s individual item page.
- No reviewed source establishes whether Vol. 6 reuses any images from the already imported Vol. 16 or physical Secret B No. 3.
