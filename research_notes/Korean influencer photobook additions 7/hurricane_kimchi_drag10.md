# Anessa and Hurricane Kimchi 10th-anniversary photobook

## Product evidence

Tumblbug's official campaign page names **드랙퀸 아네싸와 허리케인 김치의 10주년 포토북 & 포스터**, says the two drag queens are making a photobook and poster for their 10th debut anniversary, and reports a successful KRW 2,222,000 campaign backed by 60 supporters. The page says required photographs and illustrations were ready for production. It does not establish the actual shipment date, ISBN, page count, publisher imprint, or exact release date. Campaign dates are not publication dates. ([Official campaign](https://tumblbug.com/drag10))

## Creator identity and scope

Heezy Yang's official website describes them as a Seoul-born Korean queer artist and activist and connects their drag persona Hurricane Kimchi to original songwriting and music-video releases. The official music page links the Hurricane Kimchi Instagram and other public channels. This is sufficient evidence to record the book as **creator-led**, but the reviewed sources do not call either featured person an influencer. Anessa is retained as a co-featured drag performer on the strength of the campaign page; no separate creator claim is made for her. ([Official bio](https://heezyyang.wixsite.com/heezyyang), [official music/social page](https://heezyyang.wixsite.com/heezyyang/music))
