# Korean influencer photobooks 18 — Creator Stores

## Key question 1: Did a creator store or Korean marketplace yield a new source-cleared title?

### Takeaway
No title passed all gates for metadata import. Studio BOM’s Patreon page gives an exact digital photobook listing, but Patreon’s current terms limit access to creators’ creations to private, personal, non-commercial use and its Security Policy prohibits crawling, scraping, or otherwise indexing Patreon information. The product is also not verified as the work of a Korean influencer named Gyuri, so it is not ready for catalog entry.

### Cited findings
- Patreon lists **[PHOTOBOOK 51P] AUG 2026 #1 | GYURI (규리) | AFTER SCHOOL — PART 2: TEACHER**, names the creator as Studio BOM 봄, and exposes a ZIP attachment named `gyuri202608n1.zip`; the page describes downloadable 4000px and 2800px photos. This establishes an exact digital product, but the “51P” label is retained as part of the listing title, not independently verified as a count of pages in a PDF. [Studio BOM product page](https://www.patreon.com/studiobom/posts/photobook-51p-1-166816958)
- Patreon’s [Terms of Use, dated May 27, 2026](https://www.patreon.com/policy/legal), grants access to a creator’s creations only for private, personal, non-promotional, non-commercial use. Patreon’s [Security Policy](https://www.patreon.com/policy/security) separately says not to crawl, scrape, or otherwise index information on Patreon. These conditions do not clear commercial use of Patreon product data for this catalog; written authorization would be needed before importing it.
- Studio BOM’s [creator introduction](https://www.patreon.com/studiobom/posts/studio-bom-new-118010430) describes its studio as being in Seoul, South Korea and identifies OhEunDam as a Korean grid girl, YouTuber, and BJ. It describes recurring Studio BOM photobooks, but does not identify Gyuri as a Korean influencer. The studio’s location and the other model’s profile cannot be attributed to Gyuri.
- Current [Tumblbug service terms](https://help.tumblbug.com/ko/articles/3dde1950) prohibit unapproved commercial activity using the service and restrict commercial use of service material. Current [Wadiz supporter terms](https://www.wadiz.io/web/wterms/service_reward) prohibit unauthorized copying, collection, transmission, or distribution of site product, business, or content data, including automated methods. No exact candidate on either marketplace was cleared for import in this pass.
- CTEE’s [Terms of Service](https://ctee.gitbook.io/terms/en/ctee/terms_of_service) prohibit using service information for commercial purposes without company approval; its [copyright and use guidance](https://ctee.gitbook.io/help_customer/hello/faq/refund) says creator content is restricted to non-commercial use unless otherwise stated. CTEE was therefore excluded as a metadata-import source without approval.

### Inferences
- The Studio BOM page is useful as a discovery lead only. Given Patreon’s explicit no-indexing rule and the non-commercial access license, I did not treat a manually viewed listing as authorization for commercial catalog use.
- No database duplicate lookup was run: the only exact new product lead is blocked by source terms, and the associated model also fails the identity/origin gate. No import manifest was created or applied.
- No cover or product image was copied or uploaded. No reviewed page grants asset-specific permission for the exact image, R2 hosting, and public delivery.

### Gaps
- Written permission from Patreon and/or the relevant rights holder for commercial use of exact factual metadata, or an independently cleared official publisher/store source for the same edition.
- A reliable source connecting Gyuri (규리) to a specific public creator identity and establishing Korean origin.
- No new title was found on Tumblbug or Wadiz that could be verified across title, creator identity, origin, and source-use conditions within this pass.

## Key question 2: What source-access decisions should guide the next pass?

### Takeaway
The strongest new creator-store lead remains on hold because the source’s current terms make it unsuitable for a commercial metadata import without approval. Continue from a publisher or sales platform whose terms permit the intended manual factual catalog use, and verify each candidate’s person identity separately.

### Cited findings
- Patreon’s terms say creator creations are licensed to members only for private, personal, non-promotional, non-commercial use; its Security Policy prohibits crawling, scraping, or otherwise indexing Patreon information. [Terms of Use](https://www.patreon.com/policy/legal) · [Security Policy](https://www.patreon.com/policy/security)
- CTEE terms bar commercial use of service information without company approval, and its Help Center says creator content is non-commercial unless otherwise stated. [CTEE Terms](https://ctee.gitbook.io/terms/en/ctee/terms_of_service) · [CTEE copyright/use guidance](https://ctee.gitbook.io/help_customer/hello/faq/refund)
- Tumblbug’s current service terms restrict commercial use of service information/material without approval. [Tumblbug service terms](https://help.tumblbug.com/ko/articles/3dde1950)
- Wadiz’s current supporter terms prohibit unauthorized collection and redistribution of site data, including product and content information. [Wadiz supporter terms](https://www.wadiz.io/web/wterms/service_reward)

### Inferences
- A public product page, the ability to view its title, or the creator’s own right to sell the work does not itself authorize a third party to build a commercial catalog from that platform’s information or to host its cover in R2.

### Gaps
- Current source authorization for metadata use on Patreon, CTEE, Tumblbug, and Wadiz remains unconfirmed; no inquiries were sent.
- Exact cover-image permission for public R2 delivery remains unresolved for all leads reviewed here.
