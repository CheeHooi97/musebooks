# Korean Creator Photobook Campaigns — Crowdfunding Pass 11

## Is there a new creator-linked photobook product?

### Takeaway
A separate physical photobook listed by LEEHEE EXPRESS is a promising new record candidate: **WOOZIC STARS IN OKINAWA PHOTOBOOK_YORU**. The listing identifies a model as “Woo” and gives book dimensions and a page description, but the match between “Woo” and model Kim Woohyeon is not explicitly stated on the product page.

### Cited Findings
- LEEHEE EXPRESS lists the exact title **WOOZIC STARS IN OKINAWA PHOTOBOOK_YORU** at [product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060); the page shows $112.00, dimensions 225 × 305 mm, “200 page of Artwork,” “inc. extreme contents of Woo,” and names model Woo, photographer Zinho, editor Taeey, writer Chae Heejin, book designer Jeon Sujin, producer LEEHEE, and “Present by LEEHEE EXPRESS.”
- The product page presents a purchase control, so it is offered as a product rather than only announced as a future concept ([LEEHEE EXPRESS listing](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060)).
- The creator page for **우현 woohyeon** labels the account Entertainment > Model and “예술가 김우현”; its post list includes a notice that a WOOZIC STARZ “Extreme” paper-photobook project succeeded ([creator profile and post list](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/)).

### Inferences
- The product’s 225 × 305 mm size and 200-page description support classifying the LEEHEE EXPRESS listing as a physical photobook, though “200 page of Artwork” is reproduced as stated rather than normalized to a verified pagination field ([product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060)).
- “Woo” likely refers to Kim Woohyeon because the product is branded WOOZIC STARS and the creator’s Fanding page independently connects Kim Woohyeon with a WOOZIC STARZ physical-photobook project. The product page itself does not spell out Kim Woohyeon, so this identity match should be treated as provisional ([product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060); [Fanding profile](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/)).

### Gaps
- The product page does not state an ISBN, release date, formal publisher/imprint, or a clearly normalized page count. Its phrase “200 page of Artwork” needs confirmation before setting a database page-count field ([product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060)).
- Fanding’s profile lists the successful 2025 **Extreme** paper-photobook project, while the retail product is titled **YORU**; I could not verify that YORU is a reward, edition, or companion to that specific campaign. Do not describe the YORU SKU as the fulfilled Extreme campaign without that link ([Fanding profile](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/); [LEEHEE EXPRESS product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060)).

## Is there a verified campaign for this exact product, and is it distinct from existing records?

### Takeaway
The LEEHEE EXPRESS product is a credible physical-book lead, but a crowdfunding campaign for the exact **YORU** title remains unverified. A different, successful Kim Woohyeon Fanding campaign was already imported in the live workspace and is excluded from this pass to prevent a duplicate.

### Cited Findings
- The Fanding profile lists the 2025 WOOZIC STARZ **Extreme** project as a successful first paper-photobook project; this verifies that a related campaign existed, not that it was the same product as **YORU** ([Fanding creator profile](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/)).
- The workspace already contains **WOO HYEON'S Nxde PhotoBook** in [batch 138 import](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-138-import.json) and [database read-back](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-138-db-readback.json). That separate 2023 Fanding campaign is not counted as a new finding here.
- The exact YORU product is listed by LEEHEE EXPRESS with product details and a purchase control, but that listing does not identify itself as a crowdfunding reward or show campaign metrics ([LEEHEE EXPRESS product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060)).

### Inferences
- **YORU** and the 2025 **Extreme** project may be related releases under the WOOZIC STARS name, but the different title and missing project-to-SKU reference mean they should be treated as separate until directly linked by the creator or publisher ([Fanding creator profile](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/); [LEEHEE EXPRESS product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060)).

### Gaps
- I found no campaign page or campaign reward table that names the exact **YORU** product. The Fanding profile’s visible post list does not resolve the SKU/campaign mapping ([Fanding creator profile](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/)).
- I found no sufficiently verified additional campaign in this pass beyond the already-imported Nxde record and the related but unmatched Extreme post; this is a scoped search result, not proof that no other campaign exists.

## What can be concluded about creator credit and R2 image rights?

### Takeaway
The product is suitable as a research lead with a likely Kim Woohyeon model credit, subject to direct identity confirmation. The retail listing does not grant reuse rights for its cover or interior images, so it does not support an R2 upload.

### Cited Findings
- The LEEHEE EXPRESS product names the model only as “Woo” and names other book contributors separately ([product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060)).
- Kim Woohyeon’s Fanding page labels her as a model and lists a WOOZIC STARZ paper-photobook project ([creator profile](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/)).
- The listing offers the book for sale but does not display an image-reuse license or permission to redistribute cover/interior assets ([LEEHEE EXPRESS product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060)).

### Inferences
- If the creator/publisher confirms that “Woo” is Kim Woohyeon and that **YORU** is a released photo book featuring her, the book can be catalogued as a physical photobook with the observed contributor credits. The present evidence supports a provisional lead, not a fully resolved creator identity or campaign relation.
- Public product images and purchase availability do not establish permission to reproduce or host the assets; leave R2 empty pending permission for the exact image.

### Gaps
- No asset-specific cover or interior reuse license was found. No image was downloaded, copied, imported, or uploaded to R2.
- Exact release date, ISBN, imprint, verified pagination, and direct campaign linkage remain unknown ([LEEHEE EXPRESS product page](https://en.leeheeexpress.com/shop_view/2060); [Fanding creator profile](https://fanding.kr/en/@woohye0n/)).
