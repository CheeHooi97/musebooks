# Creator-Owned Storefront Photobooks

## Verified product candidates

### Takeaway
Song Joo A’s creator link page points to two digital cosplay photobooks sold under the Gumroad profile name “송주아 (Song Joo A)”: *Chainsaw Man: MAKIMA* and *NIKKE: MIHARA*. Both have exact product pages and explicit downloadable photo-book formats; the creator page links them directly, providing strong evidence that the storefront is hers. These are photo-based cosplay books rather than ordinary lifestyle portraits.

### Cited Findings
- The Linktree account headed “Jooasong Official” lists the Song Joo A YouTube account and directly links to the *ASUNA*, *MAKIMA*, *EVE*, and *MIHARA* photobook listings on `appster50.gumroad.com`. This is first-party cross-link evidence for the seller/product relationship. [Jooasong Official Linktree](https://linktr.ee/Jooasong)
- The exact Gumroad listing *MAKIMA PHOTOBOOK* identifies the seller as “송주아 (Song Joo A),” costs $20, describes three *Chainsaw Man* concepts, and specifies 60 pages of high-resolution JPEGs (560 MB). [MAKIMA PHOTOBOOK](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/makimajooa)
- The exact Gumroad listing *MIHARA PHOTOBOOK* identifies the seller as “송주아 (Song Joo A),” uses a “name a fair price” model starting at $25, and describes an approximately 70-page A4 digital cosplay photobook with Maid, Bonding & Chain, and Pain Eater concepts, delivered as high-resolution photography. [MIHARA PHOTOBOOK](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/miharaphotobook)
- A Korean gaming publication interviewed model 송주아, identified her as a racing model, and listed Instagram handle `@ww0205ww`; this supports matching the seller’s name to a public-facing Korean-model identity, although it is not a primary source. [Digital Post / PC Love profile](https://www.ilovepc.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=21711)
- The creator link page also links to *ASUNA PHOTOBOOK* and *EVE PHOTOBOOK*, but search/open results did not return reliable product descriptions, prices, contents, or page counts for those exact listings. Treat them as additional first-party leads only. [Jooasong Official Linktree](https://linktr.ee/Jooasong)

### Inferences
- **Creator identity confidence: high** for the association between the Gumroad seller and the creator identity “Jooasong / Song Joo A”: her link-in-bio account links to the exact seller’s photobook products and her YouTube, while the Korean interview corroborates the real-world name and matching Instagram identity.
- **Korean creator classification: medium-high; nationality not independently verified.** The Korean publication documents her Korean racing-model work and Korean-market activity. I am not treating Korean name or language alone as proof of citizenship.
- These two titles are distinct works and can be cataloged as digital editions if the project’s inclusion rule accepts cosplay photobooks.

### Gaps
- No publication date, ISBN, independent edition data, or publisher was found for either Gumroad work.
- The pages do not provide a product-specific license granting republication, commercial use, or third-party R2 hosting. Gumroad sale/download access is not permission to copy the image files into R2; do not upload without asset-specific authorization.
- No evidence was found tying the *ASUNA* or *EVE* products to readable product metadata; do not import those titles from link labels alone.

## Additional storefront lead held for verification

### Takeaway
Streamer/lookbook creator 겨우디 (WinterD) has a CTEE storefront advertising a newly released web pictorial at USD 45.63. The creator identity and storefront association are credible, but the indexed page is a store listing without enough title/content/date details for a database record, and the sources checked do not independently establish nationality.

### Cited Findings
- CTEE’s creator store labels the offering “룩북 유튜버 겸 스트리머 겨우디의 NEW 웹화보 발매 !!,” displays USD 45.63, and credits the storefront creator as `겨우디 @winterd`. The store also says products are sold and managed directly by the creator. [겨우디 CTEE store](https://ctee.kr/place/winterd/store?group=8856)
- WinterD’s YouTube video description links to her Instagram `winter_28270`, Patreon `winterd`, and SOOP channel; a Korean agency profile identifies her as Kyul (겨우디) and records her broadcasting history beginning on AfreecaTV. These provide cross-platform identity corroboration. [WinterD YouTube channel video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fbkgGK96ReM), [agency profile](https://www.npe.agency/kyul%EA%B2%A8%EC%9A%B0%EB%94%94)

### Inferences
- **Identity confidence: high** for the CTEE store and creator handle being associated with WinterD, based on CTEE’s creator attribution and the cross-linked creator accounts.
- **Korean creator/nationality confidence: unresolved.** AfreecaTV and Korean-language activity indicate Korean-market presence but, by themselves, do not prove nationality. Keep this as a research lead rather than a confirmed Korean-person record until an appropriate source clarifies it.
- **Product confidence: medium.** The storefront establishes a web-pictorial listing and visible price but not a stable item-specific title or detailed contents.

### Gaps
- The store page did not expose an item-specific URL or product details such as image count, file size, release date, or license.
- No image reuse terms were found. No media was downloaded or copied; purchase would not by itself grant R2 republication rights.

## Import and image-rights decision

### Takeaway
The Song Joo A titles are well-sourced candidates for metadata import, subject to the catalog’s cosplay-book inclusion policy. The 겨우디 CTEE offer remains a lead. No image assets are cleared for R2.

### Cited Findings
- The product pages specify digital image downloads and describe page counts/contents, while the creator’s link page directly links to the books. [MAKIMA listing](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/makimajooa), [MIHARA listing](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/miharaphotobook), [Jooasong Linktree](https://linktr.ee/Jooasong)
- The exact product pages contain no explicit license authorizing reuse or third-party hosting; the storefront references are not a rights grant. [MAKIMA listing](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/makimajooa), [MIHARA listing](https://appster50.gumroad.com/l/miharaphotobook)

### Inferences
- Catalog bibliographic metadata only; leave release date and publisher blank unless further source evidence appears.
- Do not fetch, upload, or mirror covers/interior images to R2 without a license or written permission covering the exact assets and use.

### Gaps
- Confirm whether cosplay photobooks meet the project’s definition of influencer photobooks before importing.
- Establish asset-specific R2 reuse permission for any cover or interior image.
- Confirm Korean nationality only if the database requires nationality; no nationality was inferred here.
