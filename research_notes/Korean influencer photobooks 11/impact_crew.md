### Inferences

- The retailers’ photo/portrait-photography classifications, physical-book labels, and model-named titles support treating these as photo books rather than general magazine mentions. They are metadata candidates, though bibliographic fields should be stored only where the specific edition source supports them.
- Do not assign the book’s editorship/authorship to the featured model: retailer records credit the Impact Crew editorial department as publisher/editor.
- Lee So-young and UNEED have multiple date/title variants across catalogs; avoid deduplicating or storing a normalized release date until ISBN, barcode, or publisher confirmation links the records.
- The 977-prefixed UNEED code is likely a product/serial-style barcode field, not a valid ISBN-13; leave the database ISBN field blank unless an ISBN registry or publisher source confirms another identifier.

### Gaps

- The precise relation among Lee So-young’s 2019-08 and 2019-12 listings is unresolved; no validated ISBN or definitive physical size was found for the Kyobo December record.
- Kim Min-ji’s physical dimensions conflict (Youngpoong generic; YES24 used record 255 × 265 mm; Aladin used record 225 × 265 mm). Do not choose one without an exact new-edition publisher record.
- UNEED’s Aug/Sep/Oct/Nov catalog dates are inconsistent; a precise publication/fulfillment date and any real ISBN remain unverified.
- The accessible Kyobo search page links to detail IDs, but some individual Kyobo pages did not expose full bibliographic tables in the web capture. No exact page/size data was recovered there for Kim Min-ji, Min Hanna, Jung Hyeyul, or UNEED.

## Does evidence verify each named subject as a model or influencer?

### Takeaway
Five of the six have reliable press or publisher evidence tying the book-name to a public model/creator identity. For Kim Min-ji, the book and official production video verify a named subject, but a reliable independent identity/profile match was not established; keep that person entity tentative.

### Cited Findings

- **Lee So-young.** Sports Seoul identifies Lee So-young as a model and says an Impact Crew photo book titled *Impact* had recently been released featuring her; a separate follow-up report says her Impact photo book sold out. ([Sports Seoul via Daum, 2018-10-26](https://v.daum.net/v/gx3sF2HlqA); [Sports Seoul via Twig24, 2018-12-17](https://www.twig24.com/news/entertainments/2018/12/17/20181217600001)). This confirms a model named Lee So-young participated in an Impact Crew book, but it does not prove that the 2018 book and later Kyobo December 2019 record are the same edition/person.
- **Kim Min-ji.** Impact Crew’s own Vimeo On Demand page lists **“[HOT] 김민지 IMPACT 화보집 영상”** among the publisher’s official photo-book videos. ([Impact Crew official Vimeo VOD page](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages)). This ties the product title to a publisher-produced subject named Kim Min-ji but does not independently distinguish her from other people with that common name or establish influencer/model credentials.
- **Min Hanna.** A Sports Seoul report states that Min Hanna released the solo *민한나 임팩트화보집* produced and published by Impact Crew, calls her a power influencer with a reported 260,000 followers, and describes her as a racing model and Double G FC ring girl. ([Sports Seoul report via Daum, 2019-10-26](https://v.daum.net/v/20191026112710609?s=print_news)). Impact Crew’s official Vimeo page separately lists her Impact photo-book video. ([Impact Crew official Vimeo VOD page](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages)).
- **Jeong Yuna.** Xports News describes a press call for the *Impact Crew Jeong Yuna IMPACT photo book* and identifies Jeong Yuna as a model attending the event. ([Xports News](https://www.xportsnews.com/article/1162930)). Ilgan Sports explicitly calls her an influencer and model and says she appeared at the Impact Crew photo-book publication showcase. ([Ilgan Sports](https://isplus.com/article/view/isp201908310012)). Impact Crew’s Vimeo page labels her making video “정유나(ULAEL) IMPACT 화보집,” which corroborates the alias/product connection. ([Impact Crew official Vimeo VOD page](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages)).
- **Jung Hyeyul.** Sports Seoul’s profile calls Jung Hyeyul an “SNS fairy,” describes her modeling career, and states she had released photo books; a separate contemporaneous news item says Impact Crew produced her named title, *임팩트화보집 정혜율*. ([Sports Seoul via Daum, 2020-03-23](https://v.daum.net/v/20200323123309765); [Halla Ilbo archive entry](https://s1.ihalla.com/section.php?page=10&sid=221&total_record=1161)). Impact Crew’s official Vimeo VOD list includes her IMPACT photo-book making video. ([Impact Crew official Vimeo VOD page](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages)).
- **UNEED.** MoneyS identifies model UNEED as an influencer with about 100,000 social followers, a model and CEO who operates her own underwear brand, and reports an Impact Crew photo shoot. ([MoneyS, 2019-03-16](https://www.moneys.co.kr/article/2019031215108075508)). Star Daily News later identifies UNEED as a fitness model, creator, influencer with about 130,000 followers and agency CEO. ([Star Daily News via Nate, 2024-10-23](https://news.nate.com/view/20241023n03412)). Impact Crew’s Vimeo page lists two UNEED IMPACT video parts. ([Impact Crew official Vimeo VOD page](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages)).

### Inferences

- The sources support featured-model credits for Lee So-young, Min Hanna, Jeong Yuna/ULAEL, Jung Hyeyul, and UNEED, while supporting an influencer label most explicitly for Min Hanna, Jeong Yuna, and UNEED.
- For Kim Min-ji, keep the book record separate from a confidently resolved person record until a first-party profile or reliable press source distinguishes the intended Kim Min-ji.
- A model’s presence in an Impact Crew book does not establish authorship or photography credit.

### Gaps

- No independent identity trail or creator profile was found for Kim Min-ji beyond the matching product and Impact Crew’s title-specific production video.
- The evidence establishes public professional activity, not necessarily present-day social handles/legal names for Lee So-young, Min Hanna, Jeong Yuna, or Jung Hyeyul. Do not infer legal identity or merge same-name people without an explicit account/source match.
- Lee’s 2018 Impact Crew book mention may relate to a prior anthology; the exact connection to the late-2019 Kyobo book remains unresolved.

## Do any sources grant permission for cover or interior images in R2?

### Takeaway
No asset-specific R2 reuse permission was found for any of the six books. Keep the records metadata-only and do not copy covers, page previews, or production footage into R2.

### Cited Findings

- Retail product records provide commercial book metadata and/or display cover art, but none of the cited YES24, Kyobo, Youngpoong, or Aladin pages grants a license to reproduce a cover or interior image. See the exact product pages linked above.
- Impact Crew’s Vimeo page offers publisher-produced video/VOD entries, but the page does not grant a license to extract frames or redistribute still images to a third-party R2 bucket. ([Impact Crew official Vimeo VOD page](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages)).
- Press articles identify source photography as Impact Crew/model material and do not grant downstream image-hosting rights. ([Sports Seoul/Daum report on Min Hanna](https://v.daum.net/v/20191026112710609?s=print_news); [Xports News report on Jeong Yuna](https://www.xportsnews.com/article/1162930)).

### Inferences

- Retail display and public videos are evidence that the books exist, not a license to mirror their commercial imagery.
- Do not upload any cover, preview, or video-derived still to R2 without written permission that covers the exact image, hosting, and intended public display.

### Gaps

- No photographer/model/publisher permission or license terms covering the exact covers/interior assets and R2 hosting were located; no media was downloaded or uploaded.
