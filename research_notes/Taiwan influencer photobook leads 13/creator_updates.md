# Taiwan creator update — round 13

## 蘋蘋澎澎: six earlier books and one extra confirmed

The creator's [official website](https://pinpinponpon.tw/) identifies 蘋蘋澎澎 as a Taiwan-based twins creator, reports 1.2 million-plus total followers, and presents a Photobooks archive of eight main books and one extra volume. The page names Vol.1–Vol.6 and displays cover images; it also says some early titles have sold out. The exact product details for older volumes are sparse, so this import leaves their ISBNs, page counts, and release dates blank.

MuseBooks already contained the creator's 2026 `純·欲姊姊特休中` physical single-book edition. After duplicate checks, this round added the first title, Vol.2–Vol.6, and `家有雙生女友` as the extra. All seven R2 covers were retrieved through the live MuseBooks API and SHA-256 matched to their object keys.

The homepage also promotes `澎然心動的蘋率30赫茲` as new and links to an official preorder page. The listing describes a 128-page book and high-resolution digital-file add-on. Its final cover has not been verified, and the add-on does not establish a separately sold ebook edition, so both remain held. ([official preorder](https://pinpinponpon627.waca.tw/product/detail/2706145))
