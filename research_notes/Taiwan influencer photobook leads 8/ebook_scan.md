# Taiwan digital photobook retailer scan

## Newly surfaced digital photobook candidate

### Takeaway
ADAM 林冠宇’s crowdfunding page describes 《FOUR》 as his first all-digital photobook, but the campaign says the encrypted PDF will be delivered after production in December 2026. Treat this as an upcoming lead, not an import-ready edition: I found no ISBN, page count, final cover asset, or live MuseBooks record.

### Cited Findings
- The project page names the product “ADAM2026電子寫真書《FOUR》,” says it is the creator’s first digital photobook, and offers a pure-digital tier; it identifies its proposer as Adam 冠宇 and gives the campaign period as 2026-10-12 through 2026-11-12. [嘖嘖 project page](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026)
- The FAQ says the deliverable is an encrypted PDF, delivery information will be provided after the campaign and production, and the project expects to fulfill in December 2026. It does not provide an ISBN or page count. [嘖嘖 FAQ](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026/faqs)
- The project bio calls Adam 林冠宇 a Taiwan emerging actor and links his Facebook and Instagram accounts; it describes his earlier work as actor/model/trainer and his three prior books. [嘖嘖 project page](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026)
- The campaign notes that displayed imagery and physical extras are illustrative and can differ from final production; I did not find a separately identified final digital cover. [嘖嘖 project page](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026)

### Inferences
- This is a credible Taiwan-based creator lead, although the campaign’s own bio presents him primarily as an actor rather than explicitly as an influencer.
- Do not import yet: the project is still in crowdfunding, the promised file is not delivered, and no verified final cover is identified. “ADAM2026” is the campaign slug, not an ISBN or ebook product ID.

### Gaps
- ISBN, publisher imprint, page count, digital cover, final edition metadata, and delivered-file confirmation are not available on the reviewed campaign/FAQ pages.
- A local catalog scan and live MuseBooks queries for “ADAM,” “林冠宇,” and “FOUR” returned no matches, but the work’s release state and cover evidence remain insufficient for an import.

## Retailer hits already represented in the catalog

### Takeaway
The latest Taiwan-facing ebook-store sweep surfaced viable digital photobooks by 寶兒, 瑟七, and 斐棋, but the relevant digital editions and covers are already represented in MuseBooks; these are duplicate checks, not additions.

### Cited Findings
- Readmoo lists 《Babe Fantasy‧寶兒寫真書（加贈多張未公開照片）》 as a fixed-layout EPUB, with publication date 2025-12-09 and online date 2026-02-04; it labels **9786267683798** as ISBN and **9786267683781** as EISBN. Kobo Taiwan uses **9786267683781** as its ebook Book ID. These identifiers should remain format-distinguished. [Readmoo product](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=150278); [Kobo Taiwan ebook](https://www.kobo.com/tw/zh/ebook/babe-fantasy)
- The live MuseBooks record for 寶兒 already contains a physical edition (ISBN 9786267683798) and digital edition (ISBN/EISBN 9786267683781), each with a stored cover. [MuseBooks work](https://musebooks.my/books/work-tw-6bfdeef40b49b66040c85d2e)
- Pubu lists 《微澀．微瑟》瑟七 Seul7 數位寫真（含影音）, ISBN 9786264555029, 294 pages, fixed-layout EPUB, released 2026-08-30; its description identifies her as a Tainan-born creator with a YouTube channel and Instagram following. [Pubu product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/690306)
- MuseBooks already holds 瑟七’s 《微澀．微瑟》 digital edition with ISBN 9786264555029 and an R2 cover, alongside other digital/physical 瑟七 editions. [MuseBooks 瑟七 query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E7%91%9F%E4%B8%83)
- Sanmin lists 《一棋一會：斐棋1st數位寫真》 as an EPUB edition, ISBN/EISBN 9786264343435, dated 2025-11-24; Eslite gives 268 pages and calls it an ebook, while the NCL deposit list records the ebook alongside the separate paperback ISBN 9786264343466. [Sanmin ebook](https://www.sanmin.com.tw/product/index/015061665); [Eslite ebook](https://www.eslite.com/product/10072302132683046912001); [NCL deposit list](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1755143472.pdf)
- MuseBooks already has both the digital edition and paperback for 斐棋’s 《一棋一會》, with the digital ISBN 9786264343435 and physical ISBN 9786264343466. [MuseBooks work](https://musebooks.my/books/work-tw-7dcb82ca95b6bee6bd8ffc3a)

### Inferences
- Storefront discovery alone would have duplicated three existing Taiwanese digital editions; direct live-record checks are necessary before importing newly surfaced titles.
- Sanmin/Eslite/NCL align on the ebook identifier for 斐棋, while the physical book has its own ISBN; these are distinct format-specific identifiers, not one product ID.

### Gaps
- No new import-ready ebook emerged from the reviewed retailer queries; the only unlisted lead, 《FOUR》, lacks a final cover and is not yet delivered.
- Product descriptions and cover assets for other search-result hits were not independently reviewed when they were already identifiable as existing catalog titles.

## Search scope and disposition

### Takeaway
This pass checked Taiwan-facing Kobo, Pubu, Readmoo, TAAZE, Sanmin, Eslite, NCL, and Zeczec leads against the Taiwan photobook catalog and live MuseBooks records. It found one promising but premature crowdfunding project and no new digital edition ready for a verified DB/R2 import.

### Cited Findings
- Pubu’s listing confirms the digital file format, identifier, date, page count and creator context for the already-cataloged 瑟七 title. [Pubu product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/690306)
- Zeczec’s project and FAQ establish the separate forthcoming digital-only 《FOUR》 product, delivery method and expected production timeline. [Project page](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026); [FAQ](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026/faqs)

### Inferences
- The current useful follow-up is to revisit 《FOUR》 after its campaign completes and a final file/cover or creator-issued edition details are published.

### Gaps
- The live retailer sweep did not yield a new, non-duplicate, already-published Taiwan creator ebook with adequate product and final-cover evidence.
