# Round 20 — Creator-store and crowdfunding sweep

## How did the held campaign leads change?

### Takeaway

The Round 19 holds remain holds: KANA, 小貓奈奈, and Office State Of Mind are still preorders with future fulfillment; Huan’s old campaign has no current fulfillment proof located in this sweep. ADAM 林冠宇’s 《FOUR》 remains a future digital PDF reward, and Q尼’s listing still does not identify a stable book title or final cover. No held lead cleared all product, origin, cover, fulfillment, and duplicate checks.

### Cited Findings

- Zi:zoo Taipei’s KANA 2026 fundraiser is live from 2026-09-23 through 2026-11-16. The NT$1,600+ reward tiers include one signed KANA2026 photobook; the Q&A says rewards are expected for pickup at the birthday event, or to be distributed when production finishes if delayed. The page gives no pagination or book-specific cover. [KANA fundraiser](https://zizootaipei.oen.tw/)
- KOLR’s archive of 小貓奈奈’s October 12 post describes a 96-page 2027 calendar photobook for NT$1,000 (NT$1,500 with a desk calendar) and links to a Google Form; her October 19 post says preorder has two weeks left. The archive shows no public product-detail page or title-specific cover. [Post archive](https://www.kolr.ai/influencer/d1953233-70a2-4042-9f92-0b05ac8cf0e4?duration=3m&lang=zh&platform=ig&postListSort=publishTime)

- Huan’s completed 2019 campaign describes a physical photo book containing the full photo set and says book rewards would ship after mid-May 2019. The page still does not establish Huan’s Taiwan origin, and I found no independent post-campaign fulfillment proof or title-specific cover in this check. [Campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/huan-first-album)
- ADAM 林冠宇’s official project still sells 《FOUR》 as a pure digital PDF reward; campaign content says access follows the campaign close and production, with projected realization in December 2026. The page’s stock dispatch estimate is within 10 business days, but no specific completed order or fulfillment announcement was found. [MyShip listing](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/general/detail/GM2508203612183)

### Inferences

- Keep KANA, 小貓奈奈, Office State Of Mind, Huan, 《FOUR》, and Q尼 on HOLD. KANA and 小貓奈奈 have stronger product details than before, but neither has a title-specific cover; Office still has future delivery and unresolved model origins; Huan still lacks origin and cover; 《FOUR》 remains an unfulfilled preorder; Q尼’s seller page does not provide enough stable bibliographic identity.
- A Taipei-based idol-group affiliation or a Taiwan-based photography studio alone is not evidence that each featured person is a Taiwan-origin creator. [Primulav biography](https://www.primulav.com/biography), [Office campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/OfficeStatOfMind)

## Did fresh creator-store or event-store titles clear the evidence bar?

### Takeaway

Two fresh Taiwan-linked physical-book leads surfaced. KANO-KANO’s 64-page birthday photobook is a real product with a past preorder/event window, but personal Taiwan origin, final cover, and fulfillment remain unverified. 四月桐’s 2026 store listing confirms a photo book but supplies only promotional/portrait images and an undated shipping-delay window. Neither is GO-ready.

### Cited Findings

- Zi:zoo Taipei’s official KKTIX page lists “KANO2026生誕寫真集” at NT$1,300 and 64 pages, with preorder cutoff April 20, 2026 and an in-person event on May 9. Its attached merchandise image labels the photobook mockup “監修中” (still under review), not a final cover. The event has passed, but the page does not confirm delivery. [KKTIX event](https://zizootaipei.kktix.cc/events/kano2026?locale=zh-TW), [book/merch image](https://assets.kktix.io/organization_resource_files/31994/76223/4.png)

The material retrieved does not itself establish KANO’s personal Taiwan origin; I did not treat group affiliation as origin evidence. [Primulav biography](https://www.primulav.com/biography)
- The direct 7-Eleven MyShip listing titled “【四月桐April】2026寫真系列預購” describes a physical photo book with 12 outfits and a separate photo calendar; the listed photo-book option is NT$500 (shown against NT$590). The seller first estimated shipping from mid-December to mid-January, then says printing delay moved dispatch to late January/early February without stating the years. [MyShip product listing](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/general/detail/GM2512029367180)
- The Four Moon listing’s main image is a collage banner reading “四月桐2026寫真書、桌曆”; its specific product image is a portrait without book-cover typography or a depicted finished book. I inspected both seller-hosted images; neither demonstrates a title-specific final cover. [Banner image](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/i/cgdm/GM2512029367180/GM2512029367180_1833102.jpg), [product image](https://myship.7-11.com.tw/i/cgdm/GM2512029367180/2512020922987203.jpg)

[TTShow profile](https://www.ttshow.tw/article/54062), [HK01 profile](https://www.hk01.com/%E7%86%B1%E7%88%86%E8%A9%B1%E9%A1%8C/469061/%E5%8F%B0%E7%BE%8E%E5%A5%B3%E7%A5%9E%E9%82%84%E5%8E%9F%E4%BA%BA%E9%80%A0%E4%BA%BA18%E8%99%9F%E7%88%86%E7%B4%85-%E8%AA%A4%E9%96%8B%E5%89%8D%E7%BD%AE%E9%8F%A1%E8%87%AA%E6%8B%8D-%E7%AB%9F%E8%AE%8A%E9%82%AA%E6%83%A1%E8%A6%96%E8%A7%92%E7%85%A7)
