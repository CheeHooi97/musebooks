# Direct Taiwan Digital Photobook Listings — Round 23

## Did this round find a safe new Taiwan influencer ebook to import?

### Takeaway

**No GO.** Adam Lin Guan-Yu’s *FOUR* is a credible new Taiwan creator digital-photobook project, but it is still crowdfunding, its PDF is scheduled for later delivery, and the campaign says the displayed images and physical extras are mockups rather than final output. His campaign identifies him as an actor, model, and fitness trainer; it does not specifically establish that he is an influencer. Keep the lead on hold until a finished product and cover are available and creator-scope is resolved.

### Cited Findings

- The creator-led Zeczec campaign calls *FOUR* Adam Lin Guan-Yu’s first digital photobook and fourth book; it says the ebook contains photos only. The live campaign runs October 12–November 12, 2026, and its “看見 FOUR” tier offers the ebook alone. [Official Zeczec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026)
- The campaign identifies Adam as a Taiwan actor, model, and fitness trainer, gives his Instagram handle `adamlin_linadam`, and describes his previous three books. It does not label him an influencer. [Official Zeczec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026)
- The campaign states that the images and physical rewards shown are examples, not final shipment versions. The ebook’s final images and design are not disclosed in advance. [Official Zeczec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026)
- The project FAQ says the product will be an encrypted PDF, delivered after the campaign ends and production is completed; the exact delivery time depends on production updates. The campaign estimates December 2026 realization. [Official Zeczec FAQ](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026/faqs), [campaign delivery tiers](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026)
- Live MuseBooks API search for `FOUR` returned two unrelated Korean photobooks, and search for `林冠宇` returned zero results. [FOUR API query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=FOUR&pageSize=50), [林冠宇 API query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E6%9E%97%E5%86%A0%E5%AE%87&pageSize=50)

### Inferences

- **HOLD — *FOUR*.** The direct campaign verifies a genuine, purchasable digital-photo-book reward and Taiwan creator identity, but the ebook is not yet delivered or publicly available as a finished edition. The campaign itself says its shown images are not final, so there is no verified final cover asset to mirror to R2 or attach to a database record. [Official Zeczec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026), [official FAQ](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026/faqs)

### Gaps

- Wait for the campaign’s completed encrypted PDF and final cover/edition artwork; confirm its release/delivery and capture any edition metadata such as page count or identifier if supplied. A primary profile describing Adam as an influencer would also resolve the narrow catalog-scope question. [Official Zeczec campaign](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026), [official FAQ](https://www.zeczec.com/projects/ADAM2026/faqs)
