# Taiwan influencer photobook creator-origin audit

## Missing qualifying creator photobooks

### Takeaway
Two digitally published JTWINS (夏恩、夏得) photobooks look like the strongest absent additions: both authors publicly identify as Taiwan-based creators/actors, and each has an independently verifiable EPUB ISBN. 《韓你菲》 by 李恩菲／菲菲 is also a confirmed new physical photobook, but its product-level bibliographic details and usable cover source were not found, so it remains a research lead rather than an import-ready record.

### Cited Findings
- Neither `scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md` nor `scraper/catalog-import-report.md` through batch 121 contains JTWINS, 夏恩夏得, BROTHER, or BROMANCE; the catalog/report do already cover other Taiwan male creators such as SHOYA, 毛祁生, S.T曾奕翔, and Troy, so those are not new candidates in this audit.
- JTWINS describe themselves as twin actors on their [official YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCtEhTM2zYxudFN6DBvIf2XA); an interview quotes 夏恩 saying he is from Taiwan and identifies 夏得 as his twin brother, while both studied at Hsing Wu University and acted in the same Taiwanese drama, [ManGu interview PDF](https://www.atmangu.com/uploads/article/zwh4MAjsWCuweHmlCIIBdprehgeGuFYC.pdf). A separate [Mirror Media profile](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20181012ent004) reports that the brothers entered the Taiwan entertainment industry after working as gym consultants.
- Taiwan's National Central Library ISBN deposit records identify two distinct Taiwan KADOKAWA EPUB photobooks, 《JTWINS夏恩夏得數位寫真.1 Brother》, EISBN 9786263780866, and 《JTWINS夏恩夏得數位寫真.2 Bromance》, EISBN 9786263780873, both deposited in 2023-10: [NCL deposit list](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1697771178.pdf).
- The first EPUB's [Readmoo record](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=135375) lists the title, EISBN 9786263780866, Taiwan KADOKAWA, EPUB fixed layout, and release date 2023-10-20. A [PChome Pubu-store listing](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBS65-D900HY8M5) describes it as a digital, non-paper product and shows NT$377 against a NT$490 list price; this is a marketplace snapshot, not a confirmed current Pubu offer.
- The second EPUB's [Eslite listing](https://www.eslite.com/product/10072302132683100269003) gives the author, Taiwan KADOKAWA, 2023-10-19, 133 pages, EPUB fixed layout, and EAN 9786263780873 (the EISBN rather than an ISBN-13 field). The [PChome/HyRead listing](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBR5E-D900IOXBZ) confirms digital delivery and the same identifier. Pubu's [2026 bundle page](https://www.pubu.com.tw/combo/668074) offers both numbered books together for NT$980 and names both component titles; its 2026-05-11 date is the bundle listing date, not the books' original publication date.
- The books are a creator-duo photobook pair rather than a drama-cast commemorative book. Individual page count for volume 1, individual current Pubu prices, and exact cover-image source URLs remain unverified. No physical editions were found in the cited records.
- 民視 reports that cheerleader 菲菲／李恩菲 announced her first solo physical photobook 《韓你菲》, photographed in Busan, with sale planned for 2026-09-02 at 20:00; the article directs readers to her official social accounts for sales details: [FTV report](https://finance.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260). UDN independently repeats the physical format, title, and announced sale date: [UDN report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294). The existing catalog/report already contains her prior digital work 《菲你莫屬》, but neither document lists 《韓你菲》.

### Inferences
- JTWINS should be reviewed for two separate digital work/edition records keyed by the two verified EISBNs; the Pubu bundle itself should not become a third photobook record. This follows from the deposit list and the bundle's explicit two-component list ([NCL](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1697771178.pdf), [Pubu bundle](https://www.pubu.com.tw/combo/668074)).
- JTWINS meet the Taiwan-creator scope through the creator's own origin statement, Taiwan-based acting career, and Taiwan-published editions; inclusion does not depend on the Pubu bundle alone ([ManGu interview](https://www.atmangu.com/uploads/article/zwh4MAjsWCuweHmlCIIBdprehgeGuFYC.pdf), [NCL deposit list](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1697771178.pdf)).
- 《韓你菲》 appears eligible as a new Taiwan cheerleader's individually centered photobook, but the news items are launch announcements and do not establish ISBN, publisher, pagination, dimensions, price, or an exact cover asset ([FTV](https://finance.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260), [UDN](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294)).

### Gaps
- JTWINS volume 1 page count, individual live Pubu offer/price, exact cover URLs, and confirmation of any print editions were not established by the sources retrieved.
- For 《韓你菲》, no official seller/product page or order link, ISBN, publisher/imprint, page count, dimensions, price, release/fulfilment detail beyond the announced 2026-09-02 sale opening, or verified cover image was found. The media image credits identify a press-supplied photo, not the book cover. Do not infer those fields from the news announcement.

## Origin checks, exclusions, and scope limits

### Takeaway
The unresolved Pubu male-model leads remain unsupported as Taiwan creators on the evidence reviewed. Mainland-connected promotion or China-origin clues are not enough to qualify; the Taiwan-market ebook listing by itself does not prove creator origin. The drama-photo results reviewed are ensemble publications and do not center an individual Taiwanese influencer, so they do not fit this assignment's individual-creator target.

### Cited Findings
- 彭果 was not matched to a Taiwan affiliation; the prior research record cites a [Hunan University of Chinese Medicine page](https://tyxy.hncu.edu.cn/info/2017/6437.htm) describing a student selected for China's national bodybuilding team. Because a same-name identity match to the Pubu model has not been independently established, treat this as an exclusion lead rather than definitive identity proof.
- Devil-Lee's promoter account advertises the 《Devillee》 photo series and cross-posts its Pubu release, but the [Sina/Weibo mirror](https://www.sina.cn/media/1212848862) provides no reliable Taiwan origin or current Taiwan residence for the model. Logan奶狼宇's [Xiaohongshu-related reference](https://www.ktyrw.com/jirou/2025/0420/2658.html) likewise did not establish Taiwan origin or base. Both remain unqualified pending direct evidence; their availability on Pubu is not origin evidence.
- The batch-121 report already records 夏勝傑 as described as from Hangzhou and Gabriel as described as from Shanghai; the catalog/report do not show new Taiwan affiliation for either, so neither should be added as a Taiwan creator. Relevant trail: [Pubu male-photo category](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook-category/male-photo-books-402), [Devil-Lee promoter posts](https://www.sina.cn/media/1212848862), and the prior [Logan reference](https://www.ktyrw.com/jirou/2025/0420/2658.html).
- Pubu's [male-photo category](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook-category/male-photo-books-402) lists 《初三的六一兒童節 戲劇寫真書》 as a 132-page fixed-layout EPUB, EISBN 9786264680035, dated 2026-08-14, NT$450, published by Taiwan Kadokawa; its listing describes selected drama stills, behind-the-scenes photographs, and the ensemble cast 何昶希、何衍朝等, rather than an individual Taiwan influencer.
- Pubu's ensemble listings include 《向流星許願的我們 戲劇寫真書》 (2026-06-12, fixed-layout EPUB, EISBN 9786264456593, 148 pages, NT$360; [product page](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/673736)), 《靈魂約定 戲劇寫真書》 (2025-08-15, fixed-layout EPUB, EISBN 9786264350754, 155 pages, NT$360; [product page](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/605898)), and 《印象・青春 戲劇寫真書》 (2025-03-21, fixed-layout EPUB, EISBN 9786264153638, 133 pages; [product page](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/495548)). Their product descriptions identify drama/CP photographs and multiple cast members, not an individual's creator photobook.
- The Taiwan-market title 《某某 戲劇寫真書 The On1y One Photobook》 is a digital ensemble product with 300-plus season stills and behind-the-scenes photos, not an individual influencer's photobook: [PChome/Pubu listing](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBS65-D900I4CQJ).

### Inferences
- Keep 彭果, Devil-Lee, Logan奶狼宇, 夏勝傑, and Gabriel out of a Taiwan-influencer import batch until reliable Taiwan origin/current-base evidence or an explicit individual Taiwan creator affiliation is found; Pubu distribution alone does not resolve the subject's origin ([Hunan University](https://tyxy.hncu.edu.cn/info/2017/6437.htm), [Sina promoter timeline](https://www.sina.cn/media/1212848862), [Xiaohongshu-related reference](https://www.ktyrw.com/jirou/2025/0420/2658.html)).
- Exclude the listed ensemble drama photo books from a creator-person photobook batch unless a source establishes that a specific Taiwanese influencer is individually featured as the book's subject; ensemble casts and group CP photos do not establish that scope ([Pubu category](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook-category/dramas-107), [《向流星許願的我們》](https://www.pubu.com.tw/ebook/673736), [《某某》](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBS65-D900I4CQJ)).

### Gaps
- The exact identity of 彭果 relative to the university biography has not been proven. No primary evidence of Taiwan residence, nationality, or professional affiliation was found for Devil-Lee or Logan奶狼宇. No qualifying Taiwan affiliation was established for the Hangzhou/Shanghai leads.
- The Pubu drama category is broad and changes over time; this pass checked representative current titles rather than establishing an exhaustive archive of every drama-cast ebook. No specific cast title in this pass met the individually centered Taiwan-influencer criterion.
