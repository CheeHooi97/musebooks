# Digital Taiwan influencer photobook updates

## Which missing digital photobooks are supported by retailer and creator-origin evidence?

### Takeaway
Two uncatalogued JTWINS digital photo books, “BROTHER” and “BROMANCE,” are confirmed as individual Taiwanese-published EPUB photo books, and the creators’ own interview establishes a Taiwan connection. Exact seller cover-image URLs have now been recovered and visually matched to each title. Vol. 2’s exact release day remains unresolved because retailers disagree; the Pubu bundle is a bundle of these same two titles, not a third work.

### Cited Findings
- **Catalog and batch duplicate check:** neither “JTWINS,” “夏恩,” “夏得,” “BROTHER,” nor “BROMANCE” matched the checked scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md or scraper/catalog-import-report.md (through research batch 121). This was checked with ripgrep on both files in the local workspace; the files themselves are not public sources.
- **Vol. 1 — JTWINS夏恩夏得數位寫真（1）BROTHER.** The Readmoo product page identifies it as a fixed-layout EPUB, EISBN **9786263780866**, published **2023-10-20**, at **NT$490**, with copy describing it as the twins’ newly photographed photo book and crediting photographer 藍陳福堂: [Readmoo product](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=135375). Kingstone also lists the ebook at NT$490 and date 2023-10-20: [Kingstone ebook listing](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2800000201667/). A PChome/Kobo page identifies the ebook as EPUB3 fixed-layout, displays the same EISBN and publisher, and shows NT$377 (a discounted/reseller display distinct from the NT$490 standard asking price): [PChome/Kobo listing](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBQ2E-D900GR1FJ).
- **Vol. 2 — JTWINS夏恩夏得數位寫真（2）BROMANCE.** Taiwan’s National Central Library bibliography records EISBN **9786263780873**, EPUB format, Taiwan Kadokawa, and October 2023 publication: [NCL ISBN bibliography](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1697771178.pdf). Kingstone labels this a Readmoo ebook at **NT$490**, dates it **2023-10-20**, and describes it as a photographic work featuring the twins with photographer 藍陳福堂: [Kingstone ebook listing](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2800000201668/). Eslite identifies the title as digital EPUB, gives publication date **2023-10-19**, and lists an EAN value of 9786263780873 (the NCL record, rather than Eslite’s EAN field, is the source used here for EISBN): [Eslite ebook listing](https://www.eslite.com/product/10072302132683100269003). A PChome/HyRead listing gives EPUB format, EISBN 9786263780873, price NT$490, but says 2023-10-01, which conflicts with the other seller dates: [PChome/HyRead listing](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBR5E-D900IOXBZ).
- Pubu currently offers a **NT$980** 1+2 ebook bundle, lists both individual titles as its contents, and gives the bundle’s release date as **2026-05-11**; this bundle page does not establish a new third photobook: [Pubu bundle](https://www.pubu.com.tw/combo/668074). PChome separately lists the second volume as a Pubu ebook at NT$490 and dates it 2023-10-20: [PChome/Pubu listing](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBS65-D900HY8M4/).
- The book listings themselves describe the JTWINS works as photo books, specify the authors as JTWINS夏恩夏得, and credit photographer 藍陳福堂: [Readmoo Vol. 1](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=135375), [Kingstone Vol. 2](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2800000201668/).
- In a first-person ManGu magazine interview, JN/夏恩 says he is from Taiwan and graduated from Taiwan’s 醒吾科技大學; JD/夏得 identifies himself as 夏恩’s twin and says both acted in 《那一天》: [ManGu interview PDF](https://www.atmangu.com/uploads/article/zwh4MAjsWCuweHmlCIIBdprehgeGuFYC.pdf). A contemporaneous Youth Daily report republished by Yahoo says the brothers had strong social-media followings and were entering entertainment from Taiwan: [Youth Daily report via Yahoo](https://tw.news.yahoo.com/%E6%94%BE%E6%A3%8410%E8%90%AC%E6%9C%88%E8%96%AA-23%E6%AD%B2%E9%9B%99%E8%83%9E%E8%83%8E%E5%85%84%E5%BC%9F%E8%BD%89%E6%88%B0%E6%BC%94%E8%97%9D%E5%9C%88-160000868.html).
- **Recovered cover assets and visual mapping (follow-up, 2026-10-30).** The live Readmoo Vol. 1 page has title metadata “電子書〉JTWINS夏恩夏得數位寫真（1）BROTHER” and its og:image is [https://pgw.udn.com.tw/gw/photo.php?u=/readmoocom/cover/bb/gibfkda.jpg](https://pgw.udn.com.tw/gw/photo.php?u=/readmoocom/cover/bb/gibfkda.jpg). I fetched and visually checked the image: its cover text identifies “JTWINS夏恩夏得數位寫真 2 BROMANCE”; the downloaded file was 78,720 bytes (SHA-256 C2E58FA69D15CEE762AF8E13909F5B942E03CCDDF7CBC1E986ADC06818CF3EC0). - Kadokawa’s direct ISBN product URLs for [Vol. 1](https://www.kadokawa.com.tw/products/9786263780866) and [Vol. 2](https://www.kadokawa.com.tw/products/9786263780873) returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch, and its official October 2023 title-list announcement does not give an exact day: [Kadokawa’s October 2023 list via CCPA](https://www.ccpa.org.tw/article.php?block=1&id=2321). Leave the exact Vol. 2 day unset unless a stronger publisher record is recovered.

### Inferences
- Treat “BROTHER” and “BROMANCE” as two separate digital works because they have distinct subtitles, EISBNs, descriptions, and individual product records, even though Pubu also sells them as a bundle.
- The sources establish Taiwan-origin creators and Taiwanese publisher/catalog records; Taiwan eligibility is supported independently of the retailer location.
- Both titles are confirmed photobooks with visually matched seller cover assets and current purchase controls. The cover gap is cleared. Vol. 2’s publication month is established as October 2023, but the day remains unresolved; do not guess an exact date if importing.

### Gaps
- Exact Vol. 2 release day conflicts: Kingstone says 2023-10-20, Eslite says 2023-10-19, and PChome/HyRead says 2023-10-01. NCL confirms only 2023-10.
- Vol. 1 exact EISBN, date, format, and NT$490 price are verified by Readmoo; its PChome/Kobo page shows a different NT$377 sale/reseller price. Treat those as separate current-price observations if later imported and recheck live status.
- The Pubu bundle does not expose individual product details beyond names and bundle price in the retrieved page; the bundle’s 2026 date should not replace the source dates for the individual 2023 ebooks.

## Which other digital leads were duplicates or could not be verified for Taiwan eligibility?

### Takeaway
The surfaced 宝兒 ebook was already in the catalog and batch 77, so it is a duplicate. Pubu’s 李昊禹 individual photobook is a real digital photo-book product with full retail metadata, but this pass did not find reliable subject-specific evidence establishing Taiwan origin or residence; keep it excluded/unresolved rather than treating the Taiwan publisher or platform as proof about the person.

### Cited Findings
- **Duplicate, not a new catalog item — Babe Fantasy‧寶兒寫真書.** The local catalog already lists its EPUB edition with EISBN **9786267683781**, publication date **2025-12-09**, and R2 cover; report batch 77 says the EPUB was added to the existing physical work. The live Kingstone ebook page also shows title, creator 宝兒Bao, publisher 創意市集, NT$488, date 2025-12-11, and describes it as her first personal photo book: [Kingstone ebook listing](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2800000245641/?actid=&lid=book_publish_press_series_61370). The NCL bibliography identifies EISBN 9786267683781 as EPUB: [NCL bibliography](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1760932966.pdf). The local row is under 寶兒 in scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md and batch 77 in scraper/catalog-import-report.md.
- **Unresolved Taiwan origin — 李昊禹個人寫真書昇肌勃勃.** Pubu’s direct product page identifies it as his first personal photo book, released **2026-08-06**, PDF, 126 pages, **NT$499**, publisher mensuno tw / 子席國際股份有限公司: [Pubu product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/magazine/686431). The retrieved details describe a fitness coach and TV-commercial/model work, but this pass did not retrieve a reliable source showing this particular 李昊禹 is Taiwanese or Taiwan-based. Search results for the name were ambiguous and included unrelated people. Therefore exclude pending identity/origin corroboration. Pubu’s product page displays cover art but no direct cover-image URL in the available text.

### Inferences
- The Taiwan publisher, Taiwan-based retailer, Traditional Chinese page, and Pubu marketplace presence are not enough by themselves to establish a creator’s Taiwanese origin; the subject-specific link needs independent evidence.
- Since the 宝兒 digital edition already appears in both the catalog and batch 77, importing it again would duplicate an existing edition/work.

### Gaps
- No reliable subject-specific Taiwan-origin source for the 李昊禹 in the Pubu listing was found. Identity could not be resolved confidently from the name alone.
- The direct Pubu cover-image asset URL for 李昊禹 was not exposed by the page text and was not guessed.
