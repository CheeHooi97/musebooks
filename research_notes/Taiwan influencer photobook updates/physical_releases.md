# Taiwan influencer physical photobooks (2025–2026)

## Candidate product verification

### Takeaway

One likely new, real physical photobook surfaced: 李恩菲（菲菲／Aviva）《韓你菲》. Two Taiwan news outlets report it as her first personal physical photobook and announce its 2026-09-02 sale, but I could not locate a publisher or seller product page with bibliographic metadata; treat it as a verified existence lead, not a fully sourced catalog record.

### Cited Findings

- 民視 reported on 2026-08-31 that 李恩菲's first personal physical photobook is titled 《韓你菲》, was photographed in Busan, and was scheduled to go on sale at 20:00 on 2026-09-02; the report says detailed sales information would be posted on her official social accounts. The accompanying photo is credited to 杰斯汀整合行銷, not identified as a book cover. [民視 report](https://finance.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260)
- 聯合報 independently reported the same title, physical format, Busan shoot, and 2026-09-02 launch time. [聯合報 report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294)
- A 2026-09-02 report describes the book as released that day, but repeats the same press-release details and does not provide a sales listing or bibliographic record. [GamerSky report](https://www.gamersky.com/news/202609/2201785.shtml)
- Neither news report provides a publisher, ISBN, page count, price, stock status, or an identified cover image. The seller route was described as official social channels rather than linked to a product-detail page. [民視 report](https://finance.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260); [聯合報 report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294)

### Inferences

- The announcement supports recording a distinct physical-work lead, but the release date should remain “announced for 2026-09-02” until an official sales or publisher record verifies the actual release.
- It may have been sold directly through the creator's social channel rather than mainstream bookstores; this is an inference from the stated sales route, not a confirmed seller identity.

### Gaps

- No publisher/seller product URL, publisher name, ISBN, page count, price, current availability, or product-cover image URL was found in indexed search results. The official profile [@aviva_1028](https://www.instagram.com/aviva_1028/) is a possible follow-up route, but it is not itself a product listing.
- The article-supplied image is not expressly labeled as the cover, so it should not be used as cover provenance. [民視 report](https://finance.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260)

## Taiwan connection and catalog deduplication

### Takeaway

The Taiwan connection is strong through 李恩菲's Taiwan professional-cheerleading work and Taiwan news coverage of the book. 《韓你菲》 is not in the current repository catalog; the existing 李恩菲 entries are older digital works and the group title 《bestie閨蜜》.

### Cited Findings

- The official Rakuten Girls roster profile identifies 李恩菲 as 菲菲 Aviva and lists her with Dragon Beauties, joining in 2025. [Rakuten Girls profile](https://cpblgirls.tw/front/4/RakutenGirlsmen/member.php?ID=Y3BibGdpcmxzJlJha3V0ZW5HaXJsc21lbg%3D%3D&PID=86)
- A 2026 Taiwan report identifies 李恩菲 as a member of the Taiwan-based Dragon Beauties and Taishin Wonders cheer teams. [民視 report](https://www.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026424W0371)
- The current catalog records 李恩菲's older digital 《菲進你心》 and 《菲你莫屬》 editions, plus physical and digital 《bestie閨蜜》 editions with 孟潔; there is no 《韓你菲》 row. [Catalog, 李恩菲 entries](../../scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md#L976)
- Recent import-report batches 115–121 do not record 《韓你菲》. [Catalog import report](../../scraper/catalog-import-report.md)

### Inferences

- This appears to be a new standalone solo physical title, rather than a duplicate of her earlier digital releases or the previously cataloged two-person group book.
- Inclusion is supported by her Taiwan work and the Taiwan-market launch announcement; this does not establish her nationality or birthplace.

### Gaps

- The sources reviewed do not establish whether the book has an ISBN or formal publisher. Do not infer either from the news agency credited for promotional photos.
- No official social launch post or post-launch order page was recovered, so current sellability cannot be verified.

## Discovery checks and other surfaced titles

### Takeaway

Exact-title searches and Taiwan bookstore/category checks surfaced several 2025–2026 titles, but catalog comparison showed them already represented. 《韓你菲》 was the only additional physical influencer title found in this pass; further discovery remains open.

### Cited Findings

- Searches of Taiwan bookstore photo-book listings and Pubu's photo-book category returned recent titles including 《微澀．微瑟》瑟七, 《芯裡都是你》王芯羽, 《Honey》林莎, and 《三倍心跳》; each already appears in the repository catalog. [Books.com photo-book category](https://www.books.com.tw/web/books_bmidm_2204/); [Pubu photo-book category](https://m.pubu.com.tw/bookbuffet/category/photo-books-399); [current catalog](../../scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md)
- The catalog already includes the 2026 physical 瑟七《微澀・微瑟》, 王芯羽《芯裡都是你》 (2025), 林莎《Honey》 (2026), and the group photobook 《三倍心跳》. [Current catalog](../../scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md)
- The recent report's batch 121 still flags drama/cast photobooks and unresolved creator-origin checks as remaining research areas. [Catalog import report](../../scraper/catalog-import-report.md)

### Inferences

- The rapid appearance of multiple 2026 releases and the catalog's extensive existing coverage indicate that exact-title deduplication against the live repository is essential before any import.
- Independent social-only sales may not be indexed by bookstore searches, which likely limits the completeness of this pass.

### Gaps

- This pass did not exhaustively review every Taiwan creator's official social pages or every drama/cast publication, so it does not establish that 《韓你菲》 is the only missing title.
- Other creator-origin leads and multi-person drama titles require separate source and subject checks before they can be treated as eligible photobooks.
