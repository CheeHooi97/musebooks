- No live database duplicate/person-identity check was run for this research assignment. Check those before importing.
- The seller’s personal-use restriction means the listing is not a basis for uploading the cover or photo files to R2. Seek permission for each exact asset before any image mirroring. ([Gumroad product page](https://pinkribbon.gumroad.com/l/seul003))

### Takeaway

### Inferences

- The CPB profile supports Ahrin’s creator eligibility, but not product completion. The MAXIM article supports Park Min-jung’s creator status, but not a discrete photobook product.

### Gaps

- No completed CPB mint/sale record, release date, formal product title, ISBN, or page count was verified. Do not import on the current evidence. ([Official CPB site](https://celebphotobooks.com/); [Sports Seoul report](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/1140993))

- Scope was limited to Korean retailer/publisher catalogs and closely related creator storefronts; this pass did not establish exhaustive coverage.
