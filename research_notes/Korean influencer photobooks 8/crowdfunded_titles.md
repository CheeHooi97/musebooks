# Korean Influencer Photobooks 8 — Crowdfunded Titles

## Is there a new campaign-backed photobook candidate with a named Korean creator/model?

### Takeaway

One conditional candidate surfaced: **2004년생 보디빌더 김홍희 & XMosaiX #1 화보집** (Kim Hong-hee & XMosaiX No. 1 Pictorial). Confidence is **medium for the advertised title, person, and planned physical format; low for confirmed publication/fulfillment**, because the accessible campaign evidence does not establish that it funded or shipped.

### Cited Findings

- The Wadiz project page names the title, identifies maker **XMosaiX**, describes it as a male body-art photobook, specifies an A4-size book and A1 poster, and shows a campaign window of 2025-10-25 through 2025-12-10. The campaign text calls it physique model Kim Hong-hee’s first photobook and gives his Instagram handle as **@kimhee1412**. [Wadiz campaign](https://www.wadiz.kr/web/campaign/detail/350003)
- Wadiz’s project story identifies Kim Hong-hee as Korean-born, born in 2004, and lists three 2025 physique competition placements. These are maker-provided claims on the campaign page, not independently corroborated here. [Wadiz campaign](https://www.wadiz.kr/web/campaign/detail/350003)
- A link-in-bio page under the matching handle, **kimhonghee1412**, self-describes a career in influencer work and Instagram sponsorship modeling, and lists XMosaiX as a web-pictorial modeling project. This supports a creator/model connection, but the page is self-authored and does not give an independently verifiable follower count. [Kim Hong-hee link profile](https://link.inpock.co.kr/kimhonghee1412)
- XMosaiX’s Linktree connects the maker name to Instagram, X, and Patreon profiles, supporting that the campaign maker has an online creator presence. [XMosaiX Linktree](https://linktr.ee/xmosaix)

### Inferences

- This is a strong **campaign lead** for a person-centered physical photobook, and its exact title and featured model are distinct from the prior campaigns explicitly excluded from this research pass. A Wadiz project page is not sufficient evidence that the reward was successfully funded or delivered.
- Keep any eventual bibliographic release date, pagination, and ISBN blank unless a post-campaign or bibliographic source supplies them. The campaign dates are funding dates, not publication dates.
- Do not add the campaign cover to R2 without permission to reuse that exact image.

### Gaps

- The accessible page did not expose a success/failed status, amount raised, backer count, reward dispatch notice, or recipient confirmation. Confirm whether this campaign funded and whether the book was actually produced before treating it as a published edition. [Wadiz campaign](https://www.wadiz.kr/web/campaign/detail/350003)
- The creator identity evidence is mostly self-reported. The campaign links the subject’s Instagram handle, but no independent source confirming that account’s ownership or audience scale was found in this pass. [Wadiz campaign](https://www.wadiz.kr/web/campaign/detail/350003); [Kim Hong-hee link profile](https://link.inpock.co.kr/kimhonghee1412)
- Page count, ISBN, publisher/imprint, exact final edition contents, release date, and post-campaign sales availability were not verified.

## Do other newly surfaced crowdfunding titles meet the same influencer-photobook threshold?

### Takeaway

No second new, person-centered creator photobook met the inclusion threshold in this scoped search. A few photo-book campaigns led by photographers/artists surfaced, but the primary pages did not establish that the advertised book was a photobook of an influencer/online personality.

### Cited Findings

- Wadiz’s **우모리의 첫 번째 사진집** describes an artist/photographer’s body of photography, offers two planned physical parts (each 150 pages, size 210 × 280 mm), and frames the book as the maker’s own photographic work; its campaign page does not identify a social-media influencer as the photobook subject. [Wadiz campaign](https://www.wadiz.io/web/campaign/detail/290989)
- Woomori’s Linktree identifies the account as **김 상우**, links Instagram, and directs visitors to the Wadiz photo-book project and an international purchase form. This verifies a creator-linked sales funnel but not influencer status or that the book is a portrait collection of an online personality. [Woomori Linktree](https://linktr.ee/woomori)
- The campaign’s Wadiz update says the project had succeeded and gives a planned 2024-08-21 to 2024-08-28 shipping window; it does not itself confirm final delivery. [Wadiz shipping update](https://www.wadiz.io/web/campaign/detailPost/290989/news/430281)

### Inferences

- Keep Woomori out of the influencer/model candidate list unless the catalog’s scope intentionally includes photographer-authored art books where the photographer is also the featured subject. Its primary campaign evidence supports an authored photography project, not the influencer-subject relationship required for this pass.

### Gaps

- The creator’s public Instagram content, follower count, and whether Woomori is the subject of the images were not verified.
- This search was bounded to crowdfunded Korean creator campaigns and did not establish an exhaustive title inventory.
