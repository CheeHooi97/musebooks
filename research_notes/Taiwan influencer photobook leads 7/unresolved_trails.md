# Unresolved Taiwan creator photobook trails

## 1. Has 菲菲／李恩菲’s 《韓你菲》 gained a direct seller record and identified cover?

### Takeaway

No. 《韓你菲》 remains a verified announcement for a physical photobook, but searches through 2026-10-30 did not recover its official order destination, an item-specific seller page, fulfillment evidence, or an image explicitly identified as the final cover. It is **not import-ready** for a physical edition with an R2 cover.

### Cited Findings

- UDN identifies 《韓你菲》 as 李恩菲／菲菲’s first solo physical photobook, says it was photographed in Busan, and reports that it was scheduled to go on sale on 2026-09-02 at 20:00. The story directs readers to the creator’s social accounts for sales details; it does not give a product URL, ISBN/EAN, publisher, pagination, or print specifications. [UDN report](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294)
- FTV independently identifies the title and physical format and repeats the announced sale time. Its image caption credits 杰斯汀整合行銷 as the provider of a publicity image, not as a publisher or seller and not as the source of an identified cover. [FTV report](https://www.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026831W0260)
- Kolr’s indexed profile snapshot, last updated 2026-10-17, recorded the Instagram bio line “韓你菲寫真書💖🛒預購連結🔗⬇️”. The snapshot does not reveal the link destination; the current page opened in this pass no longer shows that bio text. [Kolr profile snapshot](https://www.kolr.ai/influencer/a7c5f083-7e76-46ad-8b53-e6ca47e18ea9)
- A Shopee search-index result still surfaces seller wording “全新預購。10月底前出貨》 《韓你菲》在釜山等日落” and a prior search snapshot displayed NT$1,380. The URL is a name-search page, not an item URL; opening it in this pass showed unrelated items and did not identify the seller, product ID, or transaction. Do not treat “在釜山等日落” as a confirmed subtitle or the shipping promise as proof of shipment. [Shopee search results for 李恩菲](https://shopee.tw/search?keyword=%E6%9D%8E%E6%81%A9%E8%8F%B2)

- The local catalog’s 李恩菲 section contains older digital titles and the separate 《bestie閨蜜》 entry, but no 《韓你菲》 row. Prior read-only MuseBooks title queries for 《韓你菲》 also returned no match. [Taiwan photobook catalog, 李恩菲 entries](../../scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md#L976); [MuseBooks title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E9%9F%93%E4%BD%A0%E8%8F%B2)

[UDN](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294); [Kolr snapshot](https://www.kolr.ai/influencer/a7c5f083-7e76-46ad-8b53-e6ca47e18ea9); [Shopee search results](https://shopee.tw/search?keyword=%E6%9D%8E%E6%81%A9%E8%8F%B2)
- Keep the title as 《韓你菲》; neither the seller snippet nor publicity coverage establishes “在釜山等日落” as official subtitle metadata. [UDN](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9724294); [Shopee search results](https://shopee.tw/search?keyword=%E6%9D%8E%E6%81%A9%E8%8F%B2)

### Gaps

- Still missing: official preorder destination or direct item page; seller identity; ISBN/EAN; publisher/imprint; page count and dimensions; a stable image explicitly identified as the final cover; and any order or shipment evidence. Instagram was not directly inspectable during this pass and the Shopee search page did not expose the indexed item.

## 2. Is 鄭羽凡’s digital edition now stable enough to add under her physical work?

### Takeaway

The ebook’s existence and unique EISBN are now well supported by a direct Books.com product record, but the same identifier has a conflicting format in the NCL deposit list, and an ebook-specific stable cover asset was not recovered. Treat it as a **strong digital-edition lead, but not ready for a DB-plus-R2 import** until the cover provenance and format treatment are resolved or explicitly recorded as uncertain.

### Cited Findings

- Books.com’s direct ebook product page lists **《其實我有點壞Si;era’s photobook鄭羽凡寫真書 (電子書)》**, author 鄭羽凡, publisher 水靈文創, Taiwan publication, date **2026-08-18**, EISBN **9786267742044**, fixed-layout EPUB, and 21.4 MB file size. It lists the ebook and paperback as separate purchasable versions. Its ebook details do not give a numeric page count. [Books.com ebook product](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050354706)
- NCL’s 2025 deposit list records the same title, author, publisher, and identifier (978-626-7742-04-4) as **PDF**, with **11408 / 2025-08** explicitly under “expected publication month”; the pagination field is “面”, not a number. This is a real same-identifier format discrepancy. [NCL 2025 deposit list](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1755143472.pdf)
- The already cataloged 2022 physical book is a separate format and has ISBN **9786269640614**, a 2022-09-26 date, and 128 pages; Kingstone identifies it as a physical listing. Those print details must not be copied to the ebook. [Kingstone print listing](https://www.kingstone.com.tw/basic/2019910048444/); [MuseBooks work record](https://musebooks.my/books/work-tw-9bcbbed2719c0138ff33c410)
- The earlier local follow-up found no standalone image URL that could be matched to Books.com’s ebook listing; attempts to fetch the likely product-image route returned HTTP 403. This pass’s indexed searches again surfaced no direct ebook-cover asset. The existing MuseBooks cover belongs to the paperback record. [Books.com ebook product](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050354706); [MuseBooks physical work record](https://musebooks.my/books/work-tw-9bcbbed2719c0138ff33c410)
- Books.com’s author bio describes 鄭羽凡 as formerly part of the online group “在不瘋狂就等死” and active in social media for years, supporting creator/influencer relevance. [Books.com ebook product and author bio](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050354706)

### Inferences

- Books.com supplies a direct retail record that supports an ebook edition with EISBN 9786267742044. The date discrepancy can be a planned-versus-actual delay because NCL labels 2025-08 as expected, but no source explains it; that is not evidence that the PDF/EPUB disagreement was corrected. [Books.com](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050354706); [NCL](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1755143472.pdf)
- If the catalog permits a generic digital format and leaving the release date/page count blank, a later import could preserve the confirmed EISBN and direct product URL while explicitly avoiding a claim about PDF versus EPUB. The present cover gap still prevents the requested DB-and-R2 completion under the project’s cover workflow.
- The ebook should be a new edition under the existing 鄭羽凡 work, not a new work; its EISBN is distinct from the paperback ISBN. [Books.com](https://www.books.com.tw/products/E050354706); [MuseBooks work record](https://musebooks.my/books/work-tw-9bcbbed2719c0138ff33c410)

### Gaps

- No publisher statement or second independent retailer resolved PDF versus fixed-layout EPUB.
- No direct, stable ebook-specific cover URL was verified; do not reuse the paperback image without confirmation.
- No numeric page count is given by either the retailer or NCL.
- No source explains the 2025 expected date versus the live retailer date of 2026-08-18.
