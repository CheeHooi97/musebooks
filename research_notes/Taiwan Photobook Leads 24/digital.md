# Direct Taiwan Digital Photobook Listings — Round 24

## Did this round find a safe new Taiwan influencer ebook to import?

### Takeaway

**No GO.** The only fresh, promising direct digital listing in this sweep was *PASSION：人態好攝影集4（數位特別版）*. The ebook is real, released, and has an EISBN, but the book is a photographer-led group collection; the available sources do not establish that both featured models are Taiwan-origin influencers, and the current direct listing image is an age-restriction placeholder rather than an inspectable final cover. Keep it **HOLD**; no R2 or catalog writes were made.

### Cited Findings

- Readmoo sells *PASSION：人態好攝影集4（數位特別版）* as a fixed-layout EPUB released June 6, 2025, ISBN 9786264157292 / EISBN 9786264158145, with a listed retail price of NT$490. The seller description names two subjects only as Bin (`@binbincoach`) and Ray (`@ray._.tuntun`), and calls them a private trainer and fitness coach. [Readmoo direct ebook product](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=137751)
- Google Play Books independently lists the title as a 162-page June 2025 ebook from Taiwan Kadokawa and repeats the Bin/Ray handles and descriptions. It says the ebook layout has about 90% different content from the print version. [Google Play Books product](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=9P5fEQAAQBAJ)
- The NCL deposit list supports a Taipei print edition with ISBN 9786264157292, 160 pages, but this record is for the paperback and does not identify the ebook cover or verify either model’s origin. [NCL deposit list](https://nclfile.ncl.edu.tw/files/202507/b4b0a95d-62aa-43ba-9f06-34e98f79920e.pdf)
- The photographer’s linked account advertises separate physical and digital editions. A third-party X profile snapshot describes `@npp2021` as based in Taiwan, but that describes the photographer rather than either featured coach. [Photographer account links](https://linktr.ee/nicepeoplephotography); [third-party profile snapshot](https://www.sotwe.com/npp2021)
- A third-party Instagram-influencer ranking lists `@binbincoach` as 陳宏彬 and classifies the account in Taiwan, but it does not establish origin and is not an authoritative identity source. No comparably useful result connected Ray’s handle to a Taiwan-origin identity. [Scrumball influencer listing](https://www.scrumball.com/ranking/top-workout-influencers-on-instagram-in-taiwan)

[Readmoo direct ebook product](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=137751); [Readmoo image asset](https://pgw.udn.com.tw/gw/photo.php?u=https://dc.udn.com/hsuanhao/TEK2209DP007/images/R18.jpg)

This indicates no matching record was returned by those exact queries; it does not resolve the creator-scope or cover gaps. [MuseBooks title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=PASSION%20%E4%BA%BA%E6%85%8B%E5%A5%BD%E6%94%9D%E5%BD%B1%E9%9B%864&pageSize=50); [MuseBooks EISBN query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=9786264158145&pageSize=50)

### Inferences

- **HOLD — *PASSION*.** The digital product is verified, but the work is credited to a photography collective rather than a named Taiwan influencer, the product identifies the models only by first name/handle, and a final ebook cover could not be checked. A Taiwan-based publisher/photographer or Taiwan-located influencer ranking does not by itself establish the requested Taiwan-origin identity. [Readmoo product](https://reading.udn.com/ebook/store/store_product.do?pid=137751); [photographer account links](https://linktr.ee/nicepeoplephotography); [Scrumball listing](https://www.scrumball.com/ranking/top-workout-influencers-on-instagram-in-taiwan)
