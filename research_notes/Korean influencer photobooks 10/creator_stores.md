# Korean Creator Digital Photobooks and Web-Pictorial Stores

Research date: 2026-10-30

## Which creator-store listings verify new digital photobooks?

### Takeaway
Two distinct paid digital issues from YooSeorin's LOVEYS series have exact Gumroad product pages and first-person creator copy. The creator’s first-party Patreon page categorizes her magazine as Korean glamour and includes “Korean Model” among its tags; credit the public identity “Seorin/YooSeorin” without inferring a legal name or citizenship.

### Cited Findings
- The exact Gumroad listing **[LOVEYS Vol.2 – 소녀, 그리고 설레임](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_02)** is offered for $18.99 as both KR/EN PDF and KR/EN JPG ZIP. The page describes three concepts (“Girlhood,” “Purity,” and the excitement of the coming night), says it contains 43 pages, and includes short letters written by the first-person speaker who identifies herself as Seorin. [Gumroad product page](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_02)
- The separate exact listing **[LOVEYS Vol.3 – Winter Trip](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_03)** calls itself Seorin’s third love letter and presents an illustrated winter pictorial/photo diary narrative. It offers KR/EN PDF and JPG ZIP files. The page text reviewed does not establish price, page count, or publication date. [Gumroad product page](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_03)
- The creator’s Patreon account is named **YooSeorin 🌸**; her first-person 2026 guide says that her two main magazines are LOVEYS and RINIQ, describes the monthly issue cadence, and explains how the paid extended editions are sold. This supports that the LOVEYS product line is her own creator publication, rather than a third-party title merely featuring an unrelated person of the same name. [YooSeorin’s 2026 publishing guide](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/2026nyeon-balmae-147382927?l=de)
- In an earlier Patreon post, the creator describes LOVEYS as her monthly photo diary and identifies herself in first person as Seorin. [YooSeorin’s LOVEYS Vol.1 post](https://www.patreon.com/posts/loveys-vol-1-141787962)

### Inferences
- The self-authored notes, creator-controlled Patreon identity, and recurring LOVEYS magazine schedule make confidence **high** that these are digital portrait/photo-diary issues published by creator “Seorin” (Patreon display name “YooSeorin”). The sources do not establish that “Yoo” is a legal family name.
- The issues are written in Korean and English, and the creator’s Patreon publication identifies its magazine as Korean glamour and includes “Korean Model” among its tags. This supports the Korean creator/model scope at the publication level, but does not establish citizenship; keep nationality/origin unset.
- Treat Vol.2 and Vol.3 as separate titled issues if the catalog stores each monthly issue as a work; do not make up edition dates or page counts for Vol.3.

### Gaps
- The Gumroad product pages do not show a reliable publication date for either issue in the text reviewed; only Vol.2’s description calls it the November issue. Do not convert that month mention into a year or exact release date. [Vol.2 product page](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_02); [Vol.3 product page](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_03)
- No asset-specific reuse or redistribution license for either issue was found. The Gumroad purchase pages are not permission to mirror paid PDFs, JPGs, or cover images in R2. Keep media empty pending written authorization from the rights holder. [Vol.2 product page](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_02); [Vol.3 product page](https://rinibooks.gumroad.com/l/loveys_03)

### Takeaway
The first-party sources verify a real creator-led photo publication under the public name Seorin/YooSeorin, and her Patreon publication categorizes the magazine as Korean glamour / model content. These sources do not verify a legal name, citizenship, or audience size. Metadata can use the creator’s public name and model category without adding a legal name or nationality.

[YooSeorin Patreon guide](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/2026nyeon-balmae-147382927?l=de)

### Inferences

Her creator-controlled Patreon publication categorizes the magazine as Korean glamour/model content; citizenship remains unverified.

## Which adjacent leads should be held, and why?

### Takeaway
Several exact web-pictorial pages confirm digital products but do not meet the direct-creator and/or identity bar for this pass. Keep them as leads only, and do not treat their page images or purchase files as reusable R2 media.

### Cited Findings
- Bluecake’s Gumroad product **[꾸뿌 美人](https://s2bluecake.gumroad.com/l/ggubbu016)** advertises two GGuBBu photo concepts (70 and 82 images, or a combined 152-image set), priced $22.99 each or $39.99 together. It identifies GGuBBu as the model and Garam & Dall as photographers, but the page states BLUECAKE holds copyright and limits use to private collection. GGuBBu’s [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/ggubbu) links to a separate “ggubbu gumroad” account; that does not establish that this BLUECAKE product is sold through her own account. [Bluecake product page](https://s2bluecake.gumroad.com/l/ggubbu016); [GGuBbu Linktree](https://linktr.ee/ggubbu)
- Movie Fantasy’s own 2022 announcement calls GguBbu a model and describes a different digital issue, **HIGH FANTASY Vol.7 in the Garden**, as 52 JPG files in one 489 MB ZIP. It is useful as model/product evidence but is a studio product, not a creator-owned store listing; it also does not establish a current sale. [Movie Fantasy announcement](https://moviefantasy.co.kr/notice/?bmode=view&idx=12384252&q=YToxOntzOjEyOiJrZXl3b3JkX3R5cGUiO3M6MzoiYWxsIjt9&t=board); [GGuBbu Linktree](https://linktr.ee/ggubbu)
- A CTEE listing, **[Yoonvely’s Second Photobook “Bedroom Story”](https://ctee.kr/item/store/49279)**, calls Yoonvely an influencer/model and specifies two concepts, 58 high-resolution photos, instant ZIP download, and a displayed discounted price of USD 12.66 (regular USD 18.72). The listing is now marked private/no longer for sale and explicitly assigns copyright to ICON Photoworks; its only Instagram URL is ICON Photoworks, not a verified model profile. [CTEE product page](https://ctee.kr/item/store/49279)
- A personal-link page for **pureding** links to “buy web pictorials on CTEE,” confirming a storefront route but the readable store pages did not reveal an exact product listing and contents. Hold until a specific issue/product URL is documented. [pureding link page](https://link.inpock.co.kr/pureding); [CTEE store page](https://ctee.kr/place/pureding/store?group=3249)
- A Gumroad product called **Yoona’s flight attendant outfit_web photo book** describes a daily-fashion web photobook, $5+ and 114 MB, and forbids unauthorized redistribution. However, it gives only the model label “Yoon A!” and links to a seller named Toonit; I found no reliable creator profile tying that stage name to a Korean influencer/model. Hold rather than create a weak person record. [Toonit product page](https://toonit.gumroad.com/l/flight)

### Inferences
- GGuBbu’s first-party Linktree verifies an active social/creator identity, and the Movie Fantasy announcement verifies her as a model, but the two product listings above are third-party studio products; neither is counted as a confirmed new creator-owned Gumroad product in this pass.
- A seller’s private-collection restriction or explicit copyright statement is a strong reason to withhold assets from R2. No image, preview, or paid file was copied or uploaded in this research pass.

### Gaps
- The GGuBbu and Yoonvely products lack an exact creator-owned product/rights chain; Yoonvely additionally lacks a verified first-party model profile. The pureding link page lacks a readable, exact product record. Keep all three unimported until those gaps close.
- No purchase grants catalog/R2 hosting rights by itself. A written license or direct rights-holder permission covering the exact cover/interior assets and R2 redistribution is still needed.
