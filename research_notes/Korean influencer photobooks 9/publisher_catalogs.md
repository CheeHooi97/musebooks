## Verified physical photobook

### 아쿠아 AQUA — ROTTA × ZENNY (신재은)

- **Exact product:** [YES24 listing, goods ID 57954502](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/57954502)
- **Cross-retailer record:** [Aladin listing, item ID 129267562](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?itemid=129267562)
- **Format:** Physical print photo book / 사진집. YES24 categorizes it under photo books and says a new copy cannot currently be purchased; its page shows used copies. Aladin marks the publisher-confirmed edition discontinued (with its page warning that residual stock observations may be stale).
- **Imprint/publisher:** 브라보(bravo)
- **Release date:** 2018-01-26
- **Pagination and size:** 168 pages; 183 × 257 mm. YES24 lists 540g; Aladin lists 687g, so weight conflicts and is not treated as settled metadata.
- **ISBN:** 9791188073146 (ISBN-13)
- **Subject:** 신재은 (Shin Jae-eun), also known as Zenny / ZENNY / @zennyrt. The YES24 description calls her the model known by the ZENNY alias and describes the book as photographer ROTTA’s collaboration with her.
- **Primary creator evidence:** Zenny’s [official About page](https://zenn.ee/en/about/) self-identifies her as Jae-Eun Shin (신재은), also known as Zenny and @zennyrt, and as a model. The same first-party page lists **Aqua**, **Zenny in Philippines**, and **Oasis** under “Photo books.” Her [official Videos page](https://zenn.ee/en/videos/) describes clips “from my own channel” and links to her YouTube channel. This supports a direct creator-profile and ongoing creator activity; it does not provide an independently measured audience size.
- **Confidence:** High that a distinct physical edition exists and that Shin Jae-eun/Zenny is its featured subject (retailer bibliographic record plus explicit subject description, corroborated by her official profile). Creator/influencer-scope fit is medium-high: first-party evidence identifies her as a model and online creator, but no first-party audience metric was checked.
- **Image reuse / R2:** No image reuse or redistribution license was found on the YES24 or Aladin product records or the official profile pages reviewed. Product-page availability is not permission to reproduce its cover or interior images. No images were downloaded; keep R2 media empty pending asset-specific permission.
