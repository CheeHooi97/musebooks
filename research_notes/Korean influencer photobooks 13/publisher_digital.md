## Does the product evidence credibly link the named creator to model work?

### Takeaway
The retailer page directly names Kang Gyu-nam (강규남) as author, calls the work a photobook for senior models, and the author bio identifies her as a senior advertising model. A senior-model association profile independently presents Kang Gyu-nam under its senior-model section. This is a credible public-name and model-role link, though the catalog should not turn the evidence into a claim of Korean citizenship or claim that every image in each volume exclusively depicts her.

### Cited Findings
- RIDI labels the work “시니어 모델을 위한 화보집” (a photobook for senior models), names 강규남 as author, and identifies her in the product biography as “시니어 광고모델” (senior advertising model). The same bio ties her to a senior-model agency and lists public modeling/media activities. [RIDI Vol. 1](https://ridibooks.com/books/1745005096)
- The K-Senior Model Association site has a dedicated “시니어 모델 강규남” profile and describes her as a senior advertising model; the site also identifies the associated Durimoa agency. [K-Senior Model Association profile](https://jfs77081.wixsite.com/k-seniormodel)
- A separate senior-model profile page describes her own New Face Senior magazine/photobook project, corroborating the creator-to-series relationship, though it is creator-controlled rather than independent reporting. [Senior Model Kang Gyu-nam](https://senior-model.tistory.com/6)

### Inferences
- Record the public person identity as 강규남 / Kang Gyu-nam and credit her as author/creator as shown on the product pages. Do not assign a featured-subject credit without evidence identifying the person depicted.
- The domestic Korean-language product and association sources support researching her as a Korean-market senior model; they do not establish citizenship.

### Gaps
- Neither the listing text nor accessible product preview establishes whether all six volumes exclusively feature Kang Gyu-nam, or how the photobook images are distributed among other senior-model portfolio subjects.
- No independent press source was found tying each numbered volume's contents to an explicit image subject list.
