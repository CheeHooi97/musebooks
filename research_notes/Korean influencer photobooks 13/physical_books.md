# Korean creator physical-book leads

## Key Question

Do newly found Korean influencer books meet the catalog's physical-photobook threshold?

### Takeaway

Pyo Eunji's official store offers a C106 set with three individually named, physically specified B5 books. The source calls them “new books,” not photobooks, and does not state that Pyo is the author or photographed subject of each title. **Hold all three from the photobook import** until an official creator/publisher description or reliable bibliographic listing confirms the format and Pyo's relationship to each work. Keep the cover and image fields empty: no R2 reuse permission was found.

### Cited Findings

#### C106 set: three physical books, photobook status unconfirmed

- **Direct official-store product page:** [Comic Market C106 New Books Set](https://pyoeunji.com/en/products/c106set). The page is hosted at Pyo Eunji's official site (footer: “PYO EUNJI JAPAN OFFICIAL FANCLUB”), offers the set for ¥9,000 with an “Add to Cart” control, says Pyo Eunji participated in Summer Comic Market C106 in Tokyo in August 2025, and offers the same set sold at the event. It calls the package a “C106 New Publication Set” with “3 new books”; it never calls these three items photo books or photo books featuring Pyo. There are no separate product/checkout URLs for the individual books on this page.
- The “Product Contents” section identifies these **book titles**—not model names—as separate quantity-one items: **Ping Pong: Mini-Skirt Delivery Diary** (B5, 62 pages); **The Side of Me That No One Sees** (B5, 56 pages); **Beyond the Secret Curtain** (B5, 56 pages). The other included items are one clear file, two postcards, and one sticker; these are merchandise, not book records.
- The official [Comic Market C106 notice](https://www.comiket.co.jp/info-a/C106/C106Info) dates the event to August 16–17, 2025. This supports August 2025 as the set's event-sale context, not an exact title publication date. The shop says shipping would begin “September 16” but omits the year and does not establish actual dispatch.
- [Shueisha's Weekly Playboy profile](https://wpb.shueisha.co.jp/gravure/20220821-117087/) identifies Pyo Eunji as Korean-born and working in Korea as a model and YouTuber, linking to her official site. This verifies her creator/influencer identity; it does **not** identify her as author or photo subject for any C106 book.
- One independent [eBay resale listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/397121672933) calls **The Side of Me That No One Sees** a Japanese paperback photo book and lists Pyo Eunji as the signer. This seller-provided classification gives a lead for that one title only; it is not a publisher or library record, and does not establish the contents or who is photographed. No comparable corroboration was found for the other two titles. Do not treat it as sufficient to import the trio.
- The shop's internal product number `8289238417590` is not labeled ISBN. No ISBN, publisher/imprint, title-level release date, or standalone title product page was found. I found no C106/title-string match in the repository's existing `docs`, `reports`, or `research_notes` at screening time; this is a new lead, not evidence that the works meet the photobook scope.
- The official site's [copyright/about page](https://pyoeunji.com/ko/pages/about) says site information and images are owned by Pyo Eunji's agency or other legitimate rights holders and prohibits unauthorized use/reproduction. No license or permission for the exact C106 cover/promotional images in R2 was found. No image or book file was downloaded or uploaded.

#### Other lead: C108 set, hold

- Pyo's [official C108 announcement](https://pyoeunji.com/en/blogs/blogs) confirms her planned August 16, 2026 C108 participation but says product details would be announced later. The [Cupick event listing](https://cupick.jp/products/pyoeunji-birthday-event) says its C108 set contains three new books; it does not name them or give page counts. These are unverified book leads, not photobook records. No associated image-reuse permission was found.

#### Duplicate lead excluded

- Kim Woohyeon's 2025 *Extreme* and the related *YORU* material were excluded from new-candidate counts because the repository's earlier research notes and report already cover them and leave the title/edition relationship unresolved: [prior note](../Korean%20influencer%20photobook%20additions%204/woohyun.md), [prior report](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2011.md).

### Inferences

- C106 is sufficient evidence that three physical books were offered together, but a B5 trim and page count alone do not establish that they are photo books. The safest catalog action within the photobook project is to hold each title until genre and creator/subject association are documented.
- The official store identifies Pyo's participation in C106 and sells the event set, but the page does not name an author or photographed subject beside each title. Do not make Pyo a title's author/model solely from the storefront context.
- Even if the titles are later confirmed as photobooks, the store listing does not grant rights to reuse cover art or photographs in R2. Request or locate permission for each exact asset first.

### Gaps

- Need an official creator/publisher statement, or authoritative catalog record, calling each named title a photobook and establishing its author/photographed subject.
- Need the three original-language title forms (the accessible English product page supplies display titles only), ISBNs, publisher/imprint, and exact publication dates if available.
- C108's exact title names, pagination, and genre remain undisclosed in the sources reviewed.
- No verified R2 reuse license exists for any C106/C108 image.
