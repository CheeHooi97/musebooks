# Creator-Led Korean Digital Photobooks

## Does a new first-party creator product meet the digital photobook threshold?

### Takeaway

**Clear Blue Vol. 02** by the Patreon account **서진의 아틀리에** is a strong new product-level import candidate: the creator’s own page sells a downloadable PDF photobook and supplies precise pagination and content counts. The public materials do not independently establish the creator’s Korean nationality or legal identity, so keep the public name as “Seojin/서진” and treat any person-level South Korea attribution as unresolved.

### Cited Findings

- The exact post is titled *A Little Early Summer, A Lot More Seojin | Clear Blue Vol. 02*, is listed with a paid-post control (AU$16.50 in the page snapshot), and attaches `Clear_Blue_Vol2_Digital_Edition.pdf`. The post calls it a digital photobook, says no physical item is shipped, and lists 162 pages, 39 photo series, and 357 photographs. It includes previously unpublished photos and Korean/English notes. ([Clear Blue Vol. 02 product page](https://www.patreon.com/seojinsatelier/posts/little-early-lot-169117233))
- The associated Patreon creator page is named **서진의 아틀리에** and describes itself as providing digital photobooks and wallpapers for people who love Seojin’s daily life, supporting a creator-to-product link. ([서진의 아틀리에 Patreon page](https://www.patreon.com/cw/seojinsatelier))
- The product page displays only “September 10”; it does not expose a year, ISBN, or publisher imprint in the reviewed public view. Do not normalize a full release date from the post’s relative crawl label. ([Clear Blue Vol. 02 product page](https://www.patreon.com/seojinsatelier/posts/little-early-lot-169117233))
- The product is newly distinct from the imported YooSeorin *LOVEYS*, *RINIQ*, and *O.N.O* titles: the batch 10–12 manifests and report files contain no exact Seojin/Clear Blue match. ([Batch 10 import](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-10-import.json), [Batch 11 import](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-11-import.json), [Batch 12 import](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-import.json), [report 10](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2010.md), [report 11](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2011.md), [report 12](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2012.md))

### Inferences

- Product evidence is sufficient to catalog one digital photobook work with one PDF edition under the public creator name. If the catalog requires proof of Korean nationality or residence to assign a South Korea person origin, hold that person attribution until better evidence is found.
- “Vol. 02” establishes an earlier volume exists, but it does not identify its exact title, metadata, or whether it is still sold; do not create a Vol. 01 record from this reference alone.

### Gaps

- No reliable independent source was found confirming Seojin’s nationality, full identity, or occupation as an influencer/model. The product is written in Korean and English, but language alone does not prove nationality.
- No exact release year, ISBN, or publisher imprint is stated on the reviewed page.

## What other creator/storefront leads were screened out?

### Takeaway

The search found adjacent paid-photo products, but their creator/subject identity or exact digital edition evidence is weaker than Clear Blue Vol. 02. Keep them as leads rather than imports in this pass.

### Cited Findings

- BimilStory’s Patreon post calls **Yeonjju Vol.01 <Debut: First Impression>** a new photobook and says model Yeonju debuted on BimilStory, but the post does not state a price, digital file type, pagination, or a direct sales/download route. ([Yeonjju Vol.01 post](https://www.patreon.com/bimilstory/posts/yeonjju-vol-01-153790186))
- BimilStory’s official website lists “연주” under its web-photobook section and identifies a Korean business, but this does not by itself resolve Yeonju’s identity or connect a specific official sales page to the Patreon post. ([BimilStory official site](https://bimilstory.com/))
- A BimilStory CTEE listing for **Eunsu Vol.01** describes a downloadable PDF/JPG photobook with 90 photos and separate sketch-video files. The same page says the item is private and no longer for sale. BimilStory is the listed seller, rather than an individually verified creator identity for “Eunsu.” ([Eunsu Vol.01 product page](https://ctee.kr/item/store/33737))
- CTEE describes age-restricted magazines and photo books as a digital-product category and says creators sell/manage their own products; that establishes the platform’s general sales model, not any individual model’s identity or a license for MuseBooks. ([CTEE creator guide](https://ctee.kr/guide/playbook/recipe))
- The CTEE product page’s default terms disallow commercial use unless a listing says otherwise and state that product copyright remains with its creator. ([Eunsu Vol.01 product page](https://ctee.kr/item/store/33737))

### Inferences

- Keep Yeonjju Vol.01 on hold until an exact seller/product page establishes that it is a paid digital edition and a reliable source distinguishes the subject from other people using the name Yeonju.
- Keep Eunsu Vol.01 as a publisher-store lead, not a creator-led influencer import: the listing is closed/private and the single-name subject is not independently identified.

### Gaps

- No exact public CTEE checkout page for Yeonjju Vol.01 was located in this pass.
- No independent profile/source links Eunsu or Yeonju to a unique Korean influencer identity.

## What reuse rights and corpus overlap can be established?

### Takeaway

No candidate image is cleared for R2. Clear Blue Vol. 02 expressly limits its files to personal use and forbids redistribution, resale, and commercial use; the other storefront pages likewise provide no asset-specific R2 license.

### Cited Findings

- Clear Blue Vol. 02 says its PDF is for personal use only and prohibits redistribution, resale, or commercial use. This is an explicit restriction, not permission to mirror its cover or pages. ([Clear Blue Vol. 02 product page](https://www.patreon.com/seojinsatelier/posts/little-early-lot-169117233))
- CTEE’s Eunsu Vol.01 listing says commercial use is not allowed unless otherwise stated, and purchasing does not transfer copyright or related rights. ([Eunsu Vol.01 product page](https://ctee.kr/item/store/33737))
- A keyword scan of the current workspace’s imported JSON manifests and the 10–12 research reports found no exact Seojin/Clear Blue or BimilStory/Yeonjju/Eunsu title match. Existing YooSeorin Patreon/RINI BOOKS projects were checked as explicit exclusions; the new Seojin candidate is not one of them. ([Batch 10 import](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-10-import.json), [Batch 11 import](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-11-import.json), [Batch 12 import](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-12-import.json), [report 10](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2010.md), [report 11](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2011.md), [report 12](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2012.md))

### Inferences

- Record Seojin’s bibliographic metadata only; leave cover and media references empty. Do not upload any purchased file, preview, or product-page image to R2 without separate written permission covering the exact asset and third-party hosting.
- CTEE or Patreon purchase access is not a redistribution license; the platform/product terms reserve or restrict the underlying creator content.

### Gaps

- No rightsholder grant for third-party R2 hosting was found for any screened item.
- The workspace manifest scan establishes no file-level duplicate in the reviewed corpus; a live-database duplicate check remains for the coordinator/importer before any commit.

### Addendum: official profile and linked-social check

- The accessible official Patreon profile describes “Seojin’s Atelier” as “a quiet little archive of soft portraits, imagined memories, and digital photobooks.” It does not state Seojin’s nationality, location, or occupation, and its visible navigation does not link an Instagram or other social profile. ([Official Seojin’s Atelier Patreon profile](https://www.patreon.com/cw/seojinsatelier))
- The Clear Blue Vol. 02 post mentions Instagram only to say that some photographs have not been posted there; it supplies no Instagram handle or location/identity evidence. ([Clear Blue Vol. 02 product page](https://www.patreon.com/seojinsatelier/posts/little-early-lot-169117233))
- Searches limited to the named creator and her official Patreon/social profile did not produce an official linked source identifying her as a Korean model/influencer or Korea-based creator. Search-engine results from third-party Instagram mirrors were not used as identity evidence.
- **Decision:** preserve Clear Blue Vol. 02 as a product-level lead, not an import to the Korean influencer catalog, until an official linked profile establishes the Korean creator connection. Product metadata remains well-supported; the subject-origin link does not.
