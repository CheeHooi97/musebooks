# Publisher and Press Discovery: 2024 SOOP Streamer Awards Photobook

## New physical photobook and subject fit

### Takeaway
The 2024 SOOP Streamer Awards Photobook is a verified physical, group photobook featuring Korean online creators. SOOP’s own ESG page confirms it sold the award photobook as a fundraiser; Kuki News reports the exact title, a limited sale through SOOPTORE, 65 participating streamers, a user-selectable cover, and a hard-cover album of about 100 pages. This is a plausible group work for the Korean influencer catalog, though it is not a solo model photobook.

### Cited Findings
- SOOP’s official ESG 2024 community-contribution page lists “2024 스트리머 대상 포토북 판매” and says the award photobook was sold with all proceeds donated. [SOOP ESG 2024](https://corp.sooplive.com/esg/2024/index.php?page=sharing)
- Kuki News reports that 65 creators including Gamst, Isegye Idol, Woowakgood, and UjeongIng participated in the 2024 SOOP Streamer Awards photobook; the limited release went on sale through SOOPTORE in January 2025, with proceeds donated. [Kuki News, 12 March 2025](https://m.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503120038)
- Kuki News describes the product as a hard-cover photo album of about 100 pages, containing participant portraits, behind-the-scenes images, award-show scenes, and performances; buyers could choose a participating streamer’s photograph for the cover. [Kuki News, 12 March 2025](https://m.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503120038)
- SOOP’s official event page confirms the 2024 Streamer Awards was an SOOP-produced event and names a fan-signing lineup including UjeongIng, Jipiti, Park Ppippi, and Wanana. [2024 SOOP Streamer Awards program](https://2024award.sooplive.co.kr/?page=program)
- A Newsis profile describes Gamst as an internet broadcaster and a prominent Korean streamer, supporting at least one named subject’s Korean creator identity. [Newsis, 5 November 2024](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20241105_0002946977)

### Inferences
- Because the physical format and several participating creators are established by SOOP and Korean press, the group product fits the user's broad creator/influencer photobook goal, subject to MuseBooks accepting group volumes and streamer categories.
- The sources name only examples of the 65 participants rather than a complete title-page credit list. The evidence does not establish that every participant is Korean or a model; represent the credited group conservatively and do not create individual credits for all 65 from the press excerpt alone.

### Gaps
- The sources do not enumerate all 65 participants or prove that every participant is Korean; they also do not identify all participants as models. This record should be treated as a group creator/streamer photobook, not as 65 individually verified model records.
- No second new candidate met the physical-product and creator-identity standard in this pass. Broad searches mostly returned unrelated self-made albums, celebrity/idol photo books, or generic photo-album pages; these were excluded.

## Is there an exact open-licensed bibliographic record, and is the work already in MuseBooks?

### Takeaway
No exact open-licensed bibliographic record was identified, and the live catalog search returned no matching photobook. The physical product is a promising research lead, but the available evidence does not clear metadata reuse for import.

### Cited Findings
- Searches for `2024 SOOP 스트리머 대상 포토북`, `SOOP 포토북`, and title variants on National Library of Korea/LOD discovery surfaces returned no exact record in the web-search results. [National Library of Korea LOD](https://lod.nl.go.kr/)
- The project source register states that no source has blanket automated-page-extraction permission; a public publisher or press page is not in itself an open metadata license. [Project Korean photobook source register](../../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)
- A live read-only check used the local `catalog-preview` service, which opens the configured MuseBooks database with `database.Open(true, false)` and exposes GET-only catalog reads. Searches for `SOOP`, `2024 SOOP Streamer Awards`, `Streamer Awards Photobook`, `2024 스트리머 대상 포토북`, and `스트리머 대상` each returned `total=0` with `publicationType=photobook`.

### Inferences
- The search results are inconclusive on NLK coverage and do not prove that no open record exists. The catalog query establishes only that no title match was returned by those live search terms; it does not authorize an import.
- Do not stage/import from these sources alone. First locate an exact open-licensed bibliographic record or obtain permission covering the intended metadata use.

### Gaps
- No ISBN, publisher imprint, stable product ID, complete bibliographic record, full participant list, or metadata-reuse license was found in the reviewed official and press sources.

## Are any photobook images cleared for R2 storage and public display?

### Takeaway
No cover image is cleared for R2 storage and public display. The reviewed SOOP and press sources do not state an asset-level image license.

### Cited Findings
- Kuki News reports that buyers could select a participating streamer’s image for the cover. [Kuki News, 12 March 2025](https://m.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503120038)
- The project source register states that product-image provenance does not itself grant reuse rights, and no blanket source permission has been recorded. [Project Korean photobook source register](../../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)

### Inferences
- A future R2 upload needs permission for the exact selected cover variant, including storage and public delivery; the customizable cover means the product may not have one universal cover image.
- No cover or other image was downloaded, copied, or uploaded during this research pass; no database writes or external inquiries were made.

### Gaps
- There is no captured product image or stable store product URL to identify the exact cover asset. Even if a compatible cover file is located, permission must cover that exact image and the chosen cover variant.
- The rights holder(s) for streamer portraits and cover-design photographs are not identified in the reviewed sources; a SOOP product page alone would not establish all image rights.
