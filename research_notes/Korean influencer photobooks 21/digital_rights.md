# Korean Influencer Digital Photobook and Exact Image Rights (Round 21)

## Did this pass find a new Korean influencer digital photobook with exact metadata from a source explicitly licensed for commercial reuse?

### Takeaway
No candidate cleared the required combination of verified creator identity, exact digital photobook metadata, and commercial-reuse authorization for the metadata source. Ayul (아율) is a promising digital-photobook lead, but the open metadata license and exact product record could not be established.

### Cited Findings
- A 2020 article describes “BJ 아율” as a popular PandaTV streamer and says her debut was published as a digital photobook; it does not provide a stable product identifier, ISBN, pagination, or full bibliographic record. [NewsCulture report reproduced by Ppomppu](https://m.ppomppu.co.kr/new/bbs_view.php?id=news_broadcast&no=128244)
- Ayul’s public Linktree labels a Puremedia release “아율 1집 / Ayul 1st Sexy photo album,” linking to Puremedia and a Puremedia Gumroad shop; this confirms the product name as presented by the creator-facing account but grants no commercial metadata reuse license. [Ayul Linktree](https://linktr.ee/store_ayul)
- Puremedia’s own current product-category page lists “Ayul / 아율 Story e-book / PURE MEDIA Vol. 51” and a separate Vol. 28 item at KRW 39,900; the page identifies these as e-books but does not establish that either is the 2020 “Ayul 1st” release. [Puremedia product listing](https://puremedia.co.kr/mall/m_mall_list.php?ps_brand=&ps_company=&ps_ctid=01010000&ps_line=high&ps_max_money=&ps_min_money=&ps_page=1&ps_search=)
- The project source register limits reuse to sources with an explicit authorization compatible with the intended commercial catalog use; its NLK LOD route is usable for records covered by the documented open license, while an ordinary publisher or store page does not itself supply that grant. [Project source-policy register](../../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)

### Inferences
- Ayul is a strong creator-led lead because the cited report identifies her as a streamer and explicitly calls the release a digital photobook, but that report does not establish nationality and is not an authorized reusable bibliographic source.
- Puremedia’s listing gives useful discovery metadata, but it is not evidence that MuseBooks may commercially republish those facts under the source register’s standard. The two currently listed Ayul volumes should not be conflated with the “Ayul 1st” title without an exact-product link.

### Gaps
- No National Library of Korea LOD record or other explicitly commercial-reuse-licensed exact bibliographic record for the Ayul digital photobook surfaced in the searches for “아율,” “아율 1집,” and the Puremedia product title; this is a scoped search result, not proof that no record exists.
- The available sources do not establish Ayul’s Korean nationality, although they do establish a Korean-platform BJ/streamer identity.
- The 2020 article and Linktree do not supply edition-level date, page count, ISBN, or stable publisher product ID. The current Puremedia Vol. 28 and 51 entries are distinct listed products, and no reviewed source connects them to the 2020 title.
