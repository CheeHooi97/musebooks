# Korean Influencer/Model Physical Photobooks: Retailer Finds (2026-10-30)

## Which new physical retailer records are sufficiently specific to consider?

### Takeaway

Two exact Korean-market physical photobook records surfaced outside the exact-title matches in the existing workspace research files: **캔디 화보집 NO.1 꾸뿌** and **KIM YOUNG JU 김영주 화보집**. The first has a strong model/influencer identity link; the second has clear physical-POD and model evidence, but its author bio does not establish nationality or online-influencer status. Neither book's live-database duplicate status was queried in this research-only pass.

### Cited Findings

- Aladin's exact listing for **캔디 화보집 NO.1 꾸뿌** credits 김선광, publisher 라이트미디어, date 2025-03-14, 128 pages, 210 × 275 mm, and 243 g. It classifies the title as a men's magazine and offers a shipped print product. The listing's code is `9773059141000`, which it labels ISBN; the International ISBN Agency says ISBN prefixes are 978 or 979, while the ISSN International Centre documents 977 as the serial-publication barcode prefix. Keep this retailer code separate from a verified ISBN. [Aladin product record](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=360355817), [International ISBN Agency](https://www.isbn-international.org/index.php/content/what-isbn/10), [ISSN International Centre](https://www.issn.org/understanding-the-issn/issn-uses/identification-with-the-ean-13-barcode/)
- MAXIM Korea's own BCUT profile labels 꾸뿌 as a model and lists her creator-page photo works. Sports Seoul identifies 꾸뿌 as Lim Ji-eun, reports that she had 150,000 Instagram followers under `@ggu.bbu` in 2019, and describes her as a Miss Maxim model. A 2020 Sports Seoul report says she was selected as a Miss Maxim model in 2019 and was active on MAXIM Live. [MAXIM Korea BCUT creator profile](https://bcut.maximkorea.net/creator/28), [Sports Seoul, 2019](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/792965), [Sports Seoul, 2020](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/933701)
- Aladin's exact **KIM YOUNG JU 김영주 화보집** record credits Kim Young Ju and Nadawoom Books, dated 2022-12-05, with ISBN 9791197931567, 134 pages, A4 dimensions, and 362 g. Aladin explicitly labels it POD and explains that the product is made as a paper copy from a digital file on customer request; the listing currently marks it out of print. [Aladin product record](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=307017330)
- Aladin's author profile describes Kim Young Ju as a working mother, curator/cultural planner, housewife model and fitness model. It says this photobook records her model work from 2019 to 2022 and was made from sessions with 25 photographers. The source does not establish her nationality or an online following. [Aladin author profile](https://www.aladin.co.kr/author/wauthor_overview.aspx?AuthorSearch=%409123536)
- Exact-title/name searches across the repository's `docs`, `reports`, and `research_notes` returned no matching records for either candidate in this pass. This workspace check does not establish whether either title is already in the live database.

### Inferences

- **캔디 화보집 NO.1 꾸뿌** is a distinct physical issue featuring the MAXIM model 꾸뿌: the printed issue title names her, while the MAXIM creator page independently confirms the same stage name and model role. The 2019 follower count is historical, not a current count.
- **KIM YOUNG JU 김영주 화보집** is a physical, print-on-demand model photobook, not merely a digital release. It is a viable model-category lead, but needs an independent Korea-origin signal if the catalog's Korean attribution requires more than Korean-language domestic publication and the author's Korean-market biography.
- Keep `9773059141000` separate from ISBN. Its 977 prefix and magazine categorization make the retailer's ISBN label unsuitable for treating it as a verified book ISBN; no verified ISBN for the Candy issue was found.

### Gaps

- Check the live catalog for exact-title, alternate-title, barcode, and creator matches before import. This pass did not access the database.
- No authoritative statement about the nationality/residence of either creator was found in the cited product/creator pages. Kim Young Ju also lacks evidence here of an online influencer following.
- The exact contents and print binding of the Candy issue are not described in the accessible Aladin text beyond its title, magazine category, pagination, trim size, and weight.

## Which other retailer hits should remain on hold?

### Takeaway

**캔디 화보집 NO.2 옐랑** is an exact physical product listing but should remain a hold until the title's named model can be linked directly to a reliable creator identity. A public creator page for someone using the same romanized/stage name lists multiple other photobooks, but does not identify this Candy issue.

### Cited Findings

- Aladin lists **캔디 화보집 NO.2 옐랑**, credits 김선광 and 라이트미디어, gives 2025-11-13, 128 pages, 210 × 275 mm, 243 g, and places it in its men's-magazine category. [Aladin product record](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=376784306)
- 11st's listing identifies a new, shipped product from Light Media and gives the same 2025-11-13 date and 977-prefixed identifier `9773059141000`, corroborating a physical edition. [11st product record](https://www.11st.co.kr/products/8832503130)
- A creator link page titled **옐랑 yellang** links Instagram, CHZZK, YouTube, TikTok, and other photo products, including SWEET BOX and Movie Fantasy releases. It does not mention *캔디 화보집 NO.2* or establish that the Candy title's 옐랑 is this account. [Creator link page](https://litt.ly/lovelyelllang)

### Inferences

- The matching stage name is suggestive but insufficient to merge the Candy model with the creator at `@lovelyelllang`; keep the product as an unresolved identity lead rather than assigning that person's social accounts.

### Gaps

- Need a first-party publisher, creator, or retailer detail explicitly tying the Candy title to the identified 옐랑 account, and a reliable source confirming the creator's Korean origin or Korea-based creator status.
- No exact ISBN was verified; the retailer code is again `9773059141000` and displayed under an ISBN label, while ISBN/ISSN authorities describe 977 as a serial-publication barcode prefix rather than a book ISBN prefix. [International ISBN Agency](https://www.isbn-international.org/index.php/content/what-isbn/10), [ISSN International Centre](https://www.issn.org/understanding-the-issn/issn-uses/identification-with-the-ean-13-barcode/)

## What is known about duplicates and R2 image reuse?

### Takeaway

The candidates are new relative to the exact-title matches found in the checked workspace files, but live-catalog duplicates remain unverified. The retailer pages establish product metadata, not permission to mirror cover or interior images to R2; no images were downloaded, copied, or uploaded in this research pass.

### Cited Findings

- The earlier Korean research note records a different digital GGuBbu title, **꾸뿌 / 봄의물결**. That is a separate format/title lead and is not an exact-title match for the physical Candy issue. [Prior digital-title note](../Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2012/digital_titles.md), [Cupick digital product](https://cupick.jp/ko/products/ggubbu-springwaves)
- The cited Aladin and 11st product pages provide bibliographic/sales details for the physical titles but no statement licensing their product-cover or interior images for third-party R2 hosting. [Candy NO.1 listing](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=360355817), [Candy NO.2 listing](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=376784306), [Kim Young Ju listing](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=307017330)

### Inferences

- Treat GGuBbu's digital title and Candy printed issue as separate works only after a live duplicate check confirms that the same digital title was not already linked to the printed issue as an edition.
- Retail display of a cover does not itself grant R2 reuse permission. Keep media fields empty unless an asset-specific license or written authorization is documented.

### Gaps

- Live database duplicate status was not queried because this assignment was limited to research notes and prohibited imports.
- No cover-image license or written R2 authorization was found for any product in this pass.
