[Pure Media Vol. 339](https://puremedia.co.kr/mall/m_mall_detail.php?ps_ctid=01020000&ps_goid=362&ps_page=1); [CTEE Vol. 339 listing](https://ctee.kr/item/store/81189)

- **Yeha — [YEHA] 예하 VOL 321.** CTEE's exact product listing calls it a digital photo book, specifies a ready-made instant-download ZIP and 119 images, names the model as Yeha, links `@_yeha_y`, and labels the product country Korea. [CTEE Vol. 321](https://ctee.kr/item/store/54596); [Yeha's Linktree](https://linktr.ee/_yeha_y)

### Inferences

- **Record-readiness:** Yuna Vol. 314 and Vol. 339 are high-confidence exact digital products with a direct creator/model-profile match. Store country-of-origin supports classifying the products as Korean; it does not independently establish Yuna's nationality. Yeha Vol. 321 is high-confidence as a historical product and creator match, with current sale availability closed.
- The product descriptions count image files/photos, not printed or PDF pages; keep those fields distinct in any catalog entry.
