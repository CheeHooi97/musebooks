# Taiwan creator physical photobooks — round 15

## Which fresh creator-shop title has enough evidence for a new physical record?

### Takeaway

**Ely Cosplay’s 《L'été~VI》 Birthday photobook is a go candidate.** Her official shop sells a clearly specified printed book and supplies a single portrait cover artwork; her About page identifies her as Taiwan-born, and exact-title/creator queries return no MuseBooks record. The shop gives no ISBN or exact release date, so those fields should stay blank rather than be inferred.

### Cited Findings

- Ely’s official About page identifies her as a concept creator, designer, and model, says she was born in Taiwan and is based in Japan, and says she creates original cosplay. ([Ely About](https://www.eeelyeee.com/about))
- Her official shop offers **Photobook - 〖L'été~VI〗Birthday生日寫真書** for **NT$900** with Add to Cart/Buy Now controls. It specifies **B5, 64 full-color pages, gold-foil finish, and six cosplay sets**; it does not state an ISBN, publisher, preorder window, or publication date. ([direct shop product page](https://www.eeelyeee.com/product-page/l-%C3%A9t%C3%A9-vi-photobook-%E7%94%9F%E6%97%A5%E5%AF%AB%E7%9C%9F%E6%9B%B8))
- The product page’s main-image link is [this official Wix asset](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/8a95b1_049b9828487c41b1a784d703c550a30b~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_2576%2Ch_3624%2Cal_c%2Cq_90%2Cenc_avif%2Cquality_auto/8a95b1_049b9828487c41b1a784d703c550a30b~mv2.jpg). I downloaded and visually inspected it (local copy: `.tmp/r15/ely-l-ete-vi.jpg`): it is a single portrait design bearing “L'été~VI” and “Ely Cosplay,” not a multi-photo promotional collage. The retrieved JPEG is **2576 × 3624**, **996,195 bytes**, SHA-256 **95f94eaff133f2598844a8fd14631f99daa0270e0becfa7ed199ea3cb9d38d43**. It appears suitable as the catalog cover artwork; it is not a photograph of a printed copy. ([product page and labeled image](https://www.eeelyeee.com/product-page/l-%C3%A9t%C3%A9-vi-photobook-%E7%94%9F%E6%97%A5%E5%AF%AB%E7%9C%9F%E6%9B%B8); [image asset](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/8a95b1_049b9828487c41b1a784d703c550a30b~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_2576%2Ch_3624%2Cal_c%2Cq_90%2Cenc_avif%2Cquality_auto/8a95b1_049b9828487c41b1a784d703c550a30b~mv2.jpg))
- Live MuseBooks queries for the exact title and creator name both returned **total 0**: [L'été~VI](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=L%27%C3%A9t%C3%A9%20VI) and [Ely Cosplay](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=Ely%20Cosplay). A search of the current [Taiwan photobook catalog](../../scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md) also found no Ely/L'été entry.

### Inferences

- This is a creator-produced print photobook, not a retailer’s fan-made preview: the creator’s own shop sells the named 64-page B5 product and the same page links its title-matched image as the product image. The cover is strong enough for a new record if the catalog accepts self-published creator-shop books.
- Recommend **GO** for one Taiwan-origin physical edition, using the shop URL and image asset above. Leave ISBN and release date empty; the source does not provide them.

### Gaps

- The exact publication or preorder date is not stated on the live product page. The page currently permits checkout, but it does not explicitly confirm that a physical copy has already shipped. Do not assign a date or claim shipment without new evidence. ([direct shop product page](https://www.eeelyeee.com/product-page/l-%C3%A9t%C3%A9-vi-photobook-%E7%94%9F%E6%97%A5%E5%AF%AB%E7%9C%9F%E6%9B%B8))

## Which other fresh storefront lead should remain on hold?

### Takeaway

洪蓉’s **〖i級豔后〗寫真雜誌 Vol.02** is a credible direct preorder for a Taiwan creator, and MuseBooks has no matching record, but I could not verify its final cover image. Keep it on hold until the seller’s actual issue cover is accessible and matched to the product.

### Cited Findings

- The official [i級豔后 storefront](https://www.yenq888.com/) lists the creator’s photo products. Its item-specific [Vol.02 preorder page](https://www.yenq888.com/e950vr), indexed on the day of this scan, describes a **2026** color, glue-bound photo magazine, **24 pages**, **21 × 29.7 cm**, priced **NT$899** (listed against NT$999), with estimated shipping “around 11/10.” This is a magazine-like format rather than a standard ISBN book, and the ship estimate is approximate.
- A 2025 profile identifies 洪蓉 (formerly 馬友蓉) as a **Taiwan influencer** and describes her creator/model history. ([HK01 profile](https://www.hk01.com/%E5%8D%B3%E6%99%82%E5%A8%9B%E6%A8%82/60269413/%E5%8F%B0%E7%81%A3%E7%B6%B2%E7%B4%85%E7%82%BA-%E8%AA%87%E5%BC%B5%E8%BA%AB%E6%9D%90%E7%A8%B1%E8%99%9F-%E8%A8%BB%E5%86%8A%E5%95%86%E6%A8%99-%E5%87%BA%E9%81%9314%E5%B9%B4%E6%8B%8D%E5%BB%A3%E5%91%8A%E8%B5%B0%E7%B4%85))
- Live MuseBooks searches for [洪蓉](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E6%B4%AA%E8%93%89), [馬友蓉](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E9%A6%AC%E5%8F%8B%E8%93%89), and [級豔后](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E7%B4%9A%E8%B1%94%E5%90%8E) each returned **total 0**. The current local catalog also has no matching name/title.

### Inferences

- The direct listing and physical specifications establish a plausible upcoming issue, but the direct page could not be fetched by the web reader and the image search did not surface an item-specific final jacket. The search result alone is insufficient to distinguish final cover art from an announcement graphic.
- **HOLD**: do not create a record or upload a cover until the item page or creator confirms the final cover and the approximate shipment date is replaced by a reliable publication/fulfillment date. Consider it only if MuseBooks includes individual photo-magazine issues in scope.

### Gaps

- Final cover/image URL, ISBN (if any), confirmed publication date, and proof of shipment were not available in retrievable sources. The official product URL is direct, but the page fetch returned a cache-miss error during this check. ([Vol.02 product page](https://www.yenq888.com/e950vr))
- The listing calls itself a photo magazine; whether to catalog a 24-page creator issue as a photobook depends on the project’s format policy. ([Vol.02 product page](https://www.yenq888.com/e950vr))
