# Taiwan creator digital photobook retailer pass

## Are any newly surfaced digital titles safe Taiwan-influencer imports?

### Takeaway
No new candidate in this pass clears both creator-scope and product checks. The strongest unrecorded product lead is 李昊禹’s 2026 digital book, but its page establishes a fitness coach/model rather than an influencer and does not establish a Taiwan creator connection; keep it on hold.

### Cited Findings
- Pubu’s direct product page identifies **《李昊禹個人寫真書昇肌勃勃》**, released **2026-08-06**, as a paid DRM PDF with **126 pages** and platform ID **686431**; it names publisher mensuno tw / 子席國際股份有限公司 and displays no ISBN. The description calls 李昊禹 a former model and advertising face who is now a fitness coach. [Pubu product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/magazine/686431)
- Live MuseBooks searches for 李昊禹, 《昇肌勃勃》, and the Pubu product ID surfaced no record. [Creator query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E6%9D%8E%E6%98%8A%E7%A6%B9); [title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=%E6%98%87%E8%82%8C%E5%8B%83%E5%8B%83); [product-ID query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=686431)
- A second unrecorded digital listing, **《Gabriel：聶裕奇數位寫真》**, is an EPUB with EISBN **9786264001489** in the National Central Library deposit record. Google Play identifies the featured Gabriel as from Shanghai, so this is not evidence of a Taiwan-influencer photobook. [NCL record](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1715851520.pdf); [Google Play product](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=9z0EEQAAQBAJ); [MuseBooks title query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=Gabriel)
- PChome lists **《只因唯你 鄔唯數位寫真（含影音）》**, EISBN **9786263779211**, as a 2024-05-01 ebook from 尖端. The NCL deposit record uses the differing title **《唯你日記：嗚唯數位寫真》** for the same EISBN. FTV identifies 鄔唯 / VALENT as a Malaysian influencer, so this Taiwan-market ebook does not meet the Taiwan-creator scope. [PChome product](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBR5E-D900IPHMB); [NCL record](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1715851520.pdf); [FTV profile/report](https://www.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2023A17W0273); [MuseBooks EISBN query](https://musebooks.my/v1/books?q=9786263779211)

### Inferences
- Keep 李昊禹 as a hold until an independent source identifies him as a Taiwan-based creator/influencer; the existence of a Taiwan retailer listing and Taiwan publisher alone is insufficient. [Pubu product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/magazine/686431)
- Exclude the Gabriel and 鄔唯 ebooks from this Taiwan-influencer batch because their identified subjects are respectively from Shanghai and Malaysia, even though both are Taiwan-market digital products. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=9z0EEQAAQBAJ); [FTV](https://www.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2023A17W0273)

### Gaps
- I did not find a reliable profile or Taiwan connection for 李昊禹, nor a separate final-cover asset for the Pubu product. Its Pubu listing alone does not show an ISBN. [Pubu product](https://www.pubu.com.tw/magazine/686431)
- The NCL and PChome titles for EISBN 9786263779211 disagree on the written name/title; because the identified creator is Malaysian and the EISBN aligns, it is an exclusion rather than an import lead. [NCL](https://isbn.ncl.edu.tw/FCKEDITOR_UploadFiles/1715851520.pdf); [PChome](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBR5E-D900IPHMB)
