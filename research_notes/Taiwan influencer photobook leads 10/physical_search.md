# Taiwan creator physical photobook search

## New or uncatalogued creator physical books

### Takeaway

The strongest new record is the 2019 physical photo-documentary 《有你的旅程：黃氏兄弟寫真紀實》 (ISBN 9789577434555). It is a two-creator YouTuber/team title, rather than an individual book. The standard physical cover is now confirmed with high confidence from an item-specific photo of the physical book and matching publisher/date/page/size metadata; it is distinct from the deluxe cover and ebook image. The uncatalogued 《2020台灣百大網紅聯名寫真》 is a 100-person collective anthology, so keep it out of the individual-creator queue unless the catalog explicitly accepts anthologies.

### Inferences

- **Go for the standard physical edition of 《有你的旅程》**: the publisher/NCL records identify ISBN 9789577434555 as a 144-page Taiwan Kadokawa paperback, and the item-specific physical listing matches that title, publisher, release date, pagination and 18 × 25.6 cm size. Its inspected cover matches the ebook artwork and is different from the deluxe boxed cover. The direct physical image has the hash recorded above; the duo should be modeled as a collaborative featured act, not as one person. ([NCL record](https://nclfile.ncl.edu.tw/files/202010/a9f748fe-c927-4a3e-a5f7-a88f3645b323.pdf); [TCSB product record](https://www.tcsb.com.tw/9789577434555); [Yahoo physical listing](https://auctions.yahoo.co.jp/jp/auction/q415612784))
- **No-go under individual-only scope for 《2020台灣百大網紅聯名寫真》**: it is directly relevant to Taiwan influencers but is explicitly a hundred-person joint anthology, not a named influencer's personal book. The evidence is enough to retain it as a separate anthology lead if that collection scope changes.

### Gaps

- The Yahoo image listing does not visibly show an ISBN/barcode in the retrieved cover photo. The cover-to-ISBN match is therefore a high-confidence cross-source identification based on the exact title plus publisher, release date, pagination, size, paperback NCL record and visual cover design, rather than an ISBN legible in the cover pixels. The listing is a reseller's ended auction, not a current retailer stock offer; an official Taiwan Kadokawa standard-edition image would be preferable if later found.
- The hundred-person anthology's full list of contributors and a canonical cover asset were not verified. It should not be normalized as a single individual creator.

### Inferences

- **No-go on re-imports** for the named live records. New retailer pages or a different seller offer do not imply a missing work; edition-level differences should be checked only if a distinct ISBN/format/contents appear.
- 《有你的旅程》 is a distinct missing volume from already-cataloged sequel 《有你的夏天》. The 2020 top-100 title is a group anthology, and the Shopee storefront teasers are not enough to create reliable new catalog records.

### Gaps

- This was a targeted source and catalog pass, not an exhaustive census of all creator self-published photobooks. The Shopee title leads require item-specific seller pages and an independent creator/profile connection before consideration.
