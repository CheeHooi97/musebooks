# Kim Woo-hyun Nxde PhotoBook

## Was the 2023 Fanding reward a physical or digital photobook?

### Takeaway
The primary campaign record strongly supports a physical photobook: its reward tiers are delivery-enabled and separately offer the Mandarin and Cyber Punk photo books, while the creator’s Fanding community includes a post explicitly titled “paper photobook review.” A later creator post calls 2025’s *Extreme* her first paper photobook with WOOZIC STARZ, so that wording needs to be retained as a qualification rather than silently reconciled.

### Cited Findings
- Fanding identifies the 2023 project as **WOO HYEON'S Nxde PhotoBook**, creator “우현 woohyeon” / Kim Woo-hyun, in Photography. The page describes it as the model’s “first and last nude photobook.” [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/funding/140/)
- The campaign’s public reward data lists “[EP 1] Mandarin” and “[EP 2] Cyber Punk” photo-book rewards separately (one or two copies of either, or one or two copies of both); the reward records have `isDelivery: true`. The page labels the promised reward time as 2023-08-16 15:00. [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/funding/140/)
- The creator’s Fanding community has a post titled “📣우현의 종이 화보집 후기📣” (“Woo-hyeon’s paper photobook review”); it thanks supporters for joining her crowdfunding and invites photobook reviews. [Creator community post](https://fanding.kr/ja/@woohye0n/community/42256/)
- The creator’s later Fanding profile lists the April 15, 2025 post announcing *Extreme* as the first paper photobook made with WOOZIC STARZ, followed by shipping-related posts dated April 18 and May 20, 2025. [Creator profile and posts](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/)

### Inferences
- Delivery-enabled tiers containing named photo-book copies, together with the creator’s 2023 “paper photobook” post, are good evidence that the Nxde campaign’s reward was physical rather than digital-only.
- The 2025 phrase “first paper photobook with WOOZIC STARZ” may refer to that company collaboration, but the available wording does not conclusively explain how it relates to the 2023 campaign.

### Gaps
- The campaign’s displayed reward date is a planned dispatch deadline; I found no primary notice establishing the exact date the 2023 copies actually shipped or became available.
- The Fanding campaign does not state page count, ISBN, publisher/imprint, trim size, or a separate publication date. Exact-title searches of retail listings did not surface a matching bibliographic record; the Yes24 search result I checked instead showed unrelated Kim Woo-hyun/MAXIM items. [Yes24 search](https://www.yes24.com/Product/Search?query=%EC%9A%B0%ED%98%84+%EC%82%AC%EC%A7%84%EC%A7%91)
- No source consulted establishes permission to reuse the campaign’s cover or promotional images in R2; no image or paid file was downloaded.

## What publication and delivery metadata can be verified?

### Takeaway
The campaign ran from April 15 to June 15, 2023, and set August 16, 2023 at 15:00 as its reward dispatch due time. This is a scheduled fulfillment date, not proof of the actual release or delivery date; page count, ISBN, and publisher remain unverified.

### Cited Findings
- Fanding gives the project period as 2023-04-15 07:00 through 2023-06-15 14:59 and the “Reward Due date” as 2023-08-16 15:00. [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/funding/140/)
- Reward tiers identify the two components as EP 1 Mandarin and EP 2 Cyber Punk, but do not provide bibliographic identifiers or book specifications. [Fanding campaign](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/funding/140/)

### Inferences
- The campaign year 2023 is safe as the project year. It should not be used as an exact publication date unless the catalog explicitly records a campaign year rather than a publication date.

### Gaps
- Actual first-availability date and actual shipment date are not established by the campaign schedule.
- Page count, ISBN, publisher/imprint, and dimensions were not found in the campaign record or the retail search checked.

## Is Nxde distinct from Extreme, and does it meet catalog import criteria?

### Takeaway
Treat Nxde and *Extreme* as separate projects: the Fanding Nxde campaign is project 140 from 2023 with Mandarin/Cyber Punk reward components; the creator’s profile separately lists *Extreme* with WOOZIC STARZ in April 2025. The evidence meets the existence and physical-format threshold for recording a creator photobook project, but it does not support filling standard publication fields by guesswork.

### Cited Findings
- The 2023 campaign has its own title and project ID (140), and its reward tiers name Mandarin and Cyber Punk as separate components. [Fanding Nxde campaign](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/funding/140/)
- The creator’s profile lists the distinct title *Extreme* in a 2025 announcement with WOOZIC STARZ and separate delivery notices. [Creator profile and posts](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/)
- The wording “first paper photobook with WOOZIC STARZ” in the 2025 *Extreme* announcement is in tension with the 2023 “paper photobook review” post; available primary pages do not explain whether the former means the company’s first paper photobook or a different “first.” [Creator profile and posts](https://fanding.kr/@woohye0n/); [2023 creator community post](https://fanding.kr/ja/@woohye0n/community/42256/)

### Inferences
- This is not a duplicate of *Extreme*: the Fanding project title, project ID, reward components, and 2023 campaign dates differ from the separately named 2025 project.
- A conservative catalog entry can preserve the Fanding title and campaign citation and identify the project as a physical creator-photobook crowdfunding release. If the local importer requires an exact publication date, ISBN, publisher, or other missing edition metadata, those fields should remain unknown and the standard edition record should stay on hold until evidence is found. Whether to model Mandarin and Cyber Punk as two works or as two components of one campaign title depends on the catalog’s work/edition convention.

### Gaps
- The 2025 “first paper photobook” wording remains unresolved against the 2023 physical-delivery evidence.
- No ISBN, publisher, page count, exact publication date, exact completed shipment date, or R2 reuse permission was established.
