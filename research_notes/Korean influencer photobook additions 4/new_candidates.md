# Additional Korean influencer photobooks

## Which new products have primary product and creator evidence?

### Takeaway
The strongest additional match found is the **Rustic Photobook 24SS** crowdfunding product (Wadiz project 250208), with three physical model-specific versions featuring gymnast/influencer Kan Hyeon-bae, fitness model/influencer Kim Dong-yoon, and climbing influencer Shim Min-sup. The campaign page provides enough evidence to prepare a metadata-only catalog candidate, but live-database absence and the book's detailed bibliographic specifications remain unverified.

### Cited Findings
- Wadiz's Rustic Studio campaign names the product **“국가대표와 모델 등 20만 남성 인플루언서들의 바디화보 '러스틱 포토북'”** and describes a returning men's body-photo book for **24SS**, released in three versions; the three ambassadors are Kan Hyeon-bae, Kim Dong-yoon, and Shim Min-sup. The page calls the product a photo book and shows book mockups and physical reward items. [Wadiz project 250208](https://www.wadiz.io/web/campaign/detail/250208)
- The Wadiz campaign identifies Kan Hyeon-bae (간현배) as a Korean national-team gymnast and influencer, and states Rustic selected him as an ambassador in 2022; the campaign gives his Instagram handle as `@kan_hyeon_bae_`. [Wadiz project 250208](https://www.wadiz.io/web/campaign/detail/250208)
- The same campaign identifies Kim Dong-yoon (김동윤) as a fitness model and influencer with 120,000 followers, and Shim Min-sup (심민섭) as a climbing influencer with 130,000 followers. It provides their social handles as `@dxyxn___` and `@mn_55up`. [Wadiz project 250208](https://www.wadiz.io/web/campaign/detail/250208)
- The campaign ran from **2023-12-06 to 2023-12-20**. The page does not give a print/publication date; “24SS” is part of the product's season designation. [Wadiz project 250208](https://www.wadiz.io/web/campaign/detail/250208)

### Inferences
- This is a physical book campaign: the project is a photo-book reward with model-specific cover/mockup designs and mailed companion rewards; no digital edition is described. Its public campaign identifier is **250208**. No ISBN, pagination, publisher imprint beyond Rustic Studio, or exact ship/publication date was found on the accessible campaign page.
- A practical catalog candidate is one **Rustic Photobook 24SS** work with three clearly named model variants, if the catalog's edition model supports those variants. Keep the campaign date distinct from a publication date and leave pages/ISBN/date unknown.
- I searched current repository catalog-related files for `250208`, “Rustic,” “러스틱,” and all three creator names; that local search found no matches. This suggests the candidate is new to the reviewed local catalog artifacts, but it does **not** prove absence from the live database; an exact live title/product/person check is still required.
- The campaign's public photos and mockups establish provenance only. They do not grant permission to copy the images into R2.

### Gaps
- No live database duplicate query was run in this research-only task.
- The campaign page does not state pages, ISBN, publication/ship date, binding, or a publisher other than the campaign producer Rustic Studio.
- Creator follower counts are self-reported by the project's campaign page; I did not independently verify current social-platform counts.

## Which other exact photo-book leads still need creator or catalog confirmation?

### Takeaway
A second physical campaign product surfaced for drag performers Anessa and Hurricane Kimchi, but I have not established that either fits the intended influencer/social-media-creator criterion. Keep it as a lead rather than a verified influencer-catalog item.

### Cited Findings

Its campaign page says photo-book and poster materials were prepared for production and delivery. [Tumblbug project drag10](https://tumblbug.com/drag10)
- The Tumblbug page identifies Hurricane Kimchi as the project creator and says the project succeeded; the product page does not give an ISBN, page count, publication date, or clear influencer/social-platform identity evidence for Anessa or Hurricane Kimchi. [Tumblbug project drag10](https://tumblbug.com/drag10)

### Inferences
- This is a credible physical-photo-book product lead, but the available project page establishes drag-performance activity and a crowdfunding campaign, not the user's narrower influencer/social-media-creator eligibility rule. Verify identity fit and check the live catalog before considering an import.
- The campaign page and its images do not authorize R2 reuse.

### Gaps

- No exact live catalog duplicate check was run; the project identifier visible in the URL is `drag10`.
- Page count, ISBN, publication date, and edition/binding details are not provided on the campaign page.
