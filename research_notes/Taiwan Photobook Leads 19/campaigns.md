# Round 19 — Creator-store and crowdfunding sweep

## Newly surfaced and revisited creator campaigns

### Takeaway
Three creator campaigns surfaced or were revisited: Huan’s completed 2019 photo-book crowdfunding project, KANA’s active 2026 birthday-project book reward, and 小貓奈奈’s announced 2027 calendar photobook. I also rechecked the older Office State Of Mind campaign. KANA and 小貓奈奈 have Taiwan-origin support, but none of these four leads has a title-specific, usable cover asset; Huan additionally lacks verified Taiwan origin, so none is GO-ready this round.

- Primulav identifies itself as a Taipei-based idol group, and FTV describes KANA as Taiwanese and a graduate of National Taiwan University. [Primulav biography](https://www.primulav.com/biography), [FTV profile](https://www.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2025308L11M1)
