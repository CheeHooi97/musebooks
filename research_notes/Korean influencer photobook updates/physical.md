## Key question 2: What should be checked before adding or mirroring these records?

### Takeaway

The initial repository text search found no exact import-manifest title for Secret B No. 2, No. 3, digital No. 13, or digital No. 14. ### Repository and duplicate warnings

- The repository already has a manifest for physical Secret B No. 1 and digital `Secret B + MAXQ Compilation Vol. 03: Park Eunhye` ([manifest](../../docs/korean-secret-b-photobooks-import.json)), plus physical Secret B No. 4 Magarin ([manifest](../../docs/korean-photobook-magarin-secret-b-4-import.json)). The specific new titles above did not appear in the import-manifest search performed for this assignment.
- The repository already has digital `Secret B 16호- 백성혜 Special`, a 114-page PDF dated 2024-08-01 ([manifest](../../docs/korean-secret-b-16-baek-seonghye-import.json); the product listing is [RIDI](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000203)). This and the No. 3 physical Baek Seonghye photobook have the same subject/publisher/series and similar reported pagination (114 vs. 116 or 166). The work may be a digital counterpart/reissue, but content equivalence was not established. Compare contents before creating a new work; if equivalent, connect it as a separate edition.
- No exact-image reuse license or permission was found for these covers. The repo source guide says product-page images are not cleared for R2 merely because they are publicly displayed; leave cover/media URLs blank until permission is documented ([source guide](../../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)).
