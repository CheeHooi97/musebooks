# Korean creator/model photobooks: additional physical leads

## Which additional physical editions are close to import-ready?

### Takeaway

This pass found identifiable new physical photobook leads from Crazy Giant, especially the two-volume *Sujeong* set and Min Hanna’s *Flower Blooms*. Neither clears the requested import threshold yet: the accessible evidence does not provide a direct edition checkout/listing URL for the individual physical volume(s), and the identity link for “Sujeong” remains ambiguous. No title from this pass is recommended for import until those gaps are closed.

### Cited Findings

- Crazy Giant’s October 15, 2026 photo-book report says a two-volume *수정 화보집* (“Sujeong Photobook”) set was published, labels editions **SUJEONG VOL.1** and **SUJEONG VOL.2**, quotes prices of ₩88,000 and ₩99,000, and says the books are printed paper copies sold through the publisher’s official mall. It does not provide ISBN, page count, release date, or a directly accessible individual checkout page in the captured report. [Crazy Giant Sujeong photo-book report](https://crazygiant.co.kr/giantgirl/?bmode=view&idx=175104830)
- The same publisher report describes the subject as a model and includes an interview in which she speaks about modeling for the books. A separate profile page identifies **Minky (민키)**, whose name is given as **Min Soo-jeong (민수정)**, as an internet broadcaster and model; however, no independent source located in this pass explicitly confirms that this Minky/Min Soo-jeong is the “수정” on the Crazy Giant covers. Treat this identity match as unverified. [Crazy Giant interview/report](https://crazygiant.co.kr/giantgirl/?bmode=view&idx=175104830), [Minky profile](https://yongstyong.com/%EB%AF%BC%ED%82%A4-%ED%94%84%EB%A1%9C%ED%95%84-%EC%9D%B8%ED%84%B0%EB%84%B7-%EB%B0%A9%EC%86%A1%EC%9D%B8-%EB%AA%A8%EB%8D%B8/)
- Crazy Giant’s physical *THE PLAYER PHOTO BOOK* catalog/report says its series contains 22 paper volumes featuring 12 models. It names, among others, **서아 Vol.1**, **안정미 Vol.1**, **벨라가르텐 Vol.1–3**, and **수정 Vol.1–2**, with prices shown for those volumes. The source does not give direct individual product pages, publication dates, pagination, or enough independent identity evidence to resolve each model. [Crazy Giant THE PLAYER photo-book listing](https://crazygiant.co.kr/article/?bmode=view&idx=175099979)
- Xports reported on November 9, 2021 that Crazy Giant had published racing model **Min Hanna’s** standalone physical photo book **민한나, 꽃이 피다** (also rendered in the article as *꽃이 피다*), that it had been available for preorder through the Crazy Giant homepage, and that it had sold out roughly a month after publication. The article ties the model to the title and format, but the article links only to the publisher homepage rather than an exact edition product page; it provides no ISBN or page count. The workspace already contains a different Min Hanna title (*민한나 임팩트화보집 [2019]*), so this would be a distinct work if its edition record is recovered. [Xports News on Min Hanna’s *Flower Blooms*](https://www.xportsnews.com/article/1497472?ac=law)
- A 2017 NewDaily article says model **Lee A-young (이아영)** released her first personal photo book in 2012. It does not name the 2012 book or identify a matching product listing, so no record can be formed from this evidence alone. [NewDaily profile/collaboration coverage](https://biz.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2017/03/30/2017033010119.html)

### Inferences

- The Crazy Giant Sujeong volumes and Min Hanna’s *Flower Blooms* appear to be new works relative to the workspace import manifests scanned for this pass. Their exact title/edition matches should still be checked against the live database before any future import.
- The Sujeong set may refer to the Min Soo-jeong/Minky streamer-model, but shared given name and a publisher-side interview are not enough to establish that identity with confidence.

### Gaps

- Exact individual edition purchase/product URLs for *SUJEONG VOL.1*, *SUJEONG VOL.2*, and *민한나, 꽃이 피다* were not established.
- No ISBN, pagination, or release date was found for the Sujeong volumes or Min Hanna’s book. The publisher report is dated October 15, 2026, but that is the report date, not a confirmed publication date for the volumes.
- No independent source unambiguously connects the Sujeong volume subject to Min Soo-jeong/Minky.
- Lee A-young’s 2012 book title and any exact edition listing remain unknown.

## Which other physical leads should be excluded or held?

### Takeaway

Crazy Giant has a wider creator/model photo-book catalog than the workspace currently reflects, but the sources found are series-level descriptions or ambiguous first-name model references. They are useful search leads, not import-ready bibliographic records.

### Cited Findings

- Crazy Giant’s own report identifies a dedicated physical *THE PLAYER PHOTO BOOK* series and says 22 volumes are on sale, with individual model labels and prices. This supports existence and physical format at series level; the listing is not a direct edition product page for each volume. [Crazy Giant THE PLAYER photo-book listing](https://crazygiant.co.kr/article/?bmode=view&idx=175099979)
- Crazy Giant promotes a separate **[THE PLAYER] Barbie Vol.1** photo book, but the available page does not establish the model’s unique identity or a distinct individual product record. Search results for “Barbie” are heavily confounded with the Mattel character and unrelated books, so it is held rather than attributed to a Korean creator. [Crazy Giant homepage](https://www.crazygiant.co.kr/)
- Crazy Giant also currently sells a digital PDF titled *e-BOOK_민한나의 철공소* and states “28pic.” This is outside this physical-books research scope and is not treated as a physical title here. [Crazy Giant exact digital product page](https://crazygiant.imweb.me/shop_view/?idx=881)

### Inferences

- Stable creator identity and direct per-volume edition pages are the practical next evidence needed for the Crazy Giant series; first-name or stage-name labels alone risk merging different people.
- The *Barbie Vol.1* title may refer to a model alias, but current search evidence does not support a safe identification.

### Gaps

- No exact direct product-page URLs for individual *THE PLAYER* print volumes were recovered from the publisher’s series report.
- The subject behind *Barbie Vol.1* remains unidentified from available sources.

## Do any sources authorize putting the exact cover images in R2?

### Takeaway

No. The available Crazy Giant source explicitly reserves copyright in its text and photographs and prohibits copying or distribution; product availability and article/cover display do not grant permission to mirror the images to R2.

### Cited Findings

- Crazy Giant’s Sujeong report says “이 기사에 실린 사진과 글의 저작권은 크레이지자이언트에 있습니다. 무단 복제·배포를 금합니다” (the photos and text are copyrighted by Crazy Giant; unauthorized reproduction/distribution is prohibited). [Crazy Giant Sujeong photo-book report](https://crazygiant.co.kr/giantgirl/?bmode=view&idx=175104830)
- The Crazy Giant *THE PLAYER* listing is publisher-owned sales/editorial material and gives no image-reuse license. [Crazy Giant THE PLAYER photo-book listing](https://crazygiant.co.kr/article/?bmode=view&idx=175099979)

### Inferences

- No cover or interior image from these sources should be copied to R2 without a license or written authorization covering the exact asset and hosting use.

### Gaps

- No image license, permissions statement, or written authorization for R2 hosting was found for any candidate in this pass.
