# Impact Crew Lead Resolution and Related Photobooks

## Which unresolved Impact Crew leads and adjacent standalone releases have verifiable product records?

### Takeaway
The local import manifests contain no matching records for Lee So-young, Kim Min-ji, UNEED, or the four digital releases below; the prior reports say the existing Impact Crew imports are other works. Retail and publisher records verify all three physical leads as products, but the edition/person caveats differ. I also found four exact, paid Impact Crew PDF photobooks that appear absent from the workspace imports and have stable ISBNs and dates. ([Batch 10 report](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2010.md); [Batch 11 report](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2011.md); workspace search of 80 import manifests, 2026-10-30.)

### Cited Findings
- **Lee So-young (이소영), physical lead:** Kyobo's creator search lists *임팩트(Impact): 이소영 화보집*, publisher Impact Crew, dated 2019-12-27, and links product ID S000001980674. Singoro's used-book listing for *임팩트 Impact: 이소영 화보집* gives Impact Crew, 2019, and 144 pages, but no ISBN or dimensions. ([Kyobo exact product](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000001980674); [Kyobo Impact Crew search](https://search.kyobobook.co.kr/search?chrc=%EC%9E%84%ED%8C%A9%ED%8A%B8%ED%81%AC%EB%A3%A8&keyword=%EC%9E%84%ED%8C%A9%ED%8A%B8%ED%81%AC%EB%A3%A8); [Singoro listing](https://www.singoro.com/store/book_info.php?no=1173906)).
- The Lee records do not yet resolve into one edition: YES24 search results list *IMPACT LEE SOYOUNG* in August 2019 and *이소영 임팩트화보집 [2019]* in December 2019, while Kyobo has the December 27 record. Separately, Sports Seoul reported in December 2018 that model Lee So-young's *Impact* photo book had recently sold out. These establish her connection to an Impact photo book but do not prove whether the 2018, August 2019, and December 2019 catalog entries are the same edition, a reissue, or separate work. ([YES24 Impact Crew search](https://www.yes24.com/product/search?company=%EC%9E%84%ED%8C%A9%ED%8A%B8%ED%81%AC%EB%A3%A8+&domain=ALL&query=%25EC%259E%2584%25ED%258C%25A9%25ED%258A%B8%25ED%25AC%25AC%25EB%25A3%25A8%2520); [Sports Seoul / Daum, 2018-12-17](https://v.daum.net/v/20181217151613563)).
- **Kim Min-ji (김민지), physical lead:** Youngpoong's exact *임팩트 김민지 화보집* page lists Impact Crew, 2019-12-11, ISBN-13 9791196797829, 152 pages, portrait-photography category, and a single-volume physical book. Kyobo lists the matching title and date under product ID S000001980673. ([Youngpoong exact product](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202308233955929488); [Kyobo exact product](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000001980673)).
- Kim Min-ji's physical dimensions are not reliable enough to normalize: Youngpoong says “other size”; YES24's used listing says 255 × 265 × 20 mm, while Aladin's used listing says 225 × 265 mm. Impact Crew's own Vimeo VOD list has a title-specific *[HOT] 김민지 IMPACT 화보집 영상*, tying a publisher-produced shoot to the book subject name, but searches did not find an independent press/profile or first-party creator account that distinguishes this Kim Min-ji from people with the same name. ([YES24 used listing](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/127088573); [Aladin used listing](https://www.aladin.co.kr/m/mproduct.aspx?ItemId=242217112); [Impact Crew Vimeo VOD list](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages)).
- **UNEED (유니드), physical lead:** Kyobo lists *임팩트(Impact): 유니드(UNEED) 2019년11월*, Impact Crew, dated 2019-11-08, product ID S000000330470. Aladin has a title variant, *임팩트화보집 유니드*, dated 2019-10-18, 144 pages, 225 × 265 mm, and code 9772672079004 in its ISBN field. YES24 search results also show *IMPACT VOL.1 UNEED* dated August 2019 and *유니드 임팩트화보집 [2019]* dated September 2019. The 977-prefixed number is not a valid ISBN-13; the International ISBN Agency says ISBN-13 uses a 978 or 979 prefix. ([Kyobo exact product](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000000330470); [Aladin exact product](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=212290261); [YES24 Impact Crew search](https://www.yes24.com/product/search?company=%EC%9E%84%ED%8C%A9%ED%8A%B8%ED%81%AC%EB%A3%A8+&domain=ALL&query=%25EC%259E%2584%25ED%258C%25A9%25ED%258A%25B8%25ED%2581%25AC%25EB%25A3%25A8%2520); [International ISBN Agency](https://www.isbn-international.org/index.php/content/what-isbn/10)).
- MoneyS identifies UNEED as a model and influencer (about 100,000 followers in March 2019), describes her underwear brand and reports an Impact Crew photo shoot. This verifies a creator/model identity for the subject, though retail date variants still prevent confidently merging every UNEED listing into one edition. ([MoneyS, 2019-03-16](https://www.moneys.co.kr/article/2019031215108075508)).
- **Four exact digital works from YES24, all sold as DRM PDF eBooks:**
  - *정유승 임팩트크루 웹화보집* — 2019-12-01, ISBN 9791196797867, 59.11 MB. The product description names models Jeong Yoo-seung, Shin Saerom, and Jung Da-yeon and the contents list sections beginning on pages 6, 56, 128, and 190. ([YES24 product 87731916](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731916)).
  - *걸크러쉬 임팩트크루 웹화보집* — 2019-12-01, ISBN 9791196797874, 61.76 MB; described as previously unpublished Girl Crush photos and Q&A, with contents naming Yona, Jia, Bomi, and Taeri. ([YES24 product 87731917](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731917)).
  - *안정미 임팩트 웹화보* — 2020-06-05, ISBN 9791196797881, 85.43 MB; product description calls An Jeong-mi a former model and says it contains unreleased photos. ([YES24 product 90474558](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474558)).
  - *레이샤 고은 임팩트 웹화보* — 2020-06-05, ISBN 9791196797898, 288.67 MB; product description identifies Goeun as a former member of girl group Laysha and describes unreleased photos. ([YES24 product 90474556](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474556)).
- The digital storefront records explicitly say the files are PDF eBooks, provide their dates and ISBNs, and distinguish them from shipped print books. Their file sizes and table-of-contents page starts are present, but none of these product pages gives a definitive page count. ([YES24 Girl Crush](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731917); [YES24 Jeong Yoo-seung](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731916); [YES24 An Jeong-mi](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474558); [YES24 Laysha Goeun](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474556)).
- Independent sources support the credited creator identities for these adjacent digital titles: Sports Kyunghyang identifies Jeong Yoo-seung as a fitness model and says Impact Crew released her named photo book; Gwanak FM describes Girl Crush as a four-member group of Jia, Bomi, Yona, and Taeri; Crazy Giant's interview names those four members and links their accounts; Star News identifies Goeun as a Laysha member and reports her own announcement of a photo release. Kyobo's Impact Crew book description lists model An Jeong-mi among people participating in Impact Crew's photo-book program. ([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/201904111050003); [Gwanak FM, 2019-04-23](https://m.radiogfm.net/news/15151); [Crazy Giant interview](https://crazygiant.co.kr/article/?bmode=view&idx=5829593); [Star News, 2021-02-23](https://www.starnewskorea.com/music/2021/02/23/2021022311030817945); [Kyobo Impact: Jeong Yuna](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000001976016)).
- A 2016 Segye Ilbo report also identifies Laysha member Goeun as a model featured in an Impact webzine collaboration, but it is a different dated project and does not establish that its images are in the 2020 eBook. ([Segye Ilbo, 2016-08-17](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20160817000896?UA=PC)).

### Inferences
- The YES24 eBooks are the strongest new import candidates in this pass: each has an exact title, publisher, date, ISBN, and digital format. The multi-model Jeong Yoo-seung volume should retain all three named subjects if imported; the group eBook should be represented as one group work and member credits only if the database supports that accurately.
- Kim Min-ji's edition metadata is importable independently of whether the database creates a resolved person profile; do not merge the name into another Kim Min-ji without an identity source.
- A cautious UNEED record could use the exact Kyobo title/product and leave ISBN and pagination blank; do not normalize the August/September/October/November variants into one edition without more publisher or ISBN-registry evidence.
- Lee So-young's product existence and model identity are supported, but the late-2019 Kyobo/used-book record should not silently inherit facts from the 2018 or August 2019 record.

### Gaps
- The direct Kyobo pages for Lee, Kim, and UNEED did not expose all bibliographic fields in the available web capture; no verified ISBN was found for Lee or UNEED.
- Lee's multiple dates/titles and UNEED's multiple dates/title variants lack an identifier or publisher statement that proves which listing is the same edition or a reissue.
- Kim Min-ji's independent creator/model identity and exact physical size remain unresolved.
- YES24's group digital edition names the individual members, but no official or press record found here confirms that the named 2019 lineup and the product's image contents were all the same session. Product metadata itself does confirm the group title and member section names.
- None of this research established a live-database duplicate check; the coordinator should check live Works/editions before any import.

## Do reliable sources tie the titles to the named models, influencers, or creator group?

### Takeaway
Lee So-young, UNEED, and the subjects in the four digital editions have identity evidence from contemporary press, an independent group interview, or the product's publisher page. Kim Min-ji remains the one unresolved individual identity; the publisher ties the name to a shoot but independent identification is lacking.

### Cited Findings
- Sports Seoul identifies Lee So-young as a model and says her recently released *Impact* photobook had sold out. The report predates Kyobo's December 2019 listing by a year, so it cannot alone identify that later edition. ([Sports Seoul / Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20181217151613563)).
- MoneyS describes UNEED as a model/influencer and reports an Impact Crew photo shoot, making the subject likely the public creator named in the retail record. ([MoneyS](https://www.moneys.co.kr/article/2019031215108075508)).
- Sports Kyunghyang identifies Jeong Yoo-seung as a fitness model, says she released an Impact photobook, and gives her prior occupation as a flight attendant and Pilates instructor. ([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/201904111050003)).
- Gwanak FM's 2019 interview identifies Girl Crush as a group consisting of Jia, Bomi, Yona, and Taeri; the YES24 eBook table of contents contains those same names. ([Gwanak FM](https://m.radiogfm.net/news/15151); [YES24 product](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731917)).
- YES24's own descriptions identify An Jeong-mi as a former model and Goeun as a former Laysha member; independent Kyobo/press sources also place An Jeong-mi in Impact Crew's modeling/photo-book roster and Goeun in Laysha's photo/model activity. ([YES24 An Jeong-mi](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474558); [Kyobo Impact title](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000001976016); [YES24 Laysha Goeun](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474556); [Star News](https://www.starnewskorea.com/music/2021/02/23/2021022311030817945)).
- Impact Crew's official Vimeo page has a Kim Min-ji-specific Impact photobook video title, but no reliable independent profile or creator account was found to disambiguate the subject. ([Impact Crew Vimeo VOD list](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages)).

### Inferences
- If importing Kim Min-ji's verified work, preserve the exact Korean name and product evidence but avoid merging it with an existing person record until that identity is independently resolved.
- Group titles are catalogable works; how to represent Girl Crush as a featured group versus four individual member credits depends on the catalog's entity model.

### Gaps
- No independent subject identity was located for Kim Min-ji beyond the first-party production title.
- Laysha Goeun's 2020 eBook product identifies her as a former member; the specific 2020 edition's shoot participants are not independently named outside the product page.
- The available sources establish public professional/model activity, not legal names or present-day social handles for all subjects.

## Do any cited sources grant permission to copy an exact cover or interior image to R2?

### Takeaway
No source found in this pass grants asset-specific permission for R2 hosting. Keep all newly researched candidates metadata-only unless the publisher, photographer, or rightsholder licenses the exact asset and the proposed hosting/display use.

### Cited Findings
- YES24 labels the four digital products as paid, DRM-protected PDF eBooks and explicitly notes that printing is disabled for copyright protection; the listings do not grant redistribution or third-party hosting rights. ([YES24 Jeong Yoo-seung](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731916); [YES24 Girl Crush](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/87731917); [YES24 An Jeong-mi](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474558); [YES24 Laysha Goeun](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/90474556)).
- Retailer covers/previews, Impact Crew's Vimeo VOD pages, and press photographs identify public product/media, but the cited pages contain no license for copying specific images to an external R2 bucket. ([Impact Crew Vimeo VOD list](https://vimeo.com/impctcrew/vod_pages); [Youngpoong Kim Min-ji listing](https://www.ypbooks.co.kr/books/202308233955929488); [Aladin UNEED listing](https://www.aladin.co.kr/shop/wproduct.aspx?ItemId=212290261)).

### Inferences
- A public sales listing, preview image, or paid download is evidence of the work's existence, not a license to mirror its artwork or pages.

### Gaps
- No written permission for the exact cover, preview, PDF page, or video frame and R2 use was found; no image assets were downloaded or uploaded.
