# Korean creator-led digital photobooks

## YooSeorin / Seorin: creator-controlled digital photo magazine releases

### Takeaway
Two additional creator-led digital photo publications are documented by YooSeorin’s first-party Patreon and linked RINI BOOKS store: *RINIQ Vol.6 — Velvet Murmur* and *O.N.O #09 Halloween (Spicy Ver.)*. They are strong title/format candidates; the public material uses the Korean-model label, but I found no reliable independent source establishing a legal name or citizenship, so retain only the public persona and do not infer either.

### Cited Findings
- The Patreon product post is published under **YooSeorin**, titled *RINIQ Vol.6 — Velvet Murmur*, says a download link is at the bottom, links to RINI BOOKS for purchase without membership, and describes two versions: Lite (49 pages) and Original (77 pages). The public page lists a ZIP and two PDF attachments and guest purchase prices of $23.99 and $28.99. [RINIQ Vol.6 product post](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/riniq-vol-6-ver-157047855)
- The creator-controlled profile page “It’s YooSeorin” identifies the account as `@seorin.is`, links its TikTok and Patreon, and links directly to the RINI BOOKS magazine store and both *Velvet Murmur* variants. [YooSeorin profile and store links](https://litt.ly/its.yoo.seorin)
- The first-party *RINIQ* page tags the work “Korean Glamour Magazine,” “Korean Model,” and “한국 화보집.” This supports the creator’s public Korean-model positioning, not citizenship. [RINIQ Vol.6 product post](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/riniq-vol-6-ver-157047855)
- A separate first-party Patreon product page, *O.N.O #09 Halloween (Spicy Ver.)*, describes a 60-page digital package, provides a direct RINI BOOKS Gumroad purchase link, and lists its limited sale window as October 21–24; its attachments include a description PDF and the Spicy PDF. [O.N.O #09 product post](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/o-n-o-09-spicy-172438442)
- The *O.N.O #09* page names YooSeorin, describes two Seorin concepts in one issue, and tags the publication “Korean Glamour Magazine” and “Korean Model.” It says the Spicy cuts are exclusive to purchasers and not added to later membership access. [O.N.O #09 product post](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/o-n-o-09-spicy-172438442)
- Workspace duplicate scan: `docs` and `reports` contain the prior LOVEYS Vol.2/Vol.3 import, but no batch import/read-back record for *RINIQ Vol.6* or *O.N.O #09*. Report 10 cites *RINIQ Vol.6* as creator-publication context, not as an imported work. This is a local-file scan only; a live database duplicate lookup is still required before import. [Batch 10 report](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2010.md), [Batch 10 manifest](../../docs/korean-influencer-photobooks-batch-10-import.json)

### Inferences
- Treat *RINIQ Vol.6* as one work with Lite and Original digital editions if the live catalog has no duplicate; the first-party listing presents them as versions of the same issue.
- *O.N.O #09* appears to be an expired limited sale campaign as the listed October 21–24 window has passed. The product post remains accessible, but current standalone availability of its Gumroad item was not confirmed.
- The creator-controlled profile makes the link between the public persona and store direct. Do not add a legal name or nationality absent better evidence.

### Gaps
- The *O.N.O #09* page does not state the year beside its sale dates in the captured text, and its Gumroad page could not be independently fetched in this pass.
- No independent press/profile source confirming YooSeorin’s model identity or Korean nationality was found. First-party Korean-model tags are the basis for the provisional project fit.
- No exact cover or interior asset was licensed for third-party storage or display.

## GGuBbu (꾸뿌): new Cupick digital title; retailer authorization needs confirmation

### Takeaway
Cupick lists a new 50-page digital title, *봄의물결* (“Spring Waves”), featuring GGuBbu (꾸뿌). Independent Korean press confirms 꾸뿌 is a public MAXIM model, while the product page identifies her Instagram handle and classifies her as a Korean model; the page does not identify the seller or document the creator’s authorization of Cupick, so treat it as a research lead until the store relationship is confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Cupick’s live Korean product page titles the digital photo book **꾸뿌 / 봄의물결**, prices it at ¥2,000, lists 50 pages, says it can be read in the browser after purchase, and credits model 꾸뿌 (`ggu.bbu2`) and photographer YURAYURA. It gives no publication date. [Cupick product page](https://cupick.jp/ko/products/ggubbu-springwaves)
- The product is presented in Cupick’s “Korean model” digital category. The page says buyers access it through “My Books” and can reread it in the browser; it does not offer a downloadable copy. [Cupick product page](https://cupick.jp/ko/products/ggubbu-springwaves)
- ChosunBiz reports that 꾸뿌 participated in the 2019 Miss Maxim Contest and worked as a MAXIM model; the article connects the public stage name 꾸뿌 to her model work. [ChosunBiz, 5 February 2024](https://biz.chosun.com/entertainment/enter_general/2024/02/05/IBGTMK2J3XIGZ55MEYKUNMXHHU/?outputType=amp)
- Workspace duplicate scan: no *봄의물결*, *Spring Waves*, or Cupick product URL appears in the checked `docs` or `reports`. A different BLUECAKE/GGuBBu product, *[꾸뿌] 美人*, was already mentioned as an unimported lead in Report 10; do not treat that older product as newly discovered. [Report 10](../../reports/Korean%20influencer%20photobooks%2010.md)

### Inferences
- The product title, model credit, Korean-model category, purchase flow, and independent MAXIM coverage together identify a Korean public model and a distinct digital photo-book product. This supports recording the public name 꾸뿌/GGuBBu without asserting a legal name or citizenship.
- Cupick appears to be the retail platform rather than the creator’s own store: the page has a “Sell Your Book” section and does not name the uploader. The exact listing is evidence of a product for sale, but not standalone proof of authorization by the model or photographer.

### Gaps
- Confirm who listed the Cupick book and whether Cupick has authorization from the creator/photographer before treating this as import-ready under the creator-owned/authorized-store criterion.
- No exact release date, publisher credit, ISBN, or explicit asset reuse license is stated on the product page.

## R2 reuse rights for the researched digital products

### Takeaway
No exact cover or interior image is cleared for R2. Keep all findings metadata-only unless the creator, photographer, or rights holder gives permission covering the specific image and third-party hosting.

### Cited Findings
- BLUECAKE’s already-known *[꾸뿌] 美人* Gumroad listing explicitly says purchased photos are for private collection only, prohibits unauthorized distribution/sharing/transmission, and asserts BLUECAKE copyright. That statement rules out copying its paid images to R2 under the listed terms. [BLUECAKE/GGuBBu product](https://s2bluecake.gumroad.com/l/ggubbu016)
- YooSeorin’s first-party product pages describe paid, member-only, or purchaser-exclusive downloadable material; the pages grant no public reuse, redistribution, or hosting rights for their images. [RINIQ Vol.6](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/riniq-vol-6-ver-157047855), [O.N.O #09](https://www.patreon.com/Riniverse/posts/o-n-o-09-spicy-172438442)
- Cupick provides a paid, browser-based reading entitlement for *봄의물결*, but its product page does not grant reuse or republication rights for the product images. [Cupick product page](https://cupick.jp/ko/products/ggubbu-springwaves)

### Inferences
- Retail availability, public previews, a Gumroad download, Patreon attachment, or browser-reader access does not by itself authorize mirroring the asset in R2.
- Do not use page thumbnails as substitutes for licensed cover art; no image files were downloaded or uploaded during this research.

### Gaps
- No asset-specific permission or license allowing R2 hosting was located for any title covered here.
