# Taiwan digital influencer photobook candidates

## Which new digital photobook records are verified?

### Takeaway
The current Taiwan catalog has no `良良` heading, while Taiwan-facing ebook listings and Google Play identify a two-volume digital photobook series, 《良良-善良的良》. Both volumes have retailer records and cover-image targets, but the listings disagree about video inclusion and publisher attribution, so those distinctions need to be preserved during any import. ([Taiwan photobook catalog](../../scraper/taiwan-photobook-catalog.md); [BOOK☆WALKER series search](https://www.bookwalker.com.tw/search?detail=1&order=rank&restricted=2&series_display=1&v=58); [Google Play, volume 1](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=KPGZEQAAQBAJ); [Kobo Taiwan, volume 1](https://www.kobo.com/tw/zh/ebook/oxu45AY-nzOxIbD1wIbS_g))

### Inferences
- The evidence supports two separate volume-level digital photobook records. The video and no-video listings may represent distinct digital variants of each volume; use each specific retailer title and format rather than treating video inclusion as settled. ([Google Play, volume 1](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=KPGZEQAAQBAJ); [Kobo Taiwan, volume 1](https://www.kobo.com/tw/zh/ebook/oxu45AY-nzOxIbD1wIbS_g); [Google Play, volume 2](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=5vGZEQAAQBAJ); [Kobo, volume 2](https://www.kobo.com/hk/en/ebook/oYDDjsx9vziK3QZ23b4idQ))
- Kobo calls `7020044020313` and `7020044020314` “Book ID,” whereas PChome labels the same numbers “ISBN.” Treat them as platform identifiers unless an ISBN/EISBN source confirms otherwise. ([Kobo Taiwan, volume 1](https://www.kobo.com/tw/zh/ebook/oxu45AY-nzOxIbD1wIbS_g); [PChome, volume 1](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBQ2E-D900GO493); [Kobo, volume 2](https://www.kobo.com/hk/en/ebook/oYDDjsx9vziK3QZ23b4idQ); [PChome, volume 2](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBQ2E-D900GQ8WB))

## Does 良良 fit the Taiwan-influencer scope?

### Takeaway
The available evidence supports 良良 as a social-media creator whose five-second weightlifting clip went viral, and confirms Taiwan-market editions from a Taiwan-based publisher/distributor. Her nationality or place of residence is not explicitly established by the sources checked, so describe her as a Taiwan-market creator candidate rather than asserting Taiwanese nationality. ([Google Play, volume 1](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=KPGZEQAAQBAJ); [PChome, volume 1](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBQ2E-D900GO493); [Next Apple report](https://news.nextapple.com/life/20221118/C5D61EEDCFD66D13ECC210C5A45C31C8))

### Cited Findings
- The Google Play author section identifies the model as 良良, gives her Instagram handle as `y61126`, and says she became popular after a five-second weightlifting video. ([Google Play, volume 1](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=KPGZEQAAQBAJ))
- Next Apple’s Taiwan entertainment/lifestyle report identifies an Instagram workout reel supplied by `y61126`; the article is dated November 18, 2022. ([Next Apple report](https://news.nextapple.com/life/20221118/C5D61EEDCFD66D13ECC210C5A45C31C8))
- The profile linked as `@y61126` provides Taiwan credit-card sponsorship links, while the Kobo/PChome listing specifies Taiwan publication and a Taiwan digital-only product. ([Linktree profile](https://linktr.ee/y61126); [PChome, volume 1](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/books/prod/DJBQ2E-D900GO493))

### Inferences
- “Influencer” is supported by the product-page account of a viral social video and the news report’s Instagram reel reference; the source evidence is consistent with a creator photobook rather than a generic photography title. ([Google Play, volume 1](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=KPGZEQAAQBAJ); [Next Apple report](https://news.nextapple.com/life/20221118/C5D61EEDCFD66D13ECC210C5A45C31C8))
