# Ran Photobook Rights Check

## Public authorization for bibliographic metadata reuse

### Takeaway
KADOKAWA's public product page supplies exact facts for *蘭 らんファースト写真集* (release date 2023-03-29, B5, 112 pages, ISBN 9784047374355) and describes Ran as a Korean influencer. The reviewed public pages do not expressly license those facts for reuse in a commercial catalog; this is an absence of a grant, not a legal determination that bare facts are protected. Under the project's source-use register, keep KADOKAWA unapproved for commercial metadata reuse unless the rightsholder confirms that scope or another authorized source supplies the fields.

### Cited Findings
- KADOKAWA's product listing names the title and states its date, format, pagination, ISBN, paper-book format, and an electronic edition; its promotional copy identifies Ran as a Korean influencer and reports 2.3 million social followers in 2023. These are the publisher's page statements, not current follower data. [KADOKAWA product record](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/322301000031)
- KADOKAWA's works-use policy says unauthorized use of its published works, including text and images, is not accepted and provides separate contact routes for individual and corporate/organizational users seeking to use publications. It does not state a license or reuse terms specifically for factual bibliographic metadata. [KADOKAWA works-use policy](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/copyrightpolicy/)
- The project source-use register says not to mark a source approved until written permission specifies intended pages, collection frequency, fields, attribution, image use, storage/hotlink conditions, and related requirements. [Project source-use register](../../docs/korean-model-photobook-sources.md)

### Inferences
- The product page is strong evidence for edition identity, but it is not itself a public commercial-metadata license. Reusing only factual fields may differ from reusing the page's expressive description; this check does not resolve the legal treatment of facts. The project's documented gate nevertheless does not treat the page as permission.

### Gaps
- No explicit KADOKAWA metadata-reuse license, open-data grant, or written permission for MuseBooks commercial catalog reuse was found on the reviewed product and rights pages. No inquiry was sent.

## Exact-cover R2 storage and public delivery

### Takeaway
KADOKAWA's current cover-use policy does not clear this exact cover for MuseBooks' commercial public catalog or R2 storage/delivery. Its no-application cover exception is limited to introducing KADOKAWA books; the policy directs cover uses outside book-introduction purposes, explicitly including commercial use, to its corporate/media form. That route is an inquiry, not an approval.

### Cited Findings
- The corporate/media cover policy limits use to introducing KADOKAWA books. It says uses for other purposes, including commercial use, should be submitted through its media/corporate form; television, video, and internet distribution are also directed to that form. [KADOKAWA media and corporate cover policy](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/support/b/media/)
- For permitted introduction use, the policy allows only cover images that can be saved from KADOKAWA's official product page, requires the bibliographic credit `『作品名』 著者名　ＫＡＤＯＫＡＷＡ／レーベル名`, prohibits cropping/compositing, and specifies using the jacketless image. This narrow permission does not authorize commercial catalog display or establish R2 storage/public delivery rights. [KADOKAWA media and corporate cover policy](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/support/b/media/)
- KADOKAWA's currently linked special-conditions PDF was checked for ISBN 9784047374355 and the exact title; neither matched. The list says special conditions may apply to certain titles, so absence from this list only means no title-specific special condition was found there; it does not broaden the base permission. [KADOKAWA special cover conditions (PDF)](https://media-cdn.kadokawa.co.jp/ijwyneak/hgdliokbjr/shoei.pdf)
- The product page visibly links cover images for this edition, but the existence of a publisher-hosted image establishes provenance, not permission for the requested R2 use. [KADOKAWA product record](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/product/322301000031)

### Inferences
- The policy's commercial-use exception is the decisive scope issue for the requested catalog. Even if an exact image meets the page's right-click criterion, the default route still does not authorize the commercial use described here; written approval must identify this cover and the intended R2 storage and public delivery. No image was downloaded or uploaded.

### Gaps
- No separate public license for this exact cover, written MuseBooks approval, or public terms covering storage, resizing, CDN/R2 delivery, and commercial catalog display was found. KADOKAWA's media/corporate form is the published inquiry route; no inquiry was sent.

## Sources and method

### Takeaway
This was a read-only review of KADOKAWA's public product and rights pages, the current special-conditions PDF, and the project's source-use register.

### Cited Findings
- The product and cover-policy pages identify the proper publication and image-use inquiry routes; this review did not contact the publisher. [KADOKAWA works-use policy](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/copyrightpolicy/); [KADOKAWA media and corporate cover policy](https://www.kadokawa.co.jp/support/b/media/)

### Inferences
- KADOKAWA has not granted permission merely by hosting the product page or providing a cover-use policy for narrower book-introduction uses.

### Gaps
- The rights holder's response on commercial metadata use and exact-cover R2 use remains unknown.
