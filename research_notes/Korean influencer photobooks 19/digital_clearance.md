# One New Korean Creator Digital Photobook Candidate

## Is there an exact creator-owned digital photobook that qualifies for catalog import?

### Takeaway
Jeon Yoonyoung’s first-party portfolio names *JEON YOONYOUNG HEART IN LOVE* and offers a digital ebook edition with immediate access after Gumroad payment. This is an exact product lead, but the evidence does not establish Jeon as an influencer, and neither the portfolio nor Gumroad terms clearly permit commercial reuse of product metadata; do not import it in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- The artist’s own website identifies the publication as “JEON YOONYOUNG HEART IN LOVE,” calls it Jeon’s first photo book, and describes it as a book about love, distance, youth, and awkward sincerity. ([Creator’s photobook page](https://jeonyoonyoung.com/photobook/))
- The same page offers a “Digital / Ebook edition,” says Gumroad payment makes the file available immediately, and links its “지금 읽기” button to the creator’s Gumroad product. ([Creator’s photobook page](https://jeonyoonyoung.com/photobook/))
- The portfolio describes Jeon’s work as portrait photography and lists commissions/publication work, exhibitions, and editorial features. It locates selected works in Seoul, KR, but does not describe Jeon as an influencer or establish nationality. ([Creator’s About and portfolio page](https://jeonyoonyoung.com/photobook/))
- The portfolio footer shows “© 2026 Jeon Yoonyoung”; the reviewed page contains no catalog-data license or permission to reuse its text or images commercially. ([Creator’s photobook page](https://jeonyoonyoung.com/photobook/))
- Gumroad’s Terms of Service prohibit commercial exploitation of the Services or a portion of them and prohibit manual or automated processes to scrape or download data from Gumroad pages, with an exception for public search-engine indexing. The Terms do not grant third-party commercial catalogs a metadata-use license. ([Gumroad Terms of Service, §14](https://gumroad.com/terms))
- The portfolio includes four preview-page images and links to the digital edition, but no notice licensing those exact images for R2 storage and public delivery. ([Creator’s photobook page](https://jeonyoonyoung.com/photobook/))

### Inferences
- Because the page is first-party and explicitly names the book and digital format, the title/format/delivery claim is stronger than a retailer-only discovery result. It still does not establish that the creator meets the project’s “influencer” scope or grant permission for commercial catalog reuse. ([Creator’s photobook page](https://jeonyoonyoung.com/photobook/); [Gumroad Terms of Service](https://gumroad.com/terms))
- “Seoul, KR” supports a Korea-based professional context for the portfolio but is not evidence of citizenship or influencer status. ([Creator’s photobook page](https://jeonyoonyoung.com/photobook/))
- Do not copy the preview images or cover to R2 without explicit rights-holder permission for the exact image, storage, and public delivery. ([Creator’s photobook page](https://jeonyoonyoung.com/photobook/))

### Gaps
- No reviewed first-party or authoritative source establishes Jeon Yoonyoung as a Korean influencer; the available first-party evidence presents Jeon as a portrait photographer.
- No statement or license was found that permits commercial reuse of the page’s factual metadata, and Gumroad’s Terms constrain commercial use of data from its pages. Obtain permission or a clearly authorized metadata source before catalog import.
- The live database was not queried because this candidate did not clear creator-scope and metadata-use gates. No manifest/import/read-back was created.
- No R2 upload was made; explicit reuse permission for a specific cover/preview image and public delivery is absent.
