# Additional Korean creator photobook candidates

## Which additional catalog titles have enough evidence to consider?

### Takeaway
Three catalog titles beyond the seven imported works and four held leads in the current report meet a product-and-creator evidence threshold: *Secret B* Vol. 11 (Jeong Hanna), *MAXQ Special Edition* Vol. 1 (Gong Minseo, Son Sohee, Choi Seolhwa), and *MAXQ Special Edition* Vol. 10 (Park Eunhye). These are product-level candidates only; no live MuseBooks duplicate search or image-level comparison was performed.

### Cited Findings

#### Verified product candidates

- **Digital — 맥스큐 디지털화보집 Secret B Vol.11 정한나 (MAXQ Secret B Vol. 11, Jeong Hanna), e-book/PDF, Aladin lists August 2022.** Aladin’s Secret B series catalogue lists this exact title, model, PDF format, and 2022-08 publication month ([Aladin Secret B series](https://www.aladin.co.kr/m/mseriesitem.aspx?srid=927494)). Sports Seoul reports the Secret B No. 11 shoot with Jeong Hanna on 2022-06-13 ([Sports Seoul](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/1132455)). Maeil Business identifies her as a “fitness influencer” in an August 2021 photo-shoot report ([MK/매일경제](https://www.mk.co.kr/news/entertain/10007223)). This title/person is not among the seven imported works or held leads in the current research report.

- **Digital — MAXQ Special Edition 1호 (Vol. 1), “머슬여제 3인방” Gong Minseo, Son Sohee, Choi Seolhwa; e-book/PDF, e-book released 2020-08-03 (print listed 2020-07-31), 50 pages.** RIDI’s exact product record names all three models, publisher, dates, PDF format and pagination, and describes the series as an online photo collection of selected MAXQ photos ([RIDI product](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000054)). The Big Data News describes Choi Seolhwa as a fitness model and TV personality and says she was sharing content through her YouTube channel ([The Big Data News](https://www.thebigdata.co.kr/view.php?ud=202007161721581840992c130dbe_23)). This title/person is not in the seven imported works or held leads in the current report.

- **Digital — MAXQ Special Edition 10호 (Vol. 10), Park Eunhye; e-book/PDF, released 2024-06-27, 33 pages.** RIDI’s exact product page names Park Eunhye, gives the publisher, release date and PDF page count, and describes the series as an online photo book of selected MAXQ images ([RIDI product](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000201)). Sports Seoul identifies Park as a YouTuber and the operator of the 자몽튜브 channel ([Sports Seoul](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/948176)). This exact title is not listed among the seven imported works or held leads, but Park already appears in the report’s physical Secret B No. 1 and digital Secret B Vol. 5. The new Special Edition is a different retail product; its image content has not been compared with those existing books.

#### Weak leads / hold for follow-up

- **Digital — 맥스큐 디지털화보집 Secret B Vol.6 백성혜 (MAXQ Secret B Vol. 6, Baek Seonghye), e-book/PDF, 2020-11.** Aladin lists this exact PDF title, model, publisher, and month in the Secret B series catalogue ([Aladin Secret B series](https://www.aladin.co.kr/m/mseriesitem.aspx?srid=927494)); MAXQ coverage describes Baek as an influencer ([MAXQ Baek Seonghye tag page](https://maxq.kr/tags/baekseonghye?page=2)). This title is not in the current report’s seven imports or four held leads, but the same model already has imported Vol. 16 and physical Secret B No. 3 is a held lead. Image overlap remains unverified, so compare contents before creating another work/edition record.

- **Digital — “MAXQ Special Edition 8호” identity conflict.** A June 2022 Aju Economy report says Baek Seonghye and Choi Inhye were the cover models for a planned July 2022 “Special Edition: MAXQ SE” No. 8 and calls them influencers ([Aju Economy](https://www.ajunews.com/view/20220604233643912?photoIdx=0&term=0)). RIDI’s current product record for *MAXQ Special Edition* No. 8 instead identifies Jo Aera, with an e-book release date of 2023-04-25, PDF, 33 pages ([RIDI product](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000166)). The issue number, subject, and date conflict; do not treat the 2022 and 2023 product references as one verified title without resolution.

- **Digital — Secret B Vol. 4 (Lee Yejin) and Vol. 7 (Lee Jongeun).** Aladin lists the exact titles and month dates (2020-05 and 2021-01) in its series catalogue ([Aladin Secret B series](https://www.aladin.co.kr/m/mseriesitem.aspx?srid=927494)); press articles document the Secret B shoots ([StarNews via Daum on Lee Yejin](https://v.daum.net/v/20200331213743949); [Newsen on Lee Jongeun](https://newsen.com/news_view.php?uid=202009231203515510)). Reviewed sources describe them as dancers/models or fitness models, but did not establish either as a creator/influencer under the current inclusion bar; eligibility needs better evidence.

### Inferences
- The three verified product candidates are different numbered titles or a separate MAXQ photo-book series, so the retail products themselves are distinguishable from the report’s seven records. That does not establish unique image content or absence from the live database.
- For Park Eunhye’s Special Edition 10, existing books featuring the same creator make an image-level duplicate check especially useful. For Baek Seonghye’s Vol. 6 and Special Edition No. 8, overlapping subjects and conflicting product metadata warrant a hold.
- Public product listings and publisher/press page access do not establish permission to copy a cover or PDF to R2.

### Gaps
- I did not query MuseBooks’ live database; report comparison is limited to the report’s seven imported records and its three held leads.
- I did not inspect or compare paid PDF contents; photo-set overlap is unknown for the same-person candidates.
- I found no well-sourced Tumblbug creator photobook candidate in the searches run. Cover-image reuse rights remain unverified for every candidate.
