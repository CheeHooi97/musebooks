# Korean Secret B overlapping print and digital issues

## Secret B Vol. 14: Choi Sohyeon vs. physical Secret B No. 2

### Takeaway
Both products and Choi Sohyeon's influencer eligibility are documented. They are distinct publisher products, but the available metadata does not establish whether Vol. 14 is a new photo set or a digital reuse of the physical No. 2 shoot; compare the image sequences before deciding whether they belong to separate works or to one work with print and digital editions.

### Cited Findings
- RIDI identifies *Secret B 14호-최소현* as MAXQ Digital Photobook Vol. 14, a 41-page PDF e-book published by ㈜맥스큐인터내셔널 on 2023-11-24. [RIDI Vol. 14](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000182)
- MAXQ's publisher coverage identifies the physical Secret B No. 2 as a standalone Choi Sohyeon photobook, due 2022-12-23, with a Jeju shoot and exclusive photo cards. [Sports Seoul, 2022-12-09](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/1184574)
- Twig24 describes Choi as an Instagram influencer in its 2020 profile. [Twig24](https://www.twig24.com/news/entertainments/2020/09/20/20200920500023)
- MAXQ later separately labels Choi's digital product as *Secret B* 14, corroborating that the RIDI item is an independently numbered digital issue. [MAXQ Secret B publisher tag](https://maxq.kr/tags/sikeuritb)

### Inferences
- Treat Vol. 14 and physical No. 2 as separate product records. The title, issue numbering, format, and publication timing differ, but product identity alone does not prove different underlying photographic content.
- If the catalog's work entity represents the photo set, link the print and digital editions to one work only if full-page comparison shows they reuse the same set; create distinct works if the photosets materially differ.

### Gaps
- Compare all obtainable, authorized interior pages from the 41-page RIDI PDF with the physical No. 2 book or its publisher-authorized preview. Covers and the shared model name are insufficient to prove image reuse. The digital listing is paid and the cited public sources do not expose the complete contents.
- RIDI gives a 2023-11-24 e-book publication date, while MAXQ's 2024 article reports Vol. 14's Google Play Books chart performance; the latter does not establish that 2024 was the original release date. [RIDI Vol. 14](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000182); [MAXQ Secret B publisher tag](https://maxq.kr/tags/sikeuritb)

## Secret B + MAXQ Compilation Vol. 02: Yoon Na-gyeom vs. Secret B Vol. 9

### Takeaway
Vol. 02 is demonstrably a separate digital compilation product, not a second edition identified as Vol. 9. Yoon's influencer status is supported. The publisher says the compilation combines selected and unreleased cuts, so overlap with Vol. 9 is possible; compare interiors to identify reused images, not to decide whether the separately titled compilation product exists.

### Cited Findings
- RIDI identifies *<시크릿 B+맥스큐> 합본호 Vol.02: 윤나겸* as a 65-page PDF e-book from 2022-06-02, published by ㈜맥스큐인터내셔널. Its description says it combines best and unreleased cuts from the Secret B photobook and MAXQ magazine. [RIDI Compilation Vol. 02](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000137)
- RIDI separately lists *Secret B 9호-윤나겸* as a digital photobook; the current catalog research records its 42-page PDF publication on 2021-09-28. [RIDI Vol. 9](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000107)
- MAXQ describes Yoon as one of the influencer participants affiliated with Beauty & View in its 2021 coverage, alongside the other named fitness personalities. [MAXQ publisher coverage](https://maxq.kr/tags/inpeulrueonseo?page=3)

### Inferences
- Record Vol. 02 as its own compilation work with its own digital edition. It is a different named and numbered product with a distinct publication date and a stated cross-publication compilation scope; the publisher description does not call it a reissue of Vol. 9.
- Image-level reuse from Vol. 9 remains plausible because Vol. 02 includes selected Secret B cuts. Any matching photographs should be linked or deduplicated at the media level after comparison, without merging the compilation's product identity into Vol. 9.

### Gaps
- Compare the 65-page compilation PDF with the 42-page Vol. 9 PDF page by page, using lawfully accessible publisher or retailer copies/previews. The public description does not identify which Secret B issues contributed images, and the full PDFs are paid products. [RIDI Compilation Vol. 02](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000137); [RIDI Vol. 9](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000107)

## Physical Secret B No. 3: Baek Seonghye vs. digital Secret B Vol. 16

### Takeaway
The physical No. 3 and digital Vol. 16 are distinct publisher products with different issue numbers, formats, and dates; Baek's influencer status is documented. They can be entered as separate product records, but whether to create separate works or attach both editions to one work depends on an interior-image comparison, which the current evidence does not supply.

- RIDI identifies *Secret B 16호-백성혜 Special* as a MAXQ digital-only photobook, a 114-page PDF e-book published on 2024-08-01. [RIDI Vol. 16](https://ridibooks.com/books/3563000203)

- The 114-page digital length and conflicting 116/166-page print records cannot establish content identity. A same-work/two-edition relationship should be used only if interiors show the same underlying photo set; otherwise use distinct works.

### Gaps
