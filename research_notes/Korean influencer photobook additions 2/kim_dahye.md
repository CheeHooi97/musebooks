# Kim Dahye / Secret B Vol. 13

## Does the featured Kim Dahye qualify as a Korean influencer or creator?

### Takeaway
The evidence strongly links the Secret B subject to a fitness content creator/model featured in a Korean-language photobook, rather than only to a same-name person. MAXQ identifies her Instagram handle and publishes her exercise tips, but the sources reviewed do not explicitly label this particular Kim Dahye an “influencer”; treat creator eligibility as supported with moderate confidence and strict influencer labeling as unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Sports Seoul reported that Kim Dahye shot the MAXQ June 2023 cover and Secret B digital photobook No. 13, and described her prior MAXQ/Musclemania contest placements. [Sports Seoul](https://www.sportsseoul.com/news/read/1303488)
- A MAXQ-authored profile identifies @dhyeello2 as Kim Dahye’s Instagram in its image credits, describes her as a Pilates/weight-training instructor and fitness model, and publishes her exercise tips and May 2023 contest results. This is direct publisher attribution of a social account and creator-style fitness material, not a follower-count or explicit influencer claim. [MAXQ profile, syndicated on Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/VWH0AnkUCo)
- MAXQ’s own July 2023 story links Kim Dahye to Secret B No. 13 and reports the same 2023 domestic sports-model grand prix and GalleryK award, as well as her 2022 MAXQ Model Contest MVP. This overlaps with the MAXQ profile and Sports Seoul’s account of the photobook subject. [MAXQ Secret B release article](https://maxq.kr/articles/meoseulmania-woldeu-bikini-chaempieon-gimdahye-sikeuritbi-hwabojip-chulgan); [MAXQ contest coverage](https://maxq.kr/articles/2023-meoseulmaniareul-bitnaen-yeongyeui-juingongeun-nuguilkka)
- A same-name Allure Korea yoga influencer has a different handle (@kathy0805) and career profile; I found no link connecting her to this photobook, so that result should not be used as evidence about the Secret B model. [Allure Korea](https://www.allurekorea.com/2016/09/08/%EA%B9%80%EB%8B%A4%ED%98%9C%EA%B0%80-%EC%A0%84%ED%95%98%EB%8A%94-%EC%B2%99%EC%B6%94-%ED%97%88%EB%A6%AC%EB%A5%BC-%EA%B0%95%ED%99%94%ED%95%98%EB%8A%94-%EC%9A%94%EA%B0%80-%EB%8F%99%EC%9E%91/)

### Inferences
- The overlap in name, Secret B cover assignment, MAXQ contests, and the same 2023 awards makes the @dhyeello2 profile a strong match for the photobook subject; it is not a match based on name alone.
- The cited Korean publication and domestic competition context supports treating this as a Korean-market fitness creator/model title. The sources I reviewed do not expressly state her nationality.

### Gaps
- No authoritative source found in this pass explicitly calls this specific Kim Dahye an influencer or uses the creator label; creator eligibility is inferred from the MAXQ-attributed Instagram content and exercise tips. I found no independently verified audience size. If catalog inclusion requires an explicit influencer label, evidence remains short of that rule.
- Nationality is not directly stated in the reviewed primary/authoritative sources.
