# Korean-model catalog goal

## Goal

Build a source-verified MuseBooks catalog of Korean-model standalone photobooks and MAXIM magazine issues. Record publication and issue dates, model and cover credits, list and ordinary sale prices, used asking prices, availability at the time each offer was observed, and product images only when the exact asset is permitted for the intended use. Keep the two publication types distinct from discovery through import and display.

## Current baseline

This goal was recorded on 2026-11-01. After the permission/date/price migrations were applied, a new read-only cumulative audit on 2026-11-01 found 110 Korean works, 208 editions, 372 listing-item references, and 136 explicit media references, with zero ledger-integrity findings and zero people missing a verification source. This matches the earlier 2026-10-25 cohort: 72 photobook works with 79 editions and 38 magazine-issue works with 129 editions, including 45 digital and 84 physical issue editions. The current audit reports 140 editions without an active listing, 5 editions without any listing, 178 edition cover URLs missing, 7 legacy release dates missing, 60 legacy release dates not day precision, 129 issue dates missing, 101 physical publication dates missing, 107 digital store-release dates missing, and 85 page counts missing. The permission audit found zero Korean source grants, zero active retained routes in all four scopes, and zero runtime-permitted images among 166 media rows linked to this cohort; across the whole catalog it found zero permitted images among 36,353 media rows. These are evidence/completeness gaps; active status remains historical, and the audit does not establish complete market coverage, current stock, source permission, or image rights. The source map documents additional scoped photobook imports through 2026-10-30.

The database already distinguishes photobooks from magazine issues. The publication-type migration requires an issue month for magazine issues and forbids it for photobooks; importer checks require a sourced cover-person credit for a magazine issue and a featured model for a photobook. Existing records include monthly MAXIM issues and B-SIDE #4/#5 as magazine issues, while standalone MAXQ B-cut pictorials remain photobooks. Separate print, PDF, A/B/S cover, and retailer editions are represented where verified. This is the existing baseline, not a claim that every Korean publication or MAXIM issue has been covered.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Keep publication types separate.** Every work has a verified type of photobook or magazine issue. A magazine issue has an issue month; record an issue number when a source supplies one. A photobook has no issue month. Attach an A/B/S print or digital edition to the matching issue work only when that exact product or variant is verified. B-SIDE remains an issue where the product is an issue even if a seller calls it a photo book. A standalone MAXIM-model pictorial with no issue identity remains a photobook. Duplicate checks use exact title, ISBN or product ID, publisher, format, and edition variant before import.
2. **Store source-backed dates with their meaning and precision.** Preserve magazine issue month and its typed issue date separately from an edition's publication and digital/store release dates. Each typed date has its own source URL and day/month/year precision; do not replace conflicts or copy one variant's date to another. The old `releaseDate` remains for previously captured records and is rejected for new Korean imports because its meaning is ambiguous.
3. **Store role-specific person credits.** Link a photobook subject as a featured model and keep photographer credits distinct. Link a magazine cover model only when a source explicitly identifies that person as the cover model or the cover itself prints that credit. Keep supplement, lineup, and pictorial subjects out of the cover role unless the source assigns that role.
4. **Make every price and availability observation auditable.** Add explicit price bases for list, ordinary sale, conditional coupon, used asking, and completed sale amounts. A single product observation can retain simultaneous list/sale/coupon components; used asking and completed sale are single-amount observations. Retain currency, exact source or seller-item URL, seller ID where present, condition, shipping, observed timestamp, availability assertion, and supporting evidence. Keep aggregate used-price counts separate from individual offers. Availability is current only for a fresh observation under an approved route; old or cached observations stay historical or unknown.
5. **Record source permissions as structured scope.** A source permission record identifies the approved source and method, fields, purpose, automation/rate limits, retention, display and redistribution rights, attribution and linking terms, effective period, and permission-evidence reference. Import only the fields and use modes the recorded grant allows. KOLIS-NET record-level rights markers must be retained; KPIPA and YES24 restrictions must be enforced at their approved scopes.
6. **Gate every image by exact-asset permission.** Store an image's exact URL or asset identity, rightsholder/source, allowed use, storage and thumbnailing rights, attribution, territory, duration, and evidence. Runtime image delivery must require an applicable grant; URLs kept for provenance alone are not public media. Covers and other product images stay absent from display until this gate passes.
7. **Verify each import without changing source meaning.** Persist field-level source URLs and capture time, validate the publication-type invariants, preserve separate editions and observations, and perform a read-back audit for exact work/edition identity, source links, dates, credits, prices, status evidence, and image permission state. A successful integrity audit does not itself approve the source or any image.

Work-level and edition-level covers plus permitted samples can carry these permissions in import manifests. The permission and date/price migrations have since been applied to the configured catalog, but the audit confirms that every media row (36,353 across all catalog regions) remains blocked and no route has a durable grant. ## Source grant enforcement update — 2026-11-01

The catalog now has a `source_permissions` grant model separate from `Source.AccessStatus`. A grant is tied to one source and one scope (`bibliography`, `current_prices`, `used_prices`, or `product_images`) and records its method, allowed fields and uses, purpose, permanent/temporary/no-retention mode, rate limit, display mode, attribution, redistribution permission, rightsholder, territory, evidence URL, review time, and effective dates. Korean sources must have an active approved grant for the scope and fields requested; scrape jobs are checked when created and again before a worker claim, and observation batches are checked before storage. Temporary-cache grants cannot authorize the current durable-import path. Product images still also require the separate exact-asset media approval.

No source grants were inserted or approved in the configured catalog. This is deliberate: no reviewed MAXIM, used-price, YES24 bulk-import, or image grant exists, and a data.gov.kr dataset-level license label does not establish permission for every record's third-party content. KOLIS-NET remains the only evidenced bibliographic route for potentially eligible records: the official public-data listing covers books and periodicals, says use restrictions are none, and shows automatic approval for development and operation, while its traffic remains subject to the provider's policy. Its route still needs the appropriate API service key, record-level rights review, and a field-scoped source grant before MuseBooks can collect or retain anything through its Korean-source pipeline. It does not supply sale/used availability or image permission. ([KOLIS-NET public-data listing](https://www.data.go.kr/data/3036346/openapi.do))

The additive `20261101_source_permissions_v1` migration depends on the earlier media-permission migration. On 2026-11-01 the migration CLI applied the pending catalog migrations to the configured `musebooks` database. ## Typed date and price modeling update — 2026-11-01

The catalog model now carries magazine `issueDate`, edition `publicationDate`, and `storeReleaseDate` with field-level source URL and precision fields. Issue month remains an independent magazine identity field; importer validation rejects issue identity on standalone photobooks and rejects ambiguous legacy `releaseDate` on new Korean imports. Typed dates are normalized to calendar dates according to their stated precision and are available in catalog responses and date filtering.

Listing observations can now keep concurrent `list`, `sale`, and `coupon` amounts in `priceComponents`, with a primary amount for existing consumers. `used_asking` and `completed_sale` are constrained to physical unsold and confirmed sold observations respectively. Korean price observations require an explicit basis and currency; price meaning flows through listing history and the active/sold projections.

The migration is `20261101_date_price_semantics_v1`, following the media and source permission migrations. It adds typed dates and price fields, updates the listing lifecycle trigger, and preserves old `releaseDate` data without guessing its meaning. It is now applied to the configured catalog; the new fields do not approve any collection route, price source, or image.

## Permission-route refresh — 2026-11-01

The official route pages were rechecked. KOLIS-NET is the only route with published terms that support a limited MuseBooks bibliographic collection: its data.go.kr listing covers books and periodicals, marks the use range unrestricted, and says development and operation access is automatically approved. The National Library API still requires an issued API key, applies the provider's traffic policy, and returns bibliographic fields such as title, creator, publisher, publication year, ISBN/control number, plus `lic_yn` and `lic_text` rights indicators. The general data.go.kr policy says public datasets containing third-party rights still require the right holder's permission. Therefore treat KOLIS-NET as a terms-cleared bibliographic route only; preserve and review its per-record rights indicators, and do not treat them as an image license or a blanket right to republish underlying works. ([KOLIS-NET listing](https://www.data.go.kr/data/3036346/openapi.do), [National Library KOLIS-NET API guide](https://www.nl.go.kr/NL/contents/N31101030400.do), [data.go.kr use policy](https://www.data.go.kr/ugs/selectPortalPolicyView.do))

The current official API guide specifies `https://www.nl.go.kr/NL/search/openApi/searchKolisNet.do`; requests need the issued `key`, `pageNum`, `pageSize`, and a keyword or detailed-search criteria. It lists `title_info`, `type_name`, `author_info`, `pub_info`, `pub_year_info`, `control_no`, `isbn`, `lic_yn`, and `lic_text` among the response fields. For MuseBooks this supports title/publisher/ISBN matching and year-precision dates only. Do not turn `author_info` into a model credit; KOLIS supplies no cover-model role, current price, availability, or reusable product image. The portal labels use as unrestricted and development/operation application as automatic approval, while the API still requires an issued key and provider traffic policy; record-level rights flags remain part of screening. ([official KOLIS-NET API guide](https://www.nl.go.kr/NL/contents/N31101030400.do), [data.go.kr KOLIS-NET listing](https://www.data.go.kr/data/3036346/openapi.do))

For KOLIS-NET records, only capture source-supported bibliographic identity fields. `pub_year_info` supports a year-precision publication date; it does not establish a day-level release date. The general listing does not prove that a particular MAXIM monthly issue is cataloged or identify its cover model, and its creator field must not be relabeled as a model credit without an explicit role source. It provides no current or used price, live availability, or reusable cover image. The local workspace has no KOLIS/National Library API key configured and the source grant has not been registered in the MuseBooks database; although the permission/date/price migrations are applied, this route is not yet an active MuseBooks import route.

## Importer field-level source-permission gate — 2026-11-01

The importer now requires a `sourceEvidence` manifest section for every Korean work and every record that supplies source evidence. Each entry names the registered route, exact cited URL, capture time, entity, and field keys it supports. The importer derives the required field set from the work, edition, model identity and role credits, typed dates, covers, and publisher/work image rows; it rejects missing, extra, duplicate, or mismatched evidence. Each cited URL must match the registered route's allowed hosts. In the same import transaction, each route registered as Korean must have a reviewed grant for the exact bibliography or product-image fields at both capture time and import time, before it writes catalog data. It then stores one auditable evidence row per field. Existing listing observations continue through the separate ingestion grant checks.

For a MAXIM listing sourced from a retailer while bibliographic facts come from MAXIM Korea, the manifest can attribute listing observations to the retailer and bibliographic fields to the publisher route independently. Cover/model credits can cite a separately verified credit page. Image fields need both a Korean route's `product_images` grant and the exact-asset media approval; source attribution alone does not make an image displayable. Captures made before a Korean grant was reviewed, outside its effective period, or after it expires are rejected for durable import.

The additive `20261101_catalog_source_evidence_v1` migration creates the field evidence ledger and was applied to the configured `musebooks` database. The read-only audit now reads back field evidence, checks each source route and field scope, and reports evidence coverage by work and edition. The current cohort has 0 evidence-backed works out of 110 and 0 evidence-backed editions out of 208, because these records predate the importer gate; they are not retrospectively grant-approved. The same audit found zero approved Korean source grants, zero usable Korean routes, and zero permitted Korean media, so no Korean record was imported. The audit is saved at [korean-catalog-coverage-audit-2026-11-01-after-source-evidence.json](korean-catalog-coverage-audit-2026-11-01-after-source-evidence.json).
