# MuseBooks logo study

Three original logo directions created from the MuseBooks product brief. These are standalone, editable SVG lockups; each uses its own mark, typography, and palette.

1. **Editorial M** — a sculpted monogram with a coral glint. Editorial and expressive.
2. **Collector's Index** — a modular index mark in cobalt and citron. Clear and app-friendly.
3. **Orbit & Glint** — a discovery orbit with a warm spark. Curated and visual.

The files in this folder are self-contained and do not depend on the existing logo artwork. Open `logo-study.html` in a browser to compare all three directions.
