Three new directions

A visual identity for discovering and collecting people-centered photobooks. Each concept is drawn as a compact, scalable mark paired with a wordmark.

Editorial M

A sculpted letterform gives the brand an expressive, publishing-led voice. The coral glint adds a small point of discovery.

EDITORIAL · EXPRESSIVE
02

Collector’s Index

Four catalog bars form a bold, compact glyph. Cobalt and citron make this the most contemporary, app-ready direction.

DIGITAL · COLLECTOR
03

Orbit & Glint

An open orbit and warm spark suggest visual discovery and inspiration, with a calmer, gallery-like tone.
